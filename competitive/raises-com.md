## Process (4 steps, "protocol")

1. **Structure** — vehicle (PPM, sub-docs, operating agreement). Reg D 506(b) & 506(c).
2. **Package** — pitch deck, financial model, investment summary, diligence room.
3. **Connect** — warm intros to family offices, HNW, institutional.
4. **Close** — wiring before exclusivity expires.
