# WattBricks — Topical Authority Content Plan

## Situation (from Domain Analysis)

| Metric | Current State |
|--------|--------------|
| Organic traffic | ~$0.10 ETV/month |
| Non-branded rankings | **Zero** — WattBricks is invisible to new buyers |
| Canada rankings | **Zero** — not indexed in CA market |
| Domain Authority | Low (new domain) |
| Content pages | **Zero** — no blog, no resource hub |
| Branded keywords indexed | 3 (positions 30–54, all brand-only) |
| Site score | D+ (38%) — SEO: F, Content Volume: F |

**Position:** Q3 — NICHE + LOW CONTENT. EcoFlow CA (161 ETV) and Bluetti (39 ETV) dominate. WattBricks must move to Q2 (broad + high content) to capture demand.

**Market timing:** "Portable power station Canada" hit peak Google Trends score 100/100 the week of March 8–14, 2026. 8,100 searches/month at KD 6. One of the most winnable high-volume energy storage keywords in Canada — completely unclaimed.

**Brand confusion:** wattbricksenergy.com is a **separate entity** selling H100/MP330 via Tractor Supply, Sam's Club, Walmart. Two domains split backlink authority. Recommended: establish clear channel separation or coordinate SEO signals.

## Competitive Moat — What Only WattBricks Can Own

These angles appear nowhere in competitor content. They are the authority anchors:

1. **"Plug and play home power"** — no competitor addresses the no-electrician-needed UPS angle
2. **"Bi-directional charging"** — WattBricks-specific technical differentiator
3. **"10ms UPS switchover"** — fastest switchover claim, no competitor matches it
4. **"wattbricks vs ecoflow"** — complete blue ocean, zero SERP competition
