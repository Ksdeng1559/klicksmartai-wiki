# LeadSniper SGI — PRD Summary

**LeadSniper SGI** = AI-enabled **domain audit and growth-intelligence application**. Evaluates a website, identifies low-competition/high-demand search opportunities, analyzes live SERPs and content gaps, measures AI-search visibility, and produces prioritized go-to-market recommendations after every audit.

**Built on:** OpenSEO + DataForSEO. Optional: Google Search Console, GA4, Serper, Exa, Parallel, Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, YouTube, reviews, government/industry data.

**Operating model:** Audit → Evidence → Opportunity → Recommendation → GTM Strategy → Execution → Measurement

## Product objective

Multi-tenant app: enter a domain → receive:
1. Technical + content audit
2. Live SERP evidence for priority topics
3. Low-competition, high-demand keyword opportunities
4. Content and competitor gaps
5. AI-search visibility and citation analysis
6. Recommended pages, tools, content, offers, campaigns
7. Prioritized GTM strategy by commercial opportunity
8. Ongoing rank/citation/lead/revenue monitoring

## Six business questions it answers

1. What prevents this domain from ranking or being cited?
2. Where is demand stronger than competition?
3. Which competitors own the opportunity?
4. Where do competitors rank with weak/incomplete/outdated/generic/poorly-localized content?
5. Which single commercially viable keyword cluster to build first?
6. What content/offer/tool/campaign produces qualified demand + measurable organic growth?

## Initial Viable Offer — Organic Gap Sprint

> "We identify the strongest commercially viable content gap your competitors have already proven, build the first optimized keyword cluster to fill that gap, connect it to a conversion offer, and measure whether organic impressions, rankings, traffic, and qualified leads improve."

Customer buys a **completed organic-growth opportunity**, not a report. Each offer produces:
- Competitor + search-gap audit
- One prioritized keyword cluster
- One optimized pillar/service/comparison/tool page
- 2–4 supporting content assets
- Internal-link architecture
- Conversion offer + CTA
- Search Console baseline
- 30/60/90-day organic growth roadmap

**Principle:** one customer → one competitor gap → one keyword cluster → one conversion offer → one measurable result.

## Target users

Business owners, growth consultants, SEO/content agencies, local service businesses, financial-services firms, mortgage & business-funding advisors, rank-and-rent operators, internal marketing/research teams.

## User journey (8 steps)

1. **Create audit project** — domain, brand, description, services, locations, segments, conversion goals, lead value, competitors, optional GSC/GA4
2. **Crawl & audit** — technical, on-page, schema, speed/CWV, mobile, content depth, CTAs, AI-crawler accessibility, rankings
3. **Opportunity universe** — seed/related keywords, PAA, autocomplete, commercial queries, local variants, competitor keywords, zero-volume, AI prompts (AIO/AI Mode, ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Perplexity)
4. **Live SERP evidence** — top URLs, page types, featured snippets, local pack, AI Overview, SERP features, authority signals, intent, format patterns, volatility
5. **Content/competitive gaps** — missing clusters/pages/local pages, weak content, missing comparisons/FAQs/tools/schema, internal-link gaps, backlink/citation gaps, AI-citation gaps
6. **Score opportunities** — transparent scoring:
   - **Organic Opportunity Score:** 25% commercial value, 20% ranking feasibility, 20% search demand, 15% authority relevance, 10% content gap, 10% conversion fit
   - **IVO Score:** 25% commercial intent, 20% competitor content weakness, 15% ranking feasibility, 15% customer-service fit, 10% search demand, 10% conversion potential, 5% production speed
   - Must NOT rely on search volume alone
7. **GTM recommendations** — SEO/AIO/citation strategy, content calendar, local SEO, lead magnets, conversion paths, competitor displacement, partnerships, social, paid, outreach targets, sequence
8. **Monitor** — rankings, visibility, AI citations, share of voice, GSC clicks, content performance, leads, revenue, regressions, new gaps

## Major modules

### 5.1 Domain Audit
Technical crawl, on-page audit, indexability, structured data, CWV, internal links, content quality, conversion path, local SEO, AI-readiness. Each issue: severity, URLs, evidence, business impact, fix, effort, priority, validation status.

### 5.2 Keyword Demand Discovery
Seed expansion, volume/trends, CPC/competition, difficulty, questions, commercial intent, local modifiers, clustering, zero-volume, conversational AI prompt generation. Must identify opportunities with meaningful demand.

## Wiring into research pipeline

- **Data sources map directly to the search stack:** DataForSEO (SERP/keywords) + Serper (Google) + Exa/Parallel (deep/AI citations) + Firecrawl (crawl)
- **Output feeds the GTM skills layer** (swan-gtm library): opportunity scoring → outreach strategy → offer creation
- **Organic Gap Sprint = the initial viable offer** for new website clients (complements [[website-onboarding-sop]])
