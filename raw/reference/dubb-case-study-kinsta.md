# Dubb Case Study — Kinsta

**Source:** dubb.com/case-studies/kinsta
**Date:** 2026 (Dubb website)
**Subject:** Kinsta — cloud platform provider (WordPress, application, database hosting)

---

## Challenge

- Kinsta had **no outbound sales team** when Rachel Steinbauer joined as Director of Sales Ops
- Industry was heavily inbound-driven — outbound had no prior success
- Cold emails and calls got minimal response
- Needed to cut through the noise and reach potential customers

---

## Solution

Rachel used Dubb to create **personalized video campaigns**:
- Walking prospects through proposals
- Giving tours of the product
- Showcasing what Kinsta offers

Key insight: Video allowed Rachel to **show her personality and passion**, making her more relatable and trustworthy than text/phone outreach.

---

## Results

| Metric | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Average response time | **Cut in half** after first week |
| Prospect engagement | Reached previously unresponsive prospects |
| Sales process | More efficient and effective |
| Lead generation | Better quality opportunities |
| Customer understanding | Prospects understood services without a phone/video call |
| Overall | Predictable and efficient growth |

---

## Key Quote

> *"Dubb changed everything for us at Kinsta by providing outbound sales tools. Using video helped us increase engagement rates, responses, and closing rates. Dubb also made it easier for people to understand our services without ever needing to hop on a phone or video call."*
> — Rachel Steinbauer, Director of Sales Operations, Kinsta

---

## Observations

1. **Video differentiates in inbound-heavy industries** — where everyone else sends text/emails, video cuts through
2. **Personality + authenticity drive trust** — Rachel's personal touch on video was key differentiator
3. **No phone call needed** — video replaced live calls for discovery and proposal walks
4. **Tracking = prioritization** — knowing who watched videos helped focus follow-up
5. **Half response time** — immediate measurable impact in week one
