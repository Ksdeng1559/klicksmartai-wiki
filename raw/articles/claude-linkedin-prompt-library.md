## Outreach Frameworks

1. **Pain-Led** — Surface a specific pain point, validate it, offer a solution
2. **Value-Led** — Lead with a free resource, follow up with more value, soft CTA
3. **Authority-Led** — Open with a statistic, bridge to your expertise, make a specific offer

**Natural Connector rule:** First message contains no pitch and no questions — just a genuine observation.

## ICP Scoring (1-5 scale)
- 5 = Perfect fit
- 4 = Strong fit
- 3 = Possible fit
- 2 = Weak fit
- 1 = Not a fit

## DISC Behavioral Profiles

- I = Influential (persuasive, social)
- S = Steady (supportive, patient)
- C = Conscientious (analytical, systematic)
