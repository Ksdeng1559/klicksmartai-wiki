## Operating-System Mandate
**Goal:** Own every commercially valuable mortgage search and borrower decision in British Columbia.
This workspace is no longer treated as a one-time keyword-research document. It is the control centre for a living **Mortgage Search Intelligence Database** that connects:
`Keyword → Cluster → Borrower Situation → Page → CTA → Lead → Application → Funded Outcome`
### Strategic Scope
- Build an estimated 2,000–5,000-keyword intelligence layer before final page allocation.
- Organize content around borrower situations and financing decisions, not isolated keyword variants.
- Create a page only when SERP evidence, underwriting differences, local context, conversion intent, or business value justifies a distinct asset.
- Target approximately **230–360 high-quality pages** as an initial ceiling: 40–60 primary landing pages, 120–180 supporting resources, 20–40 justified city pages, and 50–80 FAQ resources.
- Scale beyond that range only where live DataForSEO and SERP-overlap evidence proves distinct intent.
### Core Commercial Pillars
1. **Private and Alternative Mortgages** — private mortgage, B lender, second mortgage, bridge and exit strategies.
2. **Financial Recovery Mortgages** — bank decline, CRA debt, tax arrears, consumer proposal, bankruptcy, collections, missed payments and foreclosure prevention.
3. **Self-Employed Mortgages** — incorporated owners, sole proprietors, contractors, commission income, seasonal income, gig workers, bank statements and addbacks.
4. **Equity and Refinancing** — debt consolidation, CRA repayment, business expansion, renovation, investment funding and renewal alternatives.
5. **Commercial Financing** — owner-occupied, retail, office, warehouse, mixed-use, apartment, acquisition and refinance.
6. **Construction and Development** — owner-builder, draw mortgages, land, infill, multiplex, SSMUH, renovation and conversion.
### Page-Creation Gate
A separate page is approved only when one or more conditions are met:
- SERP results demonstrate materially distinct intent.
- The borrower requires a different financing solution or underwriting path.
- Qualification criteria or evidence requirements differ.
- A city or property market has meaningful local context and search demand.
- The conversion action is materially different.
- The page has measurable commercial value and low cannibalization risk.
### Standard Commercial Page Blueprint
1. Hero and borrower promise
2. Qualification criteria
3. Borrower situations
4. Rates and costs with appropriate disclosures
5. Process and expected timeline
6. Frequently asked questions
7. Comparison table
8. Local market context where relevant
9. Trust and evidence indicators
10. Calculator, assessment or interactive tool
11. Strong application or consultation CTA
### Trust and Evidence Layer
Every major commercial page must explain what lenders evaluate, what evidence is required, common reasons for decline, realistic timelines, risks and costs, the intended exit strategy, and when the solution may not be suitable.
### Publishing Control
- **Phase 1:** Five cornerstone pages and foundational tracking.
- **Phase 2:** Eight to twelve pages per month while validating indexation, ranking movement and conversion.
- **Phase 3:** Fifteen to twenty-five pages per month only after validated traction.
Publishing volume is an output of proven performance, not a target by itself.

## Phase 7 — Story Over Score Content Production
Every alternative-lending asset must begin with borrower discovery rather than document collection.
### Required Narrative Structure
The content brief must answer:
1. **Equity and asset story** — available equity, down payment, source of funds and property position
2. **Income story** — how the borrower actually earns money, including business cash flow, secondary income and normalized income
3. **Expense story** — true operating costs, seasonality and sustainability
4. **Credit story** — the Cause, impact, Cure and evidence that the event is unlikely to repeat
5. **Goal and exit strategy** — bridge purpose, debt reset, tax strategy, sale, renewal or return to prime
6. **Evidence path** — which documents validate the established story
7. **Lender-fit rationale** — why the proposed solution makes sense and when it may not be suitable
### Bank Statement Test
For self-employed and non-traditional income topics, the page or assessment must address:
> If the borrower’s bank statements were reviewed today, would they support the financial story being presented? If not, what explains the difference?
### Cause and Cure Standard
Credit-related content must distinguish:
- What happened
- When it began
- How long it lasted
- What changed
- What evidence supports the resolution
- Why the issue should not recur
### Alt-to-Prime Roadmap
Where appropriate, present alternative financing as a bridge with measurable milestones:
- Months 1–6: cash-flow stabilization and resolution of urgent debts or arrears
- Months 7–12: clean repayment behaviour and utilization management
- Months 13–24: proven stability, income alignment and preparation for prime underwriting
Do not imply that every alternative borrower will or should return to prime. Separate temporary-challenge borrowers, tax-planning borrowers and long-term alternative borrowers.
