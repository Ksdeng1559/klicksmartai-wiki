## 1. Purpose
This addendum establishes the evidence requirements for converting the existing keyword plan into an approved website architecture and publishing roadmap.
The current keyword clusters represent strategically informed hypotheses. They must not be treated as final merge, split, or page-creation decisions until verified through consistent live SERP comparisons.
> Keyword similarity does not determine page architecture. Shared search intent, shared ranking URLs, ranking-page format, borrower need, and expected conversion action determine page architecture.
This addendum gives priority to three connected areas:
1. Alternative and B-lender mortgages
2. Construction-related financing
3. Self-employed mortgage qualification where tax deductions reduce reported income
## 2. Strategic positioning
The website should position Dennis Eng as a specialist for borrowers and projects that do not fit conventional bank guidelines.
### Core positioning statement
**Dennis helps profitable self-employed borrowers, property owners, builders, and investors obtain financing when traditional income documentation, property type, construction requirements, or timing makes bank approval difficult.**
This positioning connects:
- B-lender mortgages
- self-employed financing
- business-income qualification
- construction mortgages
- progress-draw financing
- owner-builder financing
- laneway and coach-house financing
- private mortgages
- bridge loans
- second mortgages
- refinancing and exit strategies back to an A lender
The website must not suggest that B lending itself produces a tax benefit. The correct message is that alternative lending may help a borrower qualify when legitimate business deductions have reduced the taxable income shown on personal tax documents.

Exact ranking URLs, not merely shared domains, must be used for the primary overlap calculation.

Domain overlap may be recorded separately but must not replace exact URL overlap.

## 7. Merge and split rules
### 7.1 Merge into one page when
- at least three top-10 URLs are shared;
- the dominant intent is materially the same;
- the same page format ranks;
- the same borrower problem is being solved;
- one CTA can serve both searches;
- one page can answer both queries without becoming unfocused;
- local and national scope are compatible.
### 7.2 Split into separate pages when
At least two of the following differ materially:
- national versus local intent;
- informational versus transactional intent;
- product type;
- qualification scenario;
- property or project type;
- immediate financing need versus long-term research;
- guide versus calculator;
- comparison versus application;
- dominant ranking-page format;
- expected CTA;
- required supporting evidence.
### 7.3 Do not create a standalone page when
- the SERP is effectively identical to an approved page;
- the topic lacks a distinct borrower problem;
- the page would only change a location or occupation name;
- the query is mainly about taxation rather than mortgage qualification;
- no unique expertise or local evidence can be added;
- the topic is outside the business’s service scope;
- the page would require unsupported or frequently outdated claims.

## 13. Internal-linking model
Internal links should reflect the borrower’s actual decision journey.
### Self-employed borrower
`Low reported taxable income → How tax write-offs affect qualification → Self-employed mortgage → B-lender options → Required documentation → Mortgage assessment`
### Construction borrower
`Planning a build → Construction financing → Progress draws → Owner-builder or self-employed qualification → B-lender or private construction financing → Completion refinance`
### Bank-declined borrower
`Bank decline → What is a B lender? → B lender vs private lender → Scenario-specific qualification → Costs and exit strategy → Consultation`
Each pillar should link to:
- one problem-aware page;
- one qualification guide;
- one comparison page;
- one tool or checklist;
- one relevant local or project page;
- the main assessment CTA.

## 18. Final strategic principle
> The content architecture must reflect how Google groups borrower problems, not how a keyword list groups similar phrases.
The highest-value opportunity is not to claim that B lending provides a tax advantage. It is to explain how alternative lending can help qualified self-employed borrowers whose legitimate tax deductions cause conventional underwriting documents to understate the financial strength of their business.
