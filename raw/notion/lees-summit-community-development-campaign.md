## Campaign objective
Identify property owners, commercial brokers, real-estate groups, and project sponsors in the Lee’s Summit and Kansas City market that may need an outsourced owner-side development team, feasibility support, entitlement coordination, project controls, or construction oversight.
> **Qualification caution:** Public evidence can confirm land listings, development applications, brokerage activity, or ownership signals, but it cannot reliably prove that an organization has no internal development team. Each account below must be verified during research and discovery.
## First request — Prime Lee’s Summit Development Ground
### Account
**Organization:** Block & Company, Inc., Realtors  
**Property:** Prime Lee’s Summit Development Ground  
**Location:** NEC of Stuart Road and Missouri 291 Highway, Lee’s Summit, Missouri 64082  
**Public listing:** 19.05 acres offered for sale at US\$3,500,000; utilities are reported at the site and the property has visibility from Highway 291.  
**Listing contacts:** Bob Jaekel and Garrett Cohoon  
**Campaign classification:** Broker/referral partner first; prospective owner-side consulting collaborator second.
### Opportunity hypothesis
The conceptual residential plan shows approximately 58 detached homes, community open space, a detention pond, clubhouse, pool, gated access, parking, and six sample house plans. Converting this concept into an investment-ready opportunity may require:
- Zoning and entitlement confirmation
- Preliminary civil and utility review
- Updated yield and site-layout validation
- Phasing, infrastructure, and horizontal-development budget
- Builder and consultant coordination
- Development schedule and risk register
- Investor, lender, or buyer presentation package
Block & Company already provides development and construction-management services. Therefore, outreach should not imply that it lacks capability. The collaboration angle is **supplemental owner-side capacity**, independent feasibility, residential community-development execution, or support for a buyer introduced by the brokerage team.
### Account score
- Project activity: 5/5
- Estimated project value: 4/5
- Need for external expertise: 2/5 pending verification
- Access to decision-maker: 5/5
- Urgency signal: 4/5
- **Initial score: 20/25**

## Prioritization recommendation
Start with accounts **1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 11, 12, 13, 16 and 18**. This creates a balanced first cohort:
- Five owners or active project sponsors
- Five brokers/referral partners
- A mix of immediate project signals and repeatable referral channels
Do not begin by sending the same email to all 20. Prepare a one-page research brief for each account and use a single evidence-based trigger in the opening sentence.
## Research fields required before outreach
For each account, verify:
1. Exact ownership or applicant relationship
2. Current project stage and latest municipal action
3. Decision-maker name and role
4. Internal development capacity
5. Existing architect, engineer, developer, contractor and lender
6. Likely execution gap
7. Best Elite entry offer
8. Relevant David Poole credential or case example
9. Business email from an official or reputable source
10. Consent, suppression-list and sending-domain requirements
