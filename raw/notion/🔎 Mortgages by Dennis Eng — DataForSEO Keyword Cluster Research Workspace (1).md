## Purpose
This workspace is the operating document for completing the DataForSEO keyword research, SERP clustering, competitor analysis, local SEO mapping, and content architecture for [**mortgagesbydenniseng.ca**](http://mortgagesbydenniseng.ca).

## Research Rules
1. Use current DataForSEO data wherever available.
2. Do not invent search volume, CPC, competition, keyword difficulty, trend, or SERP metrics.
3. Label each metric by geography: city, British Columbia, or Canada.
4. Preserve raw DataForSEO values separately from strategic interpretation.
5. Cluster keywords using SERP overlap and search intent, not semantic similarity alone.
6. Do not create thin city doorway pages.
7. Do not omit low-volume keywords when borrower intent and commercial value are high.
8. Every recommended page must have a clear business objective and CTA.

### Phase 2 — SERP Analysis and Clustering
For priority keywords, retrieve Google Canada SERPs and compare the top 10 organic URLs.
Group keywords when:
- At least three top-10 URLs overlap, or
- Search intent and ranking-page formats strongly overlap
Separate keywords when:
- SERP intent differs
- Local results dominate one term but not another
- One query returns service pages while another returns guides
- Private-lender and B-lender intent differ
- Residential and commercial intent differ
- The CTA or borrower journey materially differs
**Phase 2 completion criteria:**
- Primary keyword selected for each cluster
- Secondary keywords assigned
- Shared ranking URLs recorded
- Overlap rationale documented
- Recommended page type assigned
- Create, merge, or avoid decision recorded
