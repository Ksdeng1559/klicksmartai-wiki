# Purpose
This SOP defines the standard operating system for researching, building, launching, optimizing, and scaling local-service websites and lead-generation assets using AI, search intelligence, Google Business Profile optimization, technical SEO, reputation systems, content operations, CRM workflows, and revenue attribution.
The system is designed for KlicksmartAI, LeadSniperAI, Search Engine Growth Intelligence, local-service client websites, and owned lead-generation assets.
> **Core operating principle:** Build measurable growth assets, not isolated websites.
# Strategic Outcome
The system converts market signals into qualified business opportunities through the following sequence:
**Signals → Opportunity Scoring → Business Validation → Local Entity Launch → Website Build → Search Visibility → Reputation → Lead Capture → Qualification → Routing → Revenue Attribution → Continuous Improvement**
# Scope
This SOP covers:
- Client websites and website replacements
- Owned lead-generation websites
- Local service landing pages
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Local SEO and technical SEO
- Review and reputation workflows
- AI-assisted content research and production
- Service and location page expansion
- PR and local authority building
- Lead capture, qualification, and routing
- Performance reporting and optimization
# Operating Principles
1. **Business truth before optimization.** All names, locations, claims, credentials, availability, service areas, reviews, and customer results must be accurate and verifiable.
2. **Demand before production.** Validate commercial demand and provider capacity before registering domains or creating content.
3. **Evidence before publication.** AI may research, organize, draft, and test, but unsupported claims must never be published.
4. **Intent before word count.** Pages should be as long as required to satisfy the user and conversion objective, not an arbitrary word target.
5. **Relevance before scale.** Build deep authority in a defined service, audience, or location before expanding.
6. **Conversion before vanity metrics.** Calls, qualified leads, appointments, funded deals, and revenue matter more than keyword counts or SEO scores.
7. **Automation with approval gates.** Discovery, drafting, QA, and monitoring can be automated; high-risk claims and final publishing require review.
8. **White-hat, entity-first execution.** Do not manufacture reviews, clicks, calls, directions, identities, locations, citations, or authority.

# Phase 0 — Opportunity Discovery and Commercial Validation

## Research Tasks
1. Collect keyword and SERP data using DataForSEO or equivalent.
2. Identify commercial and transactional search intent.
3. Review Google Maps and organic competitors.
4. Analyze competitor services, reviews, positioning, offers, and conversion paths.

# Phase 10 — Review and Voice-of-Customer System
## Review Request Workflow
1. Customer transaction or service is completed.
2. Satisfaction and eligibility are checked without review gating.
3. A neutral review request is sent through SMS, email, QR, or NFC.
4. The customer may provide public or private feedback.
5. New reviews are classified by service, location, sentiment, and issue.
6. AI drafts a response.
7. Sensitive or negative responses are reviewed by a human.
8. Repeated complaints generate an operational improvement task.
9. Customer language informs content and offers.
## Review Rules
- Never buy or fabricate reviews.
- Never reward customers for positive reviews.
- Never suppress requests to unhappy customers.
- Staff incentives should reward process compliance, not star ratings.
- Review velocity should reflect normal customer activity.
- Testimonials require permission and accurate attribution.

# Reference Framework — Dominate Local Search
## Purpose
This section incorporates the operating principles from *Dominate Local Search* into the KlicksmartAI Local Growth & SEO Operating System. The framework reinforces a coordinated model in which keyword intelligence, authority content, link acquisition, local citations, technical SEO, conversion optimization, and performance measurement operate as one system rather than as disconnected tactics.
## Core Local SEO Model
Execute the following pillars together:
1. **Search Demand and Keyword Intelligence**
	- Identify service-plus-location keywords.
	- Identify problem-plus-solution keywords that capture customers before they select a provider.
	- Expand research across cities, municipalities, neighbourhoods, counties, and service areas.
	- Prioritize near-me, emergency, cost, pricing, comparison, review, and provider-selection searches.
	- Validate opportunities using commercial intent, attainable competition, relevance, lead value, and conversion potential—not search volume alone.
2. **Local Authority Content**
	- Build content around customer questions, local conditions, regulations, costs, timelines, risks, and decision criteria.
	- Use a balanced content portfolio:
		- Educational content: approximately 40%
		- Local market content: approximately 25%
		- Problem-solving content: approximately 20%
		- Trust-building content: approximately 10%
		- Direct commercial content: approximately 5%
	- Treat these percentages as planning guidance, not rigid publishing quotas.
	- Create useful local resources such as cost guides, buyer guides, checklists, calculators, regulatory explainers, market reports, directories, and case studies.
3. **Authority and Link Development**
	- Prioritize legitimate links from local media, chambers, government organizations, educational institutions, industry associations, suppliers, complementary businesses, community organizations, and trusted directories.
	- Build links through relationships, expertise, community participation, original resources, local data, PR, partnerships, and useful tools.
	- Do not buy bulk links, use private blog networks, automate spam outreach, or participate in manipulative link schemes.
4. **Local Citation and Entity Consistency**
	- Maintain an identical and verified business name, address or service-area status, phone number, website, hours, and category information across major platforms.
	- Prioritize Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, relevant industry directories, chambers, and credible local directories.
	- Audit duplicate, outdated, and inconsistent listings as part of ongoing maintenance.
5. **Technical and Conversion Foundation**
	- Ensure every site supports search engines, prospective customers, and existing customers.
	- Maintain clear service pages, location pages, about and credential pages, contact paths, FAQs, evidence, and required disclosures.
	- Implement fast mobile performance, valid structured data, clear page hierarchy, internal linking, accessible forms, click-to-call actions, analytics, call tracking, and CRM attribution.
	- Every commercial page must have one dominant conversion objective and measurable completion event.
## Keyword Opportunity Matrix
Score each target query or compact keyword cluster using:
- Commercial intent
- Search and customer demand
- Estimated lead value
- Competitive attainability
- Direct service relevance
- Geographic relevance
- Content differentiation potential
- Provider capacity
- Conversion potential
- Compliance risk
### Priority Bands
**Priority 1 — Build First**
- High commercial or urgent intent
- Strong service relevance
- Attainable local competition
- Clear monetization path
- Sufficient provider capacity
**Priority 2 — Authority Expansion**
- Problem-aware and educational demand
- Cost, process, comparison, and qualification searches
- Local information gaps
- Topics that support internal linking and assisted conversions
**Priority 3 — Long-Term Authority**
- Broad or highly competitive categories
- Thought-leadership opportunities
- Resource hubs and comprehensive market reports
- Expansion into secondary services or locations
## Standard Local Content Portfolio
For each viable service-location market, evaluate the need for:
- Core service page
- Service-plus-location page
- Emergency or urgent-service page
- Cost and pricing guide
- Problem and symptom guide
- Comparison or alternatives page
- How-to-choose-a-provider guide

## 90-Day Local Growth Implementation Sequence

- Establish traffic, ranking, lead, call, and revenue baselines.
- Complete keyword, competitor, customer-language, and technical research.
- Map keyword clusters to existing and proposed pages.
- Confirm business truth, provider capacity, conversion goals, and attribution requirements.

- Create flagship local resources, calculators, buyer guides, directories, or market reports.

- Create the next 90-day roadmap based on observed performance.
## Measurement Framework
Track performance at four levels:
1. **Technical Health** — crawlability, indexing, speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile UX, structured data, and errors.
2. **Search Visibility** — impressions, rankings, local-pack visibility, click-through rate, and share of search.
3. **Traffic and Engagement** — organic sessions, landing-page engagement, calls, form activity, and assisted journeys.
4. **Business Outcomes** — qualified leads, appointments, opportunities, close rate, revenue, customer lifetime value, provider response time, and revenue by page, keyword cluster, and location.
## Operating Rule
Local SEO is considered successful only when improved visibility produces measurable qualified opportunities and revenue. Rankings, traffic, citations, backlinks, reviews, and content volume are leading indicators—not the final outcome.
