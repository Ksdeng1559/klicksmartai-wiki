## Course Overview

**Title:** AI Chief of Staff for Financial Advisors: Claude Code + Co-work Edition

**Tagline:** Run your entire advisory practice — client research, meeting prep, compliance docs, outreach — from one AI-powered workflow.

**Target Student:**
- Financial advisors / wealth managers (Canadian context, ICP: HNWIs $25M+)
- CFPs, CFAs, and portfolio managers
- Estate and succession planners
- Accountants and CPAs serving business owners

**Prerequisites:**
- Claude Pro or Max ($20/month)
- Mac, Windows, or Linux
- No coding experience required
- Curiosity and willingness to work inside AI tools

**Course Duration:** ~8–10 hours total
**Price:** $197 (launch special: $97 for first 30 students)
