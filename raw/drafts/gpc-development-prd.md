## Dubb Video Integration — Proposed

**Recommendation:** Embed Dubb Action Pages on the GPC website to replace standard contact forms and PDF downloads.

**Use cases:**
| Page | Dubb Feature | Purpose |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Contact page | Dubb Action Page + video intro | Personalized first touch — team member recorded welcome video |
| Project portfolio | Video walkthroughs | Show rather than tell — site tours, completed projects |
| Investor inquiries | Personalized video response | Higher engagement than form + PDF |
| About / Our Approach | Team video bios | Build trust before first contact |

**Kinsta proof point:** Response time cut in half in week one. 30-300% conversion lift on video testimonials.

**Implementation:** Dubb Chrome Extension for recording. Dubb Action Page templates for embedding. CRM for lead tracking.
