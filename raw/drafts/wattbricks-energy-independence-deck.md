## Slide 2 — Canada's Energy Paradox
- Canada is an energy superpower facing an impending electricity shortage
- As demand accelerates, investment in energy scalability has collapsed
- Electricity demand now growing at **12x its past pace** (RSM Canada)
- Canada recently became a **net importer** of electricity from the U.S.
- Investment fell from **$84.0B (2014) → $35.7B (2024)** — 57.5% drop
- Electricity Demand Index: 100 → 220+ (2014–2024)

## Slide 3 — Friction Matrix: Policy & Market Results
**Regulatory friction has fundamentally broken the scalability of Canadian energy infrastructure.**
Discretionary policy and red tape have choked the "All-of-the-Above" energy strategy.

| Policy Hurdle | Unintended Consequence | Market Result |
|---|---|---|
| Bill C-69 (Impact Assessment) — subjective evaluation criteria | Approvals slowed to 1 project approved in 5 years vs. 17 previously | Capital flight and extreme investor uncertainty |
| Bill C-48 & Bill C-5 — tanker bans and arbitrary cabinet fast-tracking | Invites political favoritism, limits export routing | Severe price discounts and reliance on US monopsony |
| Clean Electricity Regs (CER) — mandates grid sustainability over reliability | Grid expansion cannot keep pace with electrification mandates | Artificial scarcity and surging ratepayer costs |
