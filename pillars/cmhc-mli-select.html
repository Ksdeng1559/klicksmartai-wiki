CMHC MLI Select — BC Mortgage Broker Guide 2026

What is CMHC MLI Select?

CMHC MLI Select is a mortgage loan insurance program designed specifically for purpose-built rental housing in Canada. It offers the most favorable financing terms available for multi-unit residential projects — up to 95% loan-to-value (LTV) and 50-year amortization — provided the project meets social outcomes in affordability, energy efficiency, or accessibility.

Unlike conventional CMHC insurance (which caps at 80–85% LTV and 25–40 year amortization), MLI Select rewards developers who commit to measurable public benefits. The more points you score, the better your loan terms.

The MLI Select Points System

Projects earn points across three categories. Your total score determines your loan terms:

CategoryMax PointsHow to Earn
Affordability 100 Rent at or below 30% of median household income. More units at deeper affordability = more points. Includes rent-geared-to-income (RGI) units.
Energy Efficiency 100 Meet or exceed energy performance standards (e.g., 15–40% better than NECB 2017). Higher efficiency = more points. Includes passive house, net-zero, and LEED certifications.
Accessibility 50 Universal design features, visitable units, barrier-free access. More accessible units = more points. Includes adaptable suites and common areas.

Loan Terms by Score Tier

Score TierTotal PointsMax LTVMax AmortizationDebt Coverage
Standard 0–49 Up to 85% Up to 40 years 1.20x
Enhanced 50–99 Up to 90% Up to 45 years 1.15x
Premium 100+ Up to 95% Up to 50 years 1.10x

Note: Premium tier (100+ points) unlocks the full 95% LTV and 50-year amortization. Most competitive projects target this tier.

Who Should Use CMHC MLI Select?

  • Purpose-built rental developers — new construction of 5+ unit apartment buildings
  • Non-profit and co-op housing providers — affordable and social housing projects
  • Existing rental owners — refinancing to unlock equity for renovations or acquisitions
  • Mixed-use developers — projects with significant residential components
  • Student housing — near post-secondary institutions
  • Senior housing — independent living and assisted living facilities
  • BC developers — particularly in high-cost markets where 95% LTV makes projects viable
