Laneway House Financing Vancouver — 2026 Guide

🏡 The Untapped Vancouver Opportunity

Vancouver has over 65,000 single-family lots eligible for laneway houses. Yet fewer than 5,000 have been built. The City has streamlined permits, BC has mandated gentle density, and rental demand is insatiable. The bottleneck isn't zoning — it's homeowners not knowing how to finance construction. This page solves that.

What is a Laneway House?

A laneway house (also called a coach house or garden suite) is a secondary dwelling built in the rear yard of a single-family property, typically facing the back lane. In Vancouver, laneway houses can be up to 900–1,000 sqft (depending on lot size), with 1–2 bedrooms, a full kitchen, and separate entrance. They can be rented out for $2,000–$3,500/month in Vancouver — generating significant income while increasing property value.

3 Ways to Finance Your Laneway House

🏗️

Refinance + Construction Draw

Best for: Homeowners with equity

  • Refinance existing mortgage to access equity
  • Funds released in stages (draws) as construction progresses
  • Up to 80% LTV on completed value
  • Interest-only during construction phase
  • Roll into single mortgage at completion

Most common route. Dennis manages the draw schedule with your builder.

💳

HELOC (Home Equity Line of Credit)

Best for: Smaller projects, flexible timeline

  • Access up to 65% LTV as revolving credit
  • Pay interest only on what you use
  • No fixed draw schedule — use as needed
  • Variable rate (prime + 0.5%–1%)
  • Can combine with existing mortgage

Simplest option. Less paperwork, more flexibility.

🔨

Purchase + Renovation

Best for: Buying a property to add a laneway

  • Purchase price + construction cost in one mortgage
  • Based on "as-complete" appraised value
  • Up to 80% LTV on completed value
  • Construction holdback released on completion
  • CMHC insured options available

Ideal for investors buying with laneway potential.
