What is a Reverse Mortgage?

A reverse mortgage allows Canadian homeowners aged 55+ to access up to 55% of their home's value as tax-free cash — without selling, moving, or making monthly mortgage payments. The loan is repaid when you sell the home, move out, or pass away. You retain full ownership and title.

In Canada, reverse mortgages are offered by two providers: HomeEquity Bank (CHIP) and Equitable Bank. Both are federally regulated and insured.

Pros and Cons of a Reverse Mortgage

✅ Advantages
  • No monthly payments — interest accrues but nothing is due while you live in the home
  • Tax-free cash — proceeds are not taxable income
  • You keep ownership — title remains in your name
  • No impact on OAS/GIS — doesn't count as income for government benefits
  • Flexible payout — lump sum, monthly payments, or a combination
  • Home price protection — you'll never owe more than the home's fair market value
⚠️ Considerations
  • Higher interest rates — typically 2–3% above conventional mortgage rates
  • Interest compounds — the balance grows over time, reducing equity for heirs
  • Setup costs — appraisal, legal, and closing fees ($1,500–$2,500)
  • Reduced inheritance — the loan must be repaid from the estate
  • Prepayment limits — limited ability to pay down early without penalty
  • Not for short-term — best suited for those planning to stay 5+ years
