# SBIR Readiness Scoring Rubric

## Scoring Overview
GrantFundingAI uses a weighted score to estimate whether a company appears ready for SBIR/STTR or related funding pathways.

This score is not a legal eligibility determination. It is an evidence-based readiness score based on website content, interview answers, and supporting documents.

## Score Categories

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Eligibility | 20 |
| Problem significance | 15 |
| Innovation | 15 |
| Commercialization | 15 |
| Government fit | 15 |
| Community fit | 10 |
| Partnerships | 10 |

Total: 100

## Eligibility — 20 Points

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| U.S.-based entity appears confirmed | 5 |
| For-profit business appears confirmed | 5 |
| Small business indicators present | 3 |
| R&D / technical activity present | 4 |
| Commercial intent present | 3 |

## Problem Significance — 15 Points

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Clear problem statement | 3 |
| Problem affects large market/community | 3 |
| Problem is measurable | 3 |
| Public-sector relevance | 3 |
| Urgency / why-now signal | 3 |

## Innovation — 15 Points

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Proprietary technology | 3 |
| Novel process or method | 3 |
| Prototype or pilot evidence | 3 |
| Patent/IP or defensibility signal | 3 |
| Technical/scientific explanation | 3 |

## Commercialization — 15 Points

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Customer segment identified | 3 |
| Revenue model visible | 3 |
| Market opportunity described | 3 |
| Case studies/testimonials/pilots | 3 |
| Scaling path evident | 3 |

## Government Fit — 15 Points

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| DOE/EPA/USDA/NSF/etc. agency signal | 4 |
| Public infrastructure relevance | 3 |
| Municipal/county/tribal use case | 3 |
| Mission alignment with agency problem areas | 3 |
| Procurement or deployment pathway visible | 2 |

## Community Fit — 10 Points

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Rural community relevance | 2 |
| Tribal community relevance | 2 |
| Environmental improvement | 2 |
| Workforce/job creation | 2 |
| Housing/infrastructure benefit | 2 |

## Partnerships — 10 Points

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| University/research partner | 2 |
| Industry partner | 2 |
| Government/public partner | 2 |
| Pilot/customer partner | 2 |
| Strategic advisor/technical team | 2 |

## Decision Thresholds

| Score | Decision |
|---:|---|
| 85–100 | High Priority |
| 70–84 | Strong Candidate |
| 40–69 | Needs Development |
| 0–39 | Not SBIR Ready |

## Evidence Logic
Use three-state logic:

- YES: evidence found
- NO: evidence contradicted or absent after review
- UNKNOWN: evidence not visible from website

UNKNOWN should trigger a follow-up interview question.
