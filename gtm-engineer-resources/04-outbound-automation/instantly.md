## At a Glance

**"Email outreach platform with unlimited email account warmup — scale outbound campaigns without warming delays."**

Instantly.ai is a cold email outreach platform focused on email warming and campaign management. Key differentiator: unlimited email accounts connected, unlimited warmup, and campaign analytics in one tool. Used by SDR teams and agencies for high-volume outbound.

## GTM Engineering Fit

### Pipeline Position
- **Email delivery infrastructure:** Handles warmup + sending for outbound campaigns
- **vs. Klick2Client OS:** Instantly handles the delivery infrastructure; Klick2Client OS handles the full lifecycle (battlecards, sequencing logic, LinkedIn, CRM)
- **Best for:** Teams that need pure email warmup + sending without full GTM OS complexity

### Spectra Holdings Use Case
- Warm up faith-framed sender accounts for CDFI outreach
- Run parallel campaigns to county-level contacts (Whatcom, Bexar)
- Sync results to Klick2Client OS for battlecard tracking

### Stack Positioning

| Layer | Tool | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| Signal detection | Explorium / Apify | Find the opportunity |
| Contact data | EnrichLayer / Apollo | Get verified contacts |
| Email warmup + sending | Instantly.ai | Delivery infrastructure |
| Full GTM OS | Klick2Client OS | Lifecycle + battlecards + LinkedIn |
