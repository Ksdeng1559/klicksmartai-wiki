# KCCLT PARTNERSHIP MEMO — MCF ALIGNMENT

> *"Your land can become a permanent home for a family — and a permanent asset for the community."*

---

## 1. Why KCCLT

The Kansas City Community Land Trust is the CLT-equivalent of Kulshan CLT in the Veritas pipeline — an operational, mission-driven land trust with a dual-ownership model that removes land from the speculative market and recycles subsidy across generations of low-income owners. Critically, KCCLT already operates in the **east-side vacancy corridor** where the 18,000-unit off-market stock concentrates — the exact geography of the MCF thesis.

**KCCLT model strengths:**
- Dual ownership: sells the house, retains the land, ground-lease to homeowner
- Subsidy recycling: public subsidy never enters market or investor pockets — permanent affordability
- Equity sharing: homeowner enjoys equity on resale; price growth capped against gentrification
- Purpose-built for the systemically excluded — east-side residents displaced from economic opportunity

**The gap:** KCCLT is a developer/owner — it lacks the patient construction capital layer that turns grant commitments into rehabilitated, owner-occupied homes. That is the MCF's role.

## 2. Partnership Structure

| Layer | Partner | Role |
|-------|---------|------|
| Land acquisition | KCCLT + KC land bank | Acquire vacant/off-market parcels at basis ($30K–$80K/parcel) |
| Capital | Veritas Developments MCF | Patient equity + construction bridge; Pay-as-We-Sell repayment |
| Debt | Central Bank of KC / UMB | Construction/permanent debt at market or CDFI rates |
| Subsidy | KC Housing Trust Fund, MHDC, FHLB AHP, Regional Housing Fund | Gap capital + grant layer |
| Rehab | Local contractor (faith-CDC co-developer: PEDC, United Believers, Nash Group) | Fixed-price rehab delivery |
| Stewardship | KCCLT | Ground lease, buyer qualification, resale restriction enforcement |

**Deal architecture (per unit):**

```
Acquisition basis (avg, east side):        $60,000
Rehab cost (fixed-price, avg):             $75,000
Total cost per unit:                       $135,000
KCCLT ground lease value:                  $30,000 (land retained)
MCF equity per unit:                       $45,000 (33%)
CDFI debt per unit:                        $35,000 (26%)
Public subsidy per unit (HTF/AHP/LIHTC):   $25,000 (19%)
Buyer down payment/equity:                 $10,000 (7%)
Developer fee + soft costs:                $20,000 (15%)
```

**Repayment:** Pay-as-We-Sell — MCF recoups as units close; no accrued interest drag during absorption.

## 4. Structuring Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| KCCLT capacity (small staff, project pipeline) | Medium | High | Project-specific MOU; co-developer (PEDC/Nash) carries execution |
| Rehab overrun on 100-yr-old stock | Medium-High | Medium | Fixed-price contracts; 15% contingency; unit ceiling 85% of neighborhood median |
| Buyer qualification volume | Medium | Medium | KCCLT buyer pipeline + Habitat/United Believers referral network |
| Public subsidy timing (HTF, LIHTC cycle) | Medium | Medium | Braided stack; MCF bridge covers timing gap |
| MO CLT legal framework | Low-Medium | Medium | Verify OK/MO law — MO has no CLT-specific statute; KCCLT operates via 501c3 + ground lease (existing precedent) |
