# EAST-SIDE VACANCY CONVERSION — PILOT PRO-FORMA

> **Scope:** Prepared for Veritas Developments (David Poole) — **Prime Lee's Summit** (Hwy 291, Lee's Summit) & **Stonehaven Estates** (Cyclone School Rd, Lee's Summit), Jackson County, MO.

> *"I was a stranger and you did not welcome me."* — Matthew 25:35–46

**Thesis:** Acquire 25 off-market vacant single-family/townhome units on the KC east side at record-low basis, rehabilitate at fixed price, place in KCCLT permanent-affordable ownership for workforce households (50–80% AMI). First pilot tranche of the 18,000-unit vacancy conversion pipeline.

## 5. Unit Economics (per sold unit)

```
Resale price (CLT-restricted):      $150,000
Less: acquisition+rehab allocated:  $130,000
Gross margin:                       $20,000 (13.3%)
Less: MCF recoup + debt service:    $85,000
Net to KCCLT/community reserve:     $65,000 (recycled to next project)
```

The CLT model deliberately caps appreciation — the margin is the *permanent affordability asset*, not profit. This is the difference from market-rate pro-formas: the "return" is community equity + capital recycling, which is the faith-aligned, CDFI-compatible structure Veritas deploys.

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |

## 9. Why This Pilot Wins

1. **Lowest per-unit capital in the pipeline** — $148K all-in vs. Whatcom's $500K+ gap-bridging
2. **Demand documented** — closed HAKC waitlists, rising PIT count, shrinking affordable rental tier
3. **Partner stack complete** — KCCLT (ownership), Central Bank/AltCap (debt), HTF/AHP/LIHTC (subsidy), United Believers/PEDC (faith execution)
4. **Counter-cyclical timing** — buyer's market at exactly the acquisition tier; development wave (ballpark, riverfront) lifting the whole metro
5. **Permanent affordability** — CLT structure means every dollar deployed stays in the community
