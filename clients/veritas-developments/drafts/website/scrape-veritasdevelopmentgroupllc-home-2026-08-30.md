Founder & Principal

### David Poole

Building Vision. Creating Value. Delivering Results.

David Poole is the Founder and Principal of Veritas Development Group LLC, bringing more than two decades of experience in construction, land development, real estate investment, and project leadership.

Throughout his career, David has led projects from initial land acquisition and feasibility through planning, construction, and final delivery. His expertise spans residential communities, multifamily developments, mixed-use projects, commercial construction, and strategic real estate development.

Known for combining practical construction knowledge with long-term investment strategy, David works alongside investors, municipalities, consultants, architects, engineers, and contractors to transform opportunities into successful developments. His approach is built on disciplined planning, financial analysis, risk management, and attention to detail at every stage of the development process.

Under David's leadership, Veritas Development Group is committed to creating projects that strengthen communities while delivering lasting value for partners and investors. Every project reflects the company's core principles of integrity, excellence, transparency, and quality.

For David, development is more than building structures — it's about creating places where businesses grow, families thrive, and communities prosper.

Construction & Concrete Specialist

### Mike Poole

Originally from Bellingham, Washington, Mike Poole built his career across Vancouver Island's largest cities in residential, bridge, and commercial concrete construction. He brings 23 years of hands-on industry experience: thirteen years in residential construction, specializing in home building and foundations, before transitioning into bridge construction and large-scale commercial concrete projects.

Highly skilled at reading and interpreting large-scale construction plans, Mike specializes in concrete construction, foundations, and structural concrete — coordinating trades, leading field operations, and holding every pour to demanding quality standards. With decades of leadership and hands-on technical experience, he is known for delivering durable, high-quality work across a wide range of construction environments.

### Daniel Bailey

Daniel Bailey is a Co-Founder and Real Estate Advisor with Veritas Development Group, rooted in Lee's Summit, Missouri. Born and raised in the community, he has built a trusted reputation through integrity, hard work, and consistent, results-driven guidance. With extensive experience in the Missouri real estate market, Daniel represents buyers, sellers, investors, and developers across Lee's Summit, Kansas City, and the surrounding communities — bringing clarity, confidence, and strong direction to every transaction.

Daniel's career foundation with Keller Williams has given him a comprehensive understanding of the full real estate process, from identifying opportunities and evaluating risk to negotiating contracts, coordinating details, and closing with confidence. Beyond traditional brokerage support, he brings hands-on expertise in investing, land acquisition, development consulting, and project planning, helping clients pursue strategies that create long-term value.

Clients appreciate Daniel's straightforward communication, disciplined negotiation, and unwavering commitment to protecting their interests at every step. His local expertise, professional network, and passion for real estate consistently open doors — and close deals where others can't.

Daniel actively practices real estate with Keller Williams; his brokerage activities are performed through Keller Williams, separate from his development and advisory work with Veritas Development Group.
