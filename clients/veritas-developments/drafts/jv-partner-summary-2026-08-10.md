## 1. The Opportunity in One Paragraph

Veritas Development Group is seeking JV capital partners to execute two complementary developments in Lee's Summit, Missouri — the eastern growth corridor of the Kansas City metro: a grocery-anchored mixed-use community (**Prime Lee's Summit**) and a 57-lot executive single-family neighborhood (**Stonehaven Estates**). Both sit in Jackson County, a market entering a once-in-a-generation development wave (2026 FIFA World Cup, $3B Royals ballpark district, $653M event impact) with strong fundamentals: 93–96% multifamily occupancy, 95.4% retail occupancy, seller's-market absorption in the exact price band Stonehaven targets, and supply discipline (starts −35% YoY).
