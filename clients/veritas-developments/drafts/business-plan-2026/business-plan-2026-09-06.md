## Executive Summary

Veritas Development Group LLC is a Missouri-based real estate development, construction management, land services and development-services company. Its strategy combines near-term operating revenue with long-duration development value creation.

## Strategic Architecture

### 2026 Priorities

- Secure paying projects in the $10,000-$500,000 range and build a repeatable Greater Kansas City operating portfolio.

- Launch one Green Acres pilot using controlled downside, a minimum seven-acre acquisition screen and written investment gates.
- Develop repeat lender, investor, municipal, broker, buyer and strategic-partner relationships.

## 4. Green Acres — Development Certainty Platform

Green Acres is the land-banking and development-certainty platform powered by Veritas Development. It targets agricultural-edge and underutilized parcels and earns its return by resolving uncertainty in controlled stages—not by passively waiting for farmland appreciation.

### Five Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **Control before ownership.** Favor options, conditional contracts, seller carry and phased takedowns before an unconditional close.
2. **Underwrite backward from the exit.** Residual land value determines the maximum basis; the seller's asking price does not.
3. **Maintain optionality.** Every parcel requires at least two credible buyer classes and two exit routes.
4. **Demand before dirt.** Full horizontal construction requires verified buyers, competitive bids, permits, financing and phased absorption.
5. **Treat walking away as a successful decision when a gate fails.** Sunk-cost pressure never overrides the evidence standard.

## 13. Growth Strategy, KPIs & Risk Controls

### Management KPIs

### Principal Risk Controls

- No unconditional land close without title, access, utility, environmental, municipal, market and residual-basis support.
- No full horizontal notice to proceed without permits, competitive bids, contingency, financing and buyer/takedown evidence.
- No public-incentive credit in the base case until formally approved and collectible timing is modeled.

## 14. Veritas Advantage

### Definition of Operating Success

- Veritas establishes recurring operating revenue and a documented local project record.
- Weak land opportunities are rejected quickly and controlled downside is quantified before capital expansion.
- A buyer or lender can audit why a parcel is entitled or shovel-ready without relying on promotional claims.
- Investors can trace every capital draw to a gate, source document and written decision.
- Each completed or terminated parcel improves sourcing, municipal, cost and buyer intelligence for the next one.
