7-Touch Outreach Playbook — Veritas Lee's Summit Capital Raise

Why this isn't a typical cold-email sequence: KC FFO + wealth-management decision-makers receive 100+ sales pitches per week. A "60-90 word SaaS-style" cold email gets deleted before the second line. Veritas's play is high-trust, low-volume, relationship-driven — exactly the OPPOSITE of cold-email scale tactics. The cold-email-strategist skill's principles apply (peer proof, outcome focus, strategic ATL language, value-prop rotation). The cadence is replaced with a 7-touch, 60-90 day, in-person+written hybrid sequence.

Read this first: team-profile-daniel-bailey-2026-08-22.md (Daniel = primary warm-intro carrier) and kc-family-office-law-firm-channel-2026-08-22.md (Polsinelli/Lathrop = institutional channel).

Tactic-by-tactic templates

T1 — Daniel's warm-call to verified KC FFO program officers

Who: Daniel Bailey (Co-Founder, Veritas) Target: 5-7 verified FFO program officers at Kauffman, Hall, Bloch, Mariner, Moneta, NCF Heartland, The Signatry (per TAM #1 CSV — Tier 1 + Tier 2 verified rows) Channel: Phone (NOT email) — phone respects 506(b) pre-existing relationship Length: 5-7 minutes

Call script (Daniel voice, conversational, NOT scripted):

"Hi [Name], this is Daniel Bailey — we met at [specific event/neighborhood/school/YMCA/KW event — to be filled in by Daniel with the actual pre-existing context].

Quick reason I'm calling: I'm a co-founder now at Veritas Development Group, with David Poole. We're doing a $50-100M mixed-use + multifamily development in Lee's Summit, near the [Price Chopper / downtown LS — to be picked].

Two things I'm hoping you'd help me with:

  1. Are you still active in real estate allocations for [your foundation/family]? I know your portfolio is diversified but I want to make sure I'm not missing anything.

  2. Could I buy you a coffee? Not a pitch — David and I just want to introduce ourselves properly. I'm Lee's Summit born and raised, David's been doing development here for 22 years. We're building something long-term.

If now isn't the right time, totally fine — could I check back in [60/90 days]?"

What Daniel MUST know before this call:

  • The specific pre-existing context for each FFO (did they meet at a KC Chamber event? Through KW? At LSHS? At church?)
  • The current status of each FFO's real estate allocation (public 990s + LinkedIn)
  • Whether each FFO has previously co-invested in RE syndications (the killer signal)

Outcome: 5-7 coffees scheduled within 2 weeks. Track in the Veritas CRM (per klicksmartai-os-schematic skill).

What we send Daniel to prep: TAM #1 CSV filtered to Tier 1 + KC Tier 2 verified rows + the 5 gap-questions from team-profile-daniel-bailey-2026-08-22.md.

What changes if Daniel's T1 calls don't produce 5-7 coffees

If Daniel gets fewer than 4 coffee confirmations from his T1 calls, the playbook has a problem — it means Daniel's network doesn't reach the FFOs in TAM #1 as assumed.

Fallback sequence:

  1. Cold reframe: Switch T1 from "warm intro" to "introductory meeting request" via LinkedIn or email — but this drops the 506(b) safe harbor
  2. Add a T0 outreach to law firm first: Have Polsinelli partner (Frank Koranda) make the first call to FFO program officers with Daniel on the line — law firm pre-vetting restores the substantive relationship signal
  3. Add T0 outreach to KW LS network: Have KW LS agents introduce Veritas to FFO clients through their existing trusted-advisor relationships
