### What they fund (verified from pages)

**Bridge financing:**
- 6-24 months, interest-only
- Up to 75% LTV (as-is or as-complete)
- Term sheet in 48 hours, funding in 5-10 business days
- Asset-based underwriting (property + exit, not credit)
- First OR second mortgage positions
- Commercial, multi-residential, industrial, retail, office, mixed-use

**Construction financing:**
- Interest-only, advanced in draws
- Underwritten on total project cost + as-complete value + team strength
- Roll into permanent financing or sale at exit
- Works with experienced AND capable-but-newer teams

**Land acquisition/development:**
- $2M+, up to 50-65% LTV depending on entitlement
- 12-36 months, IO
- Covers raw acquisition + horizontal development + soft costs
- "Bridge-to-construction" sequence (land → construction with same lender)

**Mezzanine/gap financing:**
- Subordinate to senior, ahead of equity
- Second mortgage +/or equity pledge
- Total combined leverage 85-90% LTC/LTV
- Behind bank/institutional senior (intercreditor negotiated)

**Operating LOC:**
- Flexible working capital
- For seasonal swings, payroll, inventory, receivables gaps
- Draw what you need, when you need it
- "Without the long approval cycles or restrictive covenants of traditional lenders"

**Factoring:**
- Sell unpaid invoices at a discount for immediate cash
- Up to 90% of invoice within 2 days
- For slow-paying-client businesses (logistics, manufacturing, services)

| Product type | Full-doc commercial mortgage | 7 private lending products |
| Speed | 30-90 day close | 5-10 day close (bridge) |
| Deal size | $200K-$5M | **$2M+ minimum** on most products |
| Geography | Canada | **BC/AB/ON/QC only** (gaps in SK/MB/Atlantic) |
| Borrower type | Owner-occupied + investor + self-employed | Developer + investor + larger business |
| Underwriting | Reasonability + FICO floor | Asset-based, exit-driven |

**The two lenders cover different borrower needs + different deal tiers:**
- Glasslake: small commercial, owner-occupied, $200K-$500K deals, all of Canada
- Triple Queens: developer/investor, $2M+ deals, BC/AB/ON/QC, speed-critical situations
