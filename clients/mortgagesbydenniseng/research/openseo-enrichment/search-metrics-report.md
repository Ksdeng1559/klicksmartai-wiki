## 13. Bottom line for the build plan

**The keyword universe is winnable.** Average KD 22, 18% zero-competition terms, 36% easy (KD ≤ 20). Most high-CPC terms (factoring, A/R, line of credit) have zero or near-zero competition. The hard terms (BDC, government grants, head terms) are not where the comparison site should focus its first 90 days — those are the 6-12 month goals.

**First 90 days publishing priority:**
1. 6 KD-0 commercial mortgage / factoring / LOC pages (immediate ranking potential)
2. 4 KD-0-5 long-tail CPC-rich terms (highest revenue per visitor)
3. 5-7 medium-tier T2 / T3 pillar pages (build site authority)
4. 3-4 BDC / CSBFP government comparison pages (Swoop can't touch)

**By month 6:** Site should rank for ~30-40 of the 78 enriched keywords, with priority on the 10 highest-CPC + low-KD terms first.
