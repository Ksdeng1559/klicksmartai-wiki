## Product set (US arm)

Bizcap (also) is the world's most open-minded funder

| Product | Type | Min revenue | Min credit | Approval | Funding | Max | Term | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue-Based Financing (RBF) | Purchase of future receivables | $75K/mo | (not stated; "bad credit OK") | within 24 hrs | next day | $2M | daily/weekly debits, completes when paid | Inventory, payroll, seasonal, marketing, hire, expansion, equipment, growth |
| Line of Capital (LOC) | Revolver — discounted purchase of future receivables, 12-mo window | $20K/mo | 550 FICO | 3 hrs | next day | $2M | daily/weekly debits, restores as paid | Cash flow gaps, payroll, inventory, growth |
| Line of Capital Ultra (LOC Ultra) | Revolver — 6-mo window, 4-week payment discount | $30K/mo | (similar to LOC) | 3 hrs | next day | $2M | daily/weekly, 4-week discount on early pay-off | Businesses that draw >1x/year, repeat inventory/materials purchases |

**Eligibility common to all:** EIN, ≥1 year in business, US-based.

**Note on legal structure:** None of these are loans. All three are contractually a "purchase of future receivables" — no APR, no compounding interest. Factor rate disclosed upfront. Early-completion discount is given (most funders penalize this; Bizcap incentivizes it).

**Soft-credit policy:** Soft inquiry only; no hard pull on the client's credit.
