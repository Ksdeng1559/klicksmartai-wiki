Embracing the Future from a Position of Strength

Throughout our history, we’ve built a strong foundation as a successful company and reliable partner. As we continue to grow, our focus remains the same: simplifying access to capital so our clients can thrive.

After reflecting on our last 40 years as a company, we are looking forward to the next 40. At our core, we are passionate about helping entrepreneurs build their businesses.

Our incredible team stands strong in the face of challenges, committed to change, growth, and success. It is the only way to build a company that can thrive for another 40 years.

Accord is Canada’s most dynamic commercial finance company. As the pace of change accelerates, unlocking opportunity takes more than ambition; it takes financial strength, deep insight, and a relentless pursuit of simplicity. We provide growth capital, simply delivered, so our clients can thrive.

Fast, versatile financing solutions for companies in transition. We leverage our unique combination of financial strength, deep experience and independent thinking to craft winning financial solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises from coast to coast.

We’re driven by seeing our clients thrive. That’s why we’ve assembled the industry’s foremost team of experts who are committed to building lasting relationships and passionate about finding solutions to unlock our clients’ potential.

Our Values

    Human

    Put People First

    We believe in building close, collaborative relationships. That is why our top priority will always be the wellbeing and success of our clients. We seize every opportunity to support them as a trusted partner, backed by our team of passionate, like-minded, and diverse team of experts.

    Innovation

    Challenge the Status Quo

    We believe there is always room for improvement. We’re constantly evolving and thinking of new ways to be more efficient and advance the way we do business. We listen, learn, and adjust in order to deliver a greater impact and unrivaled experience for our clients.

    Curiosity

    Stay Curious

    We ask a lot of questions, because we are truly interested. Information and ideas are critical assets, which is why we bring our full attention and enthusiasm when learning about our clients. We strive to identify new perspectives in order to continually improve our products and processes.

    Success

    Raise the Bar

    We’re determined to see our clients succeed. With decades of knowledge and expertise, our foundation enables us to set our sights higher – pushing ourselves and our clients – to achieve those expectations by overcoming challenges, taking necessary risks and elevating outcomes.

    Trust

    Be Straight Up

    We speak from the heart and are true to our word. That’s why many of our relationships have held strong for decades. We pride ourselves on building each partnership with a reliable foundation of honesty, transparency, sincerity and care.
