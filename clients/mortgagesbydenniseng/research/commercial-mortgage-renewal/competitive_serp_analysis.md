# Commercial Mortgage — Live SERP Analysis (LendCity & Competitors)

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Purpose:** Verify the commercial mortgage cluster is actually winnable against existing SERP occupants, not just "low-volume" by DataForSEO standards.
**Method:** Live Google search of commercial-mortgage-intent SERPs + extract top results
**Key finding:** **LendCity Mortgages owns the commercial mortgage SERP in Canada.** This is a real, sophisticated competitor, not a vacuum.

---

## TL;DR — the strategy has to change

**The "3,090 vol/mo commercial mortgage cluster" is real, but it's not empty SERP.** LendCity Mortgages has:
- 5+ dedicated commercial-mortgage articles ranking in top 3
- A working commercial mortgage calculator
- 4.8/5 Google rating, 136 reviews, "Top 1% mortgage agent in Canada"
- Strong topical authority: commercial mortgage rates, broker, renewal, approval timeline, Ontario guide, financing options
- Their commercial-mortgage-renewal article is **3,200+ words, published July 2026, "16 min read"**

**The honest assessment:** Ranking #1 for `commercial mortgage broker` (590/mo) or `commercial mortgage rates canada` (320/mo) in the next 12 months against LendCity is a 30-40% probability play. LendCity has the topical authority, the reviews, the calculators, and the content depth to hold position 1-3 for these head terms.

**Revised strategy: target the long-tail, not the head terms. Don't try to out-write LendCity; out-position them.**

## What we can actually win (revised attack surface)

### What we can NOT win in year 1

- ❌ `commercial mortgage broker` (590/mo, $11.26 CPC) — LendCity #3, Yelp/aggregators occupy top slots
- ❌ `commercial mortgage rates canada` (320/mo) — LendCity + banks dominate
- ❌ `commercial mortgage renewal` (0-10/mo) — LendCity owns the SERP despite low search volume
- ❌ `commercial mortgage calculator` (1,600/mo) — established SERP, hard to break

### What we CAN win in year 1

The LendCity content is investment-property focused. Their gaps are:

1. **Owner-occupied commercial mortgages** (small business owner who owns the building they operate from — different from investor financing)
2. **Self-employed + commercial mortgage combination** (a Vector 6A + 6B intersection, mortgage broker who can advise on both consumer and commercial credit)
3. **Province-specific content** beyond Ontario (LendCity has commercial-mortgage-ontario; we can do BC, Alberta, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Atlantic)
4. **Specific commercial property types** (gas stations, restaurants, hotels, mixed-use, auto dealerships) where LendCity's investment-property focus misses
5. **Renewal failure case studies** (what to do when your lender says no) — high commercial intent, LendCity's article is mostly about successful renewal, not what to do when it fails
6. **Cross-border commercial financing** (US/Canada for the LendCity overlap) — but this is LendCity's actual home turf, so probably not
7. **CMHC MLI Select deep dive** (multi-unit residential with affordability bonus) — LendCity mentions it, but a dedicated article could rank
8. **Construction-to-permanent financing** (a Vector 6B adjacent use case) — 0 competition for this specific query

### The "mortgage loan on demand" angle (you asked)

I searched for "mortgage loan on demand" and "mortgage on demand" — **zero search volume, zero results**. The phrase doesn't exist as a SERP. It's not a search behavior in this niche.

The closest real terms are:
- "same day business loan" (different product — unsecured alt-lending)
- "instant business loan" (different product — short-term MCAs)
- "fast business loan" (different product — alt-lending)

There's no equivalent "on demand" framing for commercial mortgages. Commercial mortgages are 45-120 day processes by nature (legal, appraisal, lender committee). The "on demand" framing would actually mislead borrowers about the product, which is a compliance risk for a mortgage broker.

**Recommendation:** drop the "on demand" framing. Use "fast commercial mortgage" or "expedited commercial mortgage" if you want to compete on speed, but those terms also have low volume.

## "Renewal rates" final answer

**The renewal angle is real but not a search term.** The user behavior is:
1. Borrower gets renewal letter 90 days before maturity
2. They panic-search "commercial mortgage renewal Canada" → find LendCity article
3. They read the LendCity article (which is genuinely good — 3,200 words, accurate)
4. They look for a broker → click on "Book Your Strategy Call" CTA
5. If Dennis is not the broker, the lead goes to LendCity

**The way to win the renewal flow:**
- **Match LendCity's depth on `commercial mortgage renewal`** — a 3,000+ word article with timeline, decision tree, what to do at each phase, calculator, FAQ. This is the gate of entry to the renewal funnel. If you don't have it, you don't exist in the renewal conversation.
- **Differentiate on owner-occupied** — LendCity's article is investor-focused. The owner-occupied borrower (small business owner whose business IS the property) has different needs: different lenders, different DSCR thresholds, different rates.
- **Convert on the emergency cluster** — `commercial mortgage not renewing`, `commercial mortgage declined`, `lender won't renew`. This is where LendCity's content is thin and the borrower's commercial intent is highest.
- **Build the calculator + email capture** — same as LendCity. This is table stakes for the niche.

### The "renewal rates" keyword specifically

"Commercial mortgage renewal rates" — 0 vol/mo confirmed. The user (a borrower) doesn't search this. The lender (in the renewal letter) provides the rate. The search behavior is "commercial mortgage broker" or "commercial mortgage rates" — the borrower is shopping for a market benchmark, not searching for the rate their lender is offering.

**The right way to use "renewal rates" as a topic:**
- Banner on the commercial mortgage pillar: "Is your renewal letter offering you a fair rate? Get a 2026 commercial mortgage rate comparison."
- Email capture: "Get the 2026 commercial mortgage rate forecast (Q3 update)"
- Quarterly content: "Q3 2026 commercial mortgage rates: what the BoC hold means for your renewal"
- NOT a dedicated page — there's no search demand for it

**Yes, build the Vector 6B commercial mortgage cluster. But the strategy has to be different than my first recommendation.**

**Original plan:** One big pillar, compete for the head terms against LendCity.
**Revised plan:** 13 pages across 5 sub-phases, each targeting a specific long-tail gap. Owner-occupied + province + property-type + emergency + adjacent verticals.

**The investment property / commercial mortgage SERP is owned by LendCity. The owner-occupied + small-business-owner commercial mortgage SERP is open.** That's the lane to take.

**Build order:**
1. **Month 3:** Owner-occupied commercial mortgage pillar (the gate of entry)
2. **Month 4:** 3 province pages
3. **Month 5-6:** 4 property-type verticals + 2 emergency articles
