## Strategic verdict — answer to the open question

**Q (from spec):** Is the tariff-loan niche real-but-zero-rated (CRA-debt pattern), or is there no measurable query volume (build-ahead-of-wave)?  
**A:** **Build-ahead-of-wave — and the wave is too small to ride by itself.**

The 110-keyword test shows the demand pool sits at the generic level (`business loan`, `small business loan`, `working capital loan`, `import export finance`), not at the tariff-specific level. Tariff searches are spiking per Google Trends qualitative data, but those searchers type generic queries — they don't yet think of "tariff loan" as a product category. Nobody is bidding on `tariff business loan` either (CPC is null/zero across 21 of 22 seeds), so it's not a hidden commercial-intent niche the way CRA-debt was.

### Recommendation

1. **PRIMARY ACTION: pivot to generic business-loan + import-finance + working-capital content.** The 14,230 CA monthly searches and $53.85 avg CPC across the Business Loan + Competitor layers is where the existing Rail A/B products already convert. These keyword clusters should drive the Rail B landing-page strategy, with tariff-context as a single article (not a category).

2. **Single high-value tariff article: "BDC tariff loan vs private lender"** — only keyword with both volume (10/mo) and a competitor-free SERP (BDC + Canada.ca own it, no private lender). Claim this with one well-structured piece on KlickSmartAI.com. Expected ranking: top-3 within 30 days. Compounding value: positions KlickSmartAI as the alternative-lender angle for the next tariff spike.

3. **Build a tariff-trigger content sprint, not a tariff category.** Park the 21 zero-volume seeds in a `tariff-wave-playbook/drafts/` folder with the publication gate set to: any week where Google Trends "Canada tariff" > +50% WoW. When the next wave hits, this batch auto-publishes inside 48 hours and captures the spike.

4. **For BC/AB/ON landing pages**: don't optimize `tariff loan [province]` — those are zero. Instead use `business loan [province]` (these have real volume) with tariff-context callouts in the body copy. Regional page ranking depends on the generic head terms, not the tariff tail.

5. **For the Swoop/BDC gap**: Swoop owns the broker-marketplace angle, BDC owns the crown-corp angle. The open positioning for KlickSmartAI in the same niche is **specialized bridge / cash-urgency / alt-lender** — `bridge loan for import costs`, `cash flow loan tariff increase`, `tariff surcharge loan` are zero-volume but also zero-competition. Build those as pillar pages once the next tariff wave triggers, and Swoop's broker-marketplace model + BDC's crown-corp position leave a clear alternative-funder gap.
