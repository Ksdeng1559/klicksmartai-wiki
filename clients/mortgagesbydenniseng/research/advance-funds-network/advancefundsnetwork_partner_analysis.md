## Partner Program (Critical Section)

### Became a Partner — what's offered

**URL:** https://advancefundsnetwork.ca/become-a-partner/

Key terms disclosed publicly:

- **Commission:** Industry-leading; **top-tier partners earn up to 50% of net revenue per funded deal**
- **Funding speed:** **Same-day** approvals on deals they accept ("Approved Deals Funded Same Day")
- **Network size:** Self-described as **"Largest referral network in North America"**
- **Process:** Simple portal upload OR co-branded embedded link OR JSON/API integration OR plain email submission
- **Servicing:** AFN handles everything from docs to underwriting to funding + renewals — partner stays "in the loop" but doesn't do application work
- **Tracking:** Real-time partner portal
- **Coverage:** Both USA AND Canada — works for Dennis's BC personal/proprietor brokerage
- **Compliance:** Explicitly states they "adhere to the laws of each state and province" (Canadian regulatory compliance built in)

### Partners vs Affiliates (the 2-track program)

| Feature | **Partner** | Affiliate |
|---|---|---|
| Who fits | Brokers, ISOs, consultants, CPAs, advisors | Marketers, publishers, content creators, SaaS/fintech |
| What you refer | **Client with full application + financials ready** | Anyone interested in business funding |
| How you earn | **Revenue split on funded deal** | $150+ per qualified lead |
| When you get paid | When client funds | Monday after lead is billable (~48 hr) |
| Ongoing role | Hands-on through funding | Hands-off after handoff |

**For Dennis:** the **Partner** track is the right fit (he's a broker-of-record, not a marketer). Affiliate track would be a missed opportunity given his broker status.

### Partner Sign Up — qualification form

Sign-up URL: `https://advancefundsnetwork.ca/become-a-partner/sign-up/`

Asks for:

1. Sales force size (1 to 25+ reps)
2. Active clients managed (<10 to 200+)
3. Existing products sold (selectable: SBA Loans, Term Loans, LOC, MCA, Equipment Financing, Invoice Factoring, RE/CRE Loans, etc.) — **NOTE: "Broker — Commercial Mortgage" is in the dropdown** = explicit acknowledgment of CRE broker category
4. New financing inquiries/month (0-5 to 100+)
5. Deal-file submission preference (Simple Portal / Embedded Link / API JSON / Email)
6. Biggest gap in current lending network (Buy Box for high-risk / Speed for same-day / Volume $500K+ / Tech / Support)
7. "Deals ready to fund right now?" (Yes / Soon / No)
8. Industry dropdown — **"Broker — Commercial Mortgage" is explicitly listed** as an industry category

**Insight:** AFN's intake form was *built* with commercial mortgage brokers in mind. The "Broker — Commercial Mortgage" option exists in two dropdowns (industry + products sold). This is a strong signal that AFN actively solicits + supports Dennis's exact broker profile.

### What AFN does NOT do

> *"Advance Funds Network does not provide funding for startups."*

**Implication:** When sub-startup or pre-revenue leads come through our funnel (early-stage founders, pre-revenue side businesses), we route them to BDC (Start-up Loan $150K) or refer them elsewhere — NOT to AFN.
