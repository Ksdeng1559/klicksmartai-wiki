# Missouri State Capital-Gains Advantage
## A Structural Optimizer for Missouri-Domiciled Real Estate Investors

**Prepared for:** Qualified family offices considering Missouri real estate opportunities
**Subject project:** Evermont District — Lee's Summit, Missouri (mixed-use)
**Status:** Research-backed · v2.0 (re-anchored) · 2026-09-06

---

## 1. The Opportunity, Precisely Stated

Since **January 1, 2025**, Missouri has enacted a **100% Missouri-state capital-gains
deduction** for individuals and pass-through entities (Missouri House Bill 594, signed
July 10, 2025). For a **Missouri-domiciled investor** holding real estate through a
pass-through entity, this eliminates **Missouri-state** capital-gains tax on the disposition.

This is a real, source-verified advantage — one Missouri is among the first states to
offer. It is a **structural optimizer for MO-domiciled capital**, not a universal "tax-free"
claim. Federal taxation and property-level depreciation recapture **still apply** (see §5).

---

## 2. Verified Legal Mechanics (HB 594)

| Entity | Effective | Missouri-state result | Verified by |
|--------|-----------|----------------------|-------------|
| Individual (MO-resident) | Jan 1, 2025 | 100% deduction of capital gains from MO taxable income | MO DOR; Amundsen Davis; Stinson |
| LLC / LP / S-Corp (pass-through) | Jan 1, 2025 | Gain flows through; 100% deduction for resident members | Anders; MO-PTE |
| C-Corporation | **Trigger** | 100% deduction once top individual rate ≤4.5% (earliest 2028) | Kutak Rock; MO Senate; Wipfli |

- Applies to **short- and long-term** gains; **no holding-period requirement**.
- Current MO top individual rate: **4.7%** (2025, MO DOR). Trigger is **not yet met**.

**Structure for pass-through.** Realized gains at the entity level are not taxed at
source — they flow to members. For MO-domiciled members, that gain is then **100%
deductible** from MO state taxable income. This is the mechanics; it is accurate.

---

## 3. The Disposition Decision That Matters

How you sell determines where the gain is sourced — and therefore whether MO treats it as exempt.

| Disposition | Sourcing | MO result |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **Asset sale** (physical deed) | Situs of the property | Gain apportioned to the property's state — may be taxable outside MO |
| **Equity sale** (sale of LLC membership interest) | Residency of the owner | For a MO-resident individual, gain may be **wholly exempt** from MO state tax |

**Advice (confirmed by Stinson LLP):** an equity sale of the holding entity lets a
MO-resident owner keep more of the gain MO-state-tax-free. **Non-MO investors**: your
home state may still tax the income even if Missouri does not — plan accordingly.

## 5. What "Tax-Free" Does NOT Mean (Required Disclosure)

**Accuracy protects credibility.** The 100% deduction is **Missouri-state** only. Still apply:

- **Federal capital gains** (max 20% long-term + 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax).
- **Depreciation recapture** under IRC §1250 (**25% federal**) on the residential/rental component — material for a 732-unit multifamily project. §1250 recapture is NOT a capital gain and is NOT excluded by HB 594.
- **§1031 exchange** is unavailable on a *taxable exit*; 1031 deferral vs. the HB 594 deduction are **alternative** strategies to weigh with a tax advisor.

**Net-net:** the MO-state deduction is a genuine after-tax tailwind for MO-domiciled
pass-through investors. It does not make the exit "100% tax-free" in absolute terms.
Use precise language; it holds up better under scrutiny.
