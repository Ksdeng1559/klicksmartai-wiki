## Why Not 25? (Market-Structure Insight)

Missouri's real-estate-holding wealth is **concentrated in a handful of dynasty
developer-operators** (Hunt, Kemper/CCommerce, Hermann/Busch, Hall/Hallmark) rather than
distributed across a broad field of allocator family offices. This is corroborated three ways:

1. Our own discovery: repeated sweeps surfaced service firms and brokerages, not allocator FOs.
2. FINTRX's industrial-FO list is entirely out-of-state.
3. Prior Axial scan (2026-09-05) found Missouri FOs, none real-estate-focused.

**Implication for Veritas:** a cold-mail blast at 25 "MO family offices" over-fits the thesis.
The **real channel** is a smaller set of dynasty developer-operators (who already build what
Veritas builds) plus **referral channels** (Matter, Harmony, Alpha, Vineyard, Commerce Trust,
Clarity) that place family capital into products like Evermont.
