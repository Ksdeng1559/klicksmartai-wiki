## Score: 23/100 — CONDITIONAL

GPC Development comes in at 23/100 — a CONDITIONAL tier. The site has a real, winnable search opportunity in the Vancouver multifamily / general contractor market (KD 12-26 for the entry keywords, dominated by mid-tier local GCs rather than national firms), but the technical foundation makes every page effectively indistinguishable to Google. Every one of your 21 pages currently shares the same title tag and meta description, and every page is missing its H1 — so Google can't tell your homepage from your portfolio pages in search results. Until those two foundational issues are fixed, no SEO spend converts. With one developer sprint + the Foundation bundle, the recoverable traffic value is **~$280K/year** — break-even in month 4, ~35× return over 12 months. Recommend proceeding on a scoped 3-month pilot.

**CONDITIONAL** means: Fix the broken foundation first (Phase 1, $2,500, two weeks); the rest of the engagement is quoted contingent on that.
