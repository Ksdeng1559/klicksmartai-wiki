# Step 06 — Referral Channels

The trust infrastructure that converts cold outreach into warm intros. Output: `projects/<developer-slug>/06-referral-channels.md`.

## Channel types

| Channel type | What it provides | Best for | Compliance lane |
|---|---|---|---|
| Law firm (FO practice) | Pre-vetted HNW / FFO clients; tax + estate planning context | Lane 4 LP equity (506(b) relationship) | Lane 4 |
| CPA / accounting firm | Tax + wealth advisory; pre-vetted HNW | Lane 4 LP equity | Lane 4 |
| Wealth manager / RIA | Investment advisory; AUM-based relationships | Lane 4 LP equity (smaller checks) | Lane 4 |
| Mortgage broker (commercial) | Lender relationships; capital-markets desk | Lane 1 + Lane 3 debt | Lane 1 / Lane 3 |
| Mortgage broker (residential) | Residential origination | Lane 2 | Lane 2 |
| Securities broker-dealer | Institutional placement agent | Lane 4 (institutional) | Lane 4 |
| Banker / investment bank | Capital markets advisory | Lane 1 + Lane 4 | Lane 1 / Lane 4 |
| Family-office consultant | FFO introductions | Lane 4 | Lane 4 |
| Industry association | Peer introductions | All lanes | All |
| Personal network | Warm intros via principals | All lanes | All |

## How to build the referral-channel map

1. List sponsor's existing relationships (law firm, CPA, WM, broker)
2. List market's top firms in each channel type
3. For each, identify a named partner contact (where possible)
4. Classify each by lane
5. Rank by warm-intro path strength (existing relationship > known-of > cold)

## Per-market playbook

For each project market, build a list of:

- **Top 3-5 law firms** with FO / trust / tax / RE practice
- **Top 3-5 CPA firms** with HNW practice
- **Top 3-5 wealth managers** with multi-family-office or institutional practice
- **Top 3-5 commercial mortgage brokers** (if Lane 1)
- **Top 3-5 family-office consultants** (if Lane 4 institutional)

## Outreach to referral channels

Different cadence than direct lender outreach:

| # | Day | Channel | Owner | Target | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Warm intro (via existing relationship) | Sponsor | Firm partner | 30-min call |
| 2 | 14 | Lunch / dinner (1-on-1) | Sponsor | Firm partner | Build relationship |
| 3 | 45 | Co-host dinner (12-20 invitees) | Sponsor + firm partner | FFO / HNW / WM invitees | Forum / network |
| 4 | 75 | 1-on-1 with specific investor | Sponsor + firm partner | Specific FFO / WM | LP solicitation |
| 5 | 105 | Term sheet | Sponsor + counsel | FFO / WM | Receive term sheet |
