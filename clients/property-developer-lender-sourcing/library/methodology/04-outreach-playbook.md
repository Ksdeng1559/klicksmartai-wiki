# Step 04 — Outreach Playbook

The 7-touch plan that converts a scored shortlist into a term sheet. Output: `projects/<developer-slug>/05-outreach-sequence.md`.

## The 7 touches (Lane 1 / Lane 3 — debt)

| # | Day | Channel | Owner | Target | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | LinkedIn (warm intro preferred) | Sponsor / KlickSmartAI | Origination contact | Accept connection |
| 2 | 3 | Email (post-connection) | Sponsor | Origination contact | Schedule 30-min call |
| 3 | 7 | Call (30 min) | Sponsor | Origination contact | Pre-qualify, share project teaser |
| 4 | 14 | Project teaser + NDA | Sponsor | Origination contact | Open data room |
| 5 | 21 | Submission (full package) | Sponsor | Underwriting team | Formal submission |
| 6 | 35 | Follow-up | Sponsor | Underwriting team | Status check |
| 7 | 45 | Term sheet / soft circle | Sponsor / legal | Origination contact | Receive term sheet |

## The 7 touches (Lane 4 — equity, Reg D 506(b))

| # | Day | Channel | Owner | Target | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Warm intro (via referral channel) | Sponsor | LP principal | Accept meeting |
| 2 | 7 | Dinner / 1-on-1 | Sponsor | LP principal | Relationship, no solicitation |
| 3 | 21 | Teaser + confidential intro | Sponsor + counsel | LP principal | NDA, soft circle |
| 4 | 35 | Data room access | Sponsor | LP principal + advisors | Diligence |
| 5 | 49 | Term sheet / LOI | Sponsor + counsel | LP principal | Negotiate terms |
| 6 | 70 | Document negotiation | Sponsor + counsel + LP counsel | LP principal + counsel | Close docs |
| 7 | 90 | Funded | Sponsor | LP principal | Wire |

## Channel selection rules

- **Lane 1 (CRE debt):** LinkedIn + email + call. Origination contact.
- **Lane 2 (residential):** state-licensed MLO only. Email + call.
- **Lane 3 (mezz):** LinkedIn + email + call. Origination + structured credit contact.
- **Lane 4 (equity, Reg D 506(b)):** warm intro REQUIRED. No cold outreach. Law-firm or CPA referral channel.
- **Lane 4 (equity, Reg D 506(c)):** general solicitation OK if all-accredited verified. LinkedIn + email + verified-accredited funnel.

## Personalization rules

Every touch should reference:
- Sponsor's prior completed project (or another fact from intake)
- The specific project being raised (not generic)
- Why this lender (asset class, geography, or relationship reason)
- The specific ask (debt size, equity check, recourse, structure)

Avoid: generic "we are seeking capital" outreach.

## Compliance touchpoints

| Touch | Compliance action |
|---|---|
| 1 (LinkedIn) | No specific solicitation. Relationship building. |
| 2 (email) | Confidentiality notice. No offer / solicitation language. |
| 3 (call) | Disclose sponsor identity, asset, structure, lane. No specific terms. |
| 4 (NDA) | Mutual NDA executed. |
| 5 (submission) | Full disclosure. Reg D disclaimer if Lane 4. |
| 6 (follow-up) | Track response time + reasons. |
| 7 (term sheet) | Counsel-led negotiation. Compliance disclaimer attached. |
