# Capital Stack Design

The 5-layer model that gets applied to every property developer project. **Design the stack first, then match lenders** — not the other way around.

## The 5 layers

| Layer | Layer name | Typical % of total | Risk position | Typical cost | Compliance lane |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Senior debt | 50-70% | First-out | Lowest (SOFR + spread) | Lane 1 / Lane 2 |
| 2 | Mezzanine debt | 5-15% | Second-out | Mid (SOFR + higher spread + PIK option) | Lane 3 |
| 3 | Preferred equity | 5-10% | Behind debt, ahead of common | Higher (pref return + PIK) | Lane 4 |
| 4 | JV equity (co-sponsor / LP) | 10-25% | Behind pref, ahead of sponsor | Equity (pref + promote) | Lane 4 |
| 5 | Sponsor equity (GP) | 5-15% | Last-out, control | Equity (residual + promote) | Lane 4 |

## Worked example — $30M multifamily

| Layer | $ | % | Lender type |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Senior debt (construction → perm) | $20M | 66.7% | Local bank or national bridge |
| Mezz + Pref equity | $4M | 13.3% | Mezz fund or family-office pref |
| Sponsor + JV equity | $6M | 20.0% | Sponsor + LP co-invest |
| **Total** | **$30M** | **100%** | |

## Lifecycle mapping

Different lender types fit different project stages:

| Stage | Senior debt | Mezz | Equity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw land | — | — | Sponsor + JV |
| Entitlement | A&D lender | — | Sponsor + JV |
| Horizontal | Land / dev lender | — | Sponsor + JV |
| Vertical construction | Construction lender | Mezz | Sponsor + JV + LP |
| Bridge / lease-up | Bridge lender | Mezz | Sponsor + JV + LP |
| Permanent takeout | Permanent lender (life co, CMBS, bank) | — | Sponsor (refi out JV/LP) |
