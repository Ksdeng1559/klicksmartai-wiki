# Compliance

This workspace produces **financial, legal, and securities-adjacent content**. All artifacts must clear the gates below before delivery.

## Hard rules

1. **No fabricated lender data.** Every lender program, rate, fee, check size, or contact must be either (a) cited from a public source, (b) cited from a private but verifiable source (term sheet, agreement, call notes), or (c) labeled `TBD — to be confirmed by sponsor`.
2. **No Reg D solicitation without legal review.** Any outreach artifact (email, LinkedIn, dinner invite) that solicits an LP commitment is a Reg D 506(b) or 506(c) communication. It must carry the appropriate disclaimer and be reviewed by sponsor's securities counsel before sending.
3. **No mortgage broker activity without state license.** Daniel Bailey and any other individual originating residential mortgage or acting as a commercial mortgage broker must hold the appropriate state license. Activities outside the license carve-out must be referred to a licensed MLO.
4. **No securities activity without registration.** Promoting LP interests, fund interests, or any security requires registration (or exemption) with FINRA/SEC and applicable state regulators. KlickSmartAI does NOT register; KlickSmartAI produces methodology, intake, and outreach DRAFTS for the sponsor's licensed team to execute.
5. **No plain-text credentials in delivered artifacts.** Any exported summary must use `[REDACTED]` for passwords, API keys, account numbers, and PII.

## Compliance routing — 4 lanes

For every capital source, classify the lane BEFORE drafting the outreach:

| Lane | Lane owner | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lane 1: Commercial mortgage / construction debt** | Sponsor's commercial debt team or licensed commercial mortgage broker | Senior + mezz debt on commercial / multifamily / mixed-use | UMB, Commerce, Central Bank KC, ACORE, Parkview, Thorofare |
| **Lane 2: Residential mortgage** | Licensed MLO (state license required) | 1-4 unit residential, condo, SFR | BDC, Glasslake, AFN, broker-only |
| **Lane 3: Mezzanine debt** | Structured-credit team or licensed broker | Subordinated debt | Bloomfield mezz, Mesa West, Ares |
| **Lane 4: Securities — preferred equity, LP, JV, fund interests** | Registered professionals only (RIA, broker-dealer, securities counsel) | Equity raises, fund formation, LP solicitation | Veritas Capital Markets securities counsel, registered RIA |

**Default for KlickSmartAI artifacts:** we draft the **methodology, intake, lender profile, and outreach template**. The sponsor (and their licensed professionals) execute. We never execute a solicitation.

## Disclaimers

Every client-facing artifact in `deliverables/` should carry:

> **Compliance.** This document is for informational and planning purposes only. Nothing herein is an offer to sell or solicitation to buy any security. KlickSmartAI does not provide legal, tax, securities, or mortgage-origination advice. Sponsor is responsible for engagement of licensed professionals (MLO, RIA, broker-dealer, securities counsel) before any solicitation, commitment, or closing.

## State-by-state license check (Lane 2 only)

Before any Lane 2 outreach, confirm:
- Sponsor (or executing broker) holds state MLO license in target state
- NMLS unique identifier disclosed if required
- State-specific disclosure language attached to outreach

## Reg D exemption record

For any Lane 4 outreach, sponsor should file Form D with SEC within 15 days of first sale.
