# Voice & Tone

This workspace is **methodology + client-facing lender deliverables**. Voice rules:

- **Direct, evidence-based.** Cite lender name, program, source URL when known.
- **Tables over prose** for lender comparisons, stack layers, scoring.
- **Compliance-forward.** Every outreach artifact carries the appropriate Reg D / state-license / MLO / securities disclaimer.
- **No fabrication.** "No data" is a valid answer; never invent a lender's product, contact, or program.
- **Conservative numeric defaults.** When a check size, fee range, or rate is not verified, label as `TBD per underwriting` or `to be confirmed by sponsor`.

Do not paste proprietary lender rate sheets, term sheets, or non-public underwriting criteria into this workspace.
