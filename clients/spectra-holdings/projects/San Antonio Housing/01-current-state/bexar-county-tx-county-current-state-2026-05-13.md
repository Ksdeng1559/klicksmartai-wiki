# Bexar County, TX — County Current State Report

> *"The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few."* — Matthew 9:37

San Antonio is the **7th largest city in the United States** and one of its most housing-burdened mid-size metros. Bexar County carries a structural deficit of 115,000+ housing units, an eviction rate that has more than doubled since COVID, and a homeless population growing at 6.8% year-over-year — yet it also operates one of the most effective homeless response systems in the country, with unsheltered reduction outpacing national trends by 25 percentage points over five years. The data paints a large-scale, high-need, high-opportunity market for an MCF deployment.

Proposed city target: 2-to-20 unit multi-family projects. Smaller projects lack economies of scale and face financing gaps. The urban core is **losing families** to surrounding counties.
