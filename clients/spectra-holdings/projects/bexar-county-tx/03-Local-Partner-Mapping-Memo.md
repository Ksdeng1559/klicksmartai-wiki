# Bexar County, TX — Local Partner Mapping Memo

## Purpose

This memo identifies the key local institutions, housing authorities, CDFIs, faith-based organizations, landowners, and developers that are relevant to a Spectra Holdings MCF pilot in Bexar County / San Antonio, TX.

It is designed to support initial outreach, partnership identification, and capital stack assembly.

## 7. Recommended Engagement Sequence

| Step | Partner | Objective |
|------|---------|-----------|

| 4 | Eastside Promise / Council District 8 | Identify infill pipeline and community sentiment |
| 5 | Catholic Diocese of San Antonio | Explore land or faith-capital alignment |

| 7 | Habitat for Humanity / Haven for Hope | Coordinate anti-eviction and stabilization strategy |
