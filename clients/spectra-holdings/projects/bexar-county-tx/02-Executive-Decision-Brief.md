# Bexar County, TX — Executive Decision Brief

## Purpose

This brief is the executive-facing synthesis document for Spectra Holdings leadership and MCF investment committee. It distills county intelligence, capital feasibility, and community sentiment into a single decision package.

---

## Recommendation

**Conditional Go — Proceed to Pilot Validation**

Bexar County meets the threshold criteria for MCF pilot deployment. The scale of need, concentration of addressable stress, strength of the local institutional ecosystem, and fit with Spectra's vertically integrated construction model justify moving to structured validation.

**Do not treat this as full project approval.** Proceed to pilot validation only after confirming: (1) local partner alignment, (2) infrastructure readiness, (3) capital stack structure, (4) repayment logic, and (5) absorption assumptions.

## What This Requires to Proceed

### Must Have

1. **Local housing partner** — CDFI, CLT, or nonprofit aligned with Spectra's product and affordability thesis.
2. **Site or pipeline visibility** — at minimum, one infill cluster or subdivision tract with infrastructure capacity.
3. **Capital stack confirmation** — MCF senior construction debt + CDFI gap capital + faith-capital sidecar + minimum 50% leverage.
4. **Absorption gate** — confirmed buyer or renter demand for the target product at the target price point.
5. **Repayment logic** — confirmed exit mechanism (presale, rental income, permanent financing refi, or public/comprehensive purchase).

### Must Confirm Before Committing Capital

- Municipal alignment and entitlement pathway.
- Infrastructure capacity (water, sewer, roads, floodplain).
- Partner legal and governance compatibility.
- AMI targeting and affordability covenant enforceability.
- Construction cost and timeline assumptions.

---

## What Breaks the Deal

1. **No local partner** — Without a credible local institution (CLT, CDFI, or nonprofit), community trust and capital alignment are insufficient.
2. **Infrastructure incapacity** — Sites without utility access or with floodplain/environmental constraints add cost and timeline that break feasibility.
3. **Absorption uncertainty** — Markets with soft demand or high vacancy risk require conservative sizing and extended carry costs.
4. **Capital stack gap** — If CDFI gap capital or faith-capital sidecar does not materialize, the MCF position becomes too senior and risky.
5. **Entitlement delay** — Subdivision or multifamily permitting delays beyond 12 months create carry cost exposure that undermines the pro forma.

---

## What Improves the Deal

1. **CLT partnership** — A San Antonio–area CLT or affordable housing nonprofit as co-developer and land steward improves capital alignment and community trust simultaneously.
2. **City of San Antonio co-investment** —土地利用 or infrastructure co-investment reduces per-unit construction cost and improves the capital stack.
3. **NOAH pipeline** — Existing apartment acquisitions with tenants in place provide faster stabilization and lower lease-up risk than ground-up development.
4. **Council District 8 or Eastside targeting** — Specific geographic targeting reduces community opposition risk and focuses public incentive availability.
5. **Faith-capital sidecar** — A National Christian Foundation or DAF-aligned capital tranche improves the narrative and reduces the cost of gap capital.

## Pilot Recommendation

**Recommended first use case: NOAH acquisition-rehab with anti-eviction stabilization**

Rationale:
- 30 properties drive 12% of all eviction filings — addressable through targeted acquisition.
- Existing tenants in place — no lease-up risk.
- Faster delivery timeline than ground-up construction.
- Lower capital requirement per unit vs. new development.
- Clear community benefit narrative for faith-aligned and CDFI capital.

**Secondary use case (if CLT partner is confirmed): Missing-middle workforce ownership development**

Rationale:
- Addresses the 2-to-20 unit financing gap.
- Produces permanently affordable for-sale product.
- Pairs with CLT resale restrictions.
- Generates homeowner household outcomes, not just renter stabilization.
