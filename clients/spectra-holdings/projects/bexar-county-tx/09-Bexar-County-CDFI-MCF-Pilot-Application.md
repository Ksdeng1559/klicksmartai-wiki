## Project Summary

### The Problem

Bexar County has a structural housing deficit of 115,125 units, an affordability gap that has grown 53% over 10 years, and 65,920 households on affordable housing waitlists — yet market-rate developers serve only the upper income bands, and federal affordable housing programs address only the deepest poverty levels. The workforce band (60–80% AMI) falls through the gap.

Additionally, 30 properties drive 12% of all eviction filings (27,012 total in 2024), creating an identifiable concentration of displacement risk that is addressable through targeted NOAH acquisition and tenant stabilization.

### The Solution

Spectra Holdings proposes a first pilot of 20 NOAH (Naturally Occurring Affordable Housing) units in a targeted San Antonio corridor, producing below-market rental housing for workforce households at 60–80% AMI, with permanent affordability covenants and CDFI monitoring.

### Why This Works

- Existing tenants in place — no lease-up risk.
- Below-market rents attract workforce households immediately.
- Permanent affordability covenants prevent speculative resale or STR conversion.
- Proven homeless response system (unsheltered −25%) provides infrastructure for household stabilization if needed.
- Capital stack (MCF + CDFI + faith sidecar) fits the product and income band.
