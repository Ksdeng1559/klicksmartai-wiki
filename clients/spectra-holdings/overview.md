*Unregistered securities — project-specific equity participation*

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Issuer | Spectra Holdings Group, Inc. |
| Target Return | **3X** on initial investment |
| Timeline | **3–6 months** to activate |
| Distribution | Monthly, based on construction sq. ft. completion |
| Minimum Investment | **$1,000** (1 unit) |
| Maximum Investment | **$300,000** (300 units) without additional approval |
| Investor Eligibility | >5 units = accredited/sophisticated investor required |
| Construction Projection | 6 million square feet annually |
| Reporting | Quarterly "Cost to Build" statements |
| Use of Funds | Land acquisition, construction materials/labor, equipment, infrastructure, R&D |

**Unit mechanics:** Each unit represents proportional equity in specific construction projects. Unit value is tied to cost per square foot of construction. Returns are paid monthly as construction progresses. Securities may only be transferred to spouses or direct descendants with company approval.

**Participant benefits:**
- Discounted pricing on company real estate
- Priority investment opportunities in subsidiaries
- Reduced rates on vacation rentals / hospitality properties
- Product discounts on manufactured building materials
- Leadership consideration (board/senior management)

### 3. Master Credit Facility Fund (MCF)
*For residential construction — not a securities offering per se*

- Revolving 5-year fund structure
- Provides each project with up to **110% of projected per-home funding** prior to vertical construction
- CDFI and institutional investor participation via "Pay as We Sell" program
- Capital held in escrow, drawn only as needed, recycled as closed sales proceeds are received
- Risk controls: vertical construction starts only at ≥60% pre-sales; builds in tranches of 6–20 homes

## Community Development Finance Ecosystem (from notebook sources)

### CDFI Bond Guarantee Program
- Federal 100% guarantee on bonds issued by Qualified Issuers (CDFIs or their designees)
- Minimum bond issue: **$100M** | Max maturity: **30 years**
- Proceeds to Federal Financing Bank (FFB), then relent to CDFIs for large community projects
- Eligible uses: commercial real estate, rental housing, charter schools, healthcare, small business, municipal infrastructure
- **No cost to taxpayers** — generates revenue for Treasury (negative subsidy program)
- FY2025: $100M obligated to 1 CDFI; $215.7M outlayed to 11 CDFIs (2019–2024 rounds)

### Capital Magnet Fund (CMF)
- Funded by GSEs (Fannie Mae + Freddie Mac) — mandatory allocation from mortgage purchases (4.2 bps; 35% to CMF)
- **Not** through congressional appropriations
- Competitive grants to Certified CDFIs and nonprofit housing orgs
- **Leverage requirement: 10-to-1** (must finance $10 for every $1 awarded)
- **70% minimum** for affordable housing; up to 30% for linked economic development
- Must commit funds within 2 years; expend within 3 years; complete within 5 years
- FY2024: $1.1B in demand vs. $246M available — acute affordable housing finance gap
- All-time: $610M committed to active projects generating $26.5B in total project costs (leveraging $18.8B private capital)

### CDFI Certification Requirements
- Be a legal entity
- Primary mission: community development
- Be a financing entity
- Provide development services
- Principally serve eligible target markets
- Be accountable to those markets
- Not a government entity or government-controlled

### LIHTC (Low-Income Housing Tax Credit)
- Federal tax credit program for affordable rental housing development
- Referenced in notebook as complementary financing layer alongside CDFI capital

### Individual Development Accounts (IDAs)
- Asset-building tool for lower-income consumers
- Matched savings accounts with proceeds targeted to education, homeownership, or small business
