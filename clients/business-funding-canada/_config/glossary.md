# Glossary

> Add terms as they appear, with sources. Cite the regulator or lender URL where applicable.

## A
- **A/R financing** — Accounts Receivable financing. Lender advances against outstanding invoices. See Accord's `/services/accounts-receivable-financing/`.
- **ABL** — Asset-Based Lending. Lending secured by a company's assets (A/R, inventory, equipment). See Accord's `/services/asset-based-lending/`.
- **Accord Financial** — Public Canadian SMB lender. TSX: ACD. **Full competitor profile:** `research/competitors/accord-financial/2026-10-02/`. **Summary:** `drafts/competitors/accord-financial-summary-2026-10-02.md`.
- **AccordExpress** — Accord's online, lower-ticket factoring product. Self-serve variant.
- **Advance rate** — Percentage of invoice (or asset) value that a lender will fund. Common ABL advance rates: 70–85% on eligible A/R.

## B
- **BCFSA** — BC Financial Services Authority. Provincial regulator for **mortgage brokers** in BC. Most alternative SMB lending is NOT regulated by BCFSA. https://www.bcfsa.ca/

## C
- **CASL** — Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation. Applies to marketing email + SMS to any Canadian business. Express consent required. https://www.fightspam.gc.ca/
- **Channel financing** — White-label / B2B financing where one lender finances another lender's or platform's customers. See Accord's `/expertise/lenders/`.

## D
- **DIP financing** — Debtor-in-Possession financing. Used in restructuring / turnaround. See `/solutions_restructuring-and-turnaround_.md`.

## E
- **Equipment financing** — Loan or lease secured by equipment being purchased. Common in trucking, manufacturing, construction.

## F
- **Factoring** — Selling A/R (invoices) to a factor at a discount. Recourse (borrower liable if customer doesn't) vs non-recourse (factor absorbs the risk).
- **Floorplan financing** — Inventory financing common in auto, RV, equipment dealers. See Accord's `/services/retail-inventory-financing/`.

## H
- **Hit rate** — Internal metric: % of qualified leads that fund. Useful for channel economics.

## I
- **Inventory financing** — Loan secured by inventory. Retail + wholesale. See Accord's `/services/retail-inventory-financing/`.

## L
- **Lender anchor** — A direct / contracted lender who funds deals sourced by the brokerage. BFC's channel model.

## M
- **MCA** — Merchant Cash Advance. US-style product; **not common in Canada** — avoid using this term on Canadian copy.
- **MCF** — Micro Community Funding (used in adjacent Spectra / Kulshan CLT context, not BFC). Different domain.

## P
- **PIPEDA** — Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act. Federal privacy law. SMB-owner PII is protected at the application stage.
- **Platform financing** — Embedded lending where a SaaS / marketplace offers financing to its users at point of need. Adjacent to channel financing.

## R
- **RECA** — Real Estate Council of Alberta. Mortgage broker regulator in AB.
- **Restructuring** — Work-out facility for distressed businesses. See `/solutions_restructuring-and-turnaround_.md`.
- **Recourse vs non-recourse factoring** — Recourse: borrower liable if customer doesn't pay. Non-recourse: factor absorbs credit risk. Pricing differs.

## S
- **SLA** — Service Level Agreement. Time-to-fund benchmarks.
- **Source-of-truth gate** — KlickSmartAI canonical acceptance /
- **Sub-line** — 75% on capital. If a research artifact needs a real-world example.

## T
- **TSX: ACD** — Accord Financial Corp. Toronto Stock Exchange ticker. Public competitor.
