# Voice

> Stub. Populated after Dennis provides tone direction. Default: clear, evidence-led, no fluff. Match Accord's neutral-expert tone but lean harder into transparency (Accord hides rates — we publish indicative ranges).

## Audience
- **Primary:** Canadian SMB owners (1–50 employees, $500K–$10M revenue) considering growth capital, restructuring, equipment purchase, factoring, or ABL.
- **Secondary:** Accountants + bookkeepers who advise SMBs on financing.
- **Tertiary:** Brokers / independent agents looking for a partner channel.

## Tone (initial)
- Direct, plain business English.
- Lead with the borrower's outcome ("get funded in 14 days", "advance 80% of invoice value within 24h") — not with the product name.
- Transparent: publish rate ranges, advance rates, ticket sizes, qualification criteria where competitors (Accord) hide them.
- No high-pressure language. No "act now" on financial products.

## Do
- Use Canadian spelling.
- Cite the regulator (BCFSA / RECA / FSRA / OACIQ) where relevant. Note that **most alternative lending is unregulated** in Canada — this is itself a differentiator.
- Publish example deals ("$250K ABL at 70% advance, monthly reporting") when the math is illustrative.

## Don't
- Don't quote a specific rate as "today's" without date stamp + lender source.
- Don't promise guaranteed approval.
- Don't use US terminology (MCA, merchant cash advance, SBA).
- Don't borrow Accord's "40+ years of experience" framing as a competitor — they're the incumbent on that claim. Own the opposite: "modern, transparent, fast."
