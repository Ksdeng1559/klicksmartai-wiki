Capital Solutions  
Tailored to  
Your Industry
===============================================

Every industry faces its own set of challenges, and every business has its own unique path to success. With over 40 years of experience, Accord knows what it takes to navigate to a competitive advantage; to not only survive, but ultimately to thrive.

#### Your industry is diverse, but your business is particular

We have over 40 years of experience providing financial solutions that have worked for many companies in your industry, the solution we can provide for you would be unique to your specific needs and challenges. Accord is committed to understanding your business on a granular level in order to deploy a strategic financial solution.

So, whether you’re struggling to make payroll, on the verge of acquiring your biggest competitor or somewhere in between, you can rely on Accord’s fast and flexible financing services to solve your unique business needs.

Unlike banks with tighter loan regulations, Accord is an independent finance company that recognizes the value in the services you provide. We offer the flexibility to provide financial solutions that help you control your operations and achieve your business’ full potential.

As a retailer, you need to have what your customers want, at a competitive price and timely to their purchasing decision. Whether your sales are sluggish and seasonal or you want to gain market share, Accord offers asset-based financing solutions to sustain your retail business and help you become a market leader.

[Learn More](https://accordfinancial.com/expertise/retail/)

Increasing complexities inherent to manufacturing can affect your business operations. Accord’s fast and flexible financing solutions are tailored around the unique needs and challenges facing by your business that can help you manage economic instability and fluctuations in supply and demand.
