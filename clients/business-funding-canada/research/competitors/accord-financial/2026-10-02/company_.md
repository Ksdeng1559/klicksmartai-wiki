Reinventing the Middle-Market Supply Chain of Capital
=====================================================

Our mission is to simplify access to capital so businesses can thrive. By eliminating points of friction, we create systems that seamlessly integrate our clients and financial partners. The result: moving from origination to funding at light speed.

#### Financing Solutions That Meet You Where You Are

Accord Financial is one of Canada’s leading independent finance companies, providing distinctive working capital solutions to small and medium-sized businesses nationwide.

With offices in Montreal, Toronto, and Vancouver, we offer a broad range of financing programs from $5,000 to $30,000,000. Our experienced team, backed by decades of expertise, proudly supports some of Canada’s most dynamic industries with confidence.
