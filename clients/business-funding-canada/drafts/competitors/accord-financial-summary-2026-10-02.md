### 2.3 Ticket & structure (from copy)

- **Min/Max:** $50K to $3MM (homepage hero).

- **Express variant:** **AccordExpress Factoring** is the online / lower-ticket factoring product (`accordexpress-factoring.md`). This is the wedge competitor for SMBs who don't want a full ABL relationship.

| **No pricing / rate ranges published.** Accord says "$50K to $3MM" but no rate bands. | Hard to comparison-shop. Business Funding Canada has the same gap — but a competitor who publishes *indicative ranges with date stamps* wins on transparency. |
| **No calculator.** No payment calculator, no ABL advance-rate calculator. | Easy win — both Accord and the mortgage-by-denniseng site lack one. |

| **No blog / content hub surfaced.** IA has `/latest/` (news only) but no editorial content. | Accord does not own long-tail informational keywords. Open lane. |

| **AccordExpress is the only self-serve surface.** Everything else routes to "apply now" / "contact a specialist." | Self-serve qualification is the wedge. |

## 11. Wedge hypotheses for Business Funding Canada

Based on the gaps above, ranked by ease × SEO value:

1. **Self-serve qualification + advance-rate calculator** — Accord's biggest UX gap.
2. **Case studies by industry** — 9 industry pages × 3 case studies = 27 SEO + trust assets.
3. **Editorial content / blog** — Accord doesn't blog. Own long-tail informational keywords.
4. **Province-specific landing pages** — BC-only / AB-only / ON-only versions of the 5 solution pages = 15 hyper-local landing pages.
5. **Transparent rate ranges with date stamps** — even indicative bands beat Accord's silence.
6. **Lead magnets** — qualification worksheet, "is your business ready for ABL" PDF, factoring-101 guide.
7. **Founder / broker bios** — trust gap.
