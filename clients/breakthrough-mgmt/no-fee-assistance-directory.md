---

## 1. Purpose of This Directory

This directory supports the grant-led GTM strategy by identifying organizations that can provide consultation, advisory support, technical assistance, grant navigation, business planning, procurement guidance, and partner introductions at no fee or low cost.

The objective is to reduce grant-search risk before paying consultants or committing to full applications.

Breakthrough should use this directory to answer:

1. Which funding programs are realistic?
2. Who can help validate eligibility?
3. Which partner structure is best?
4. Should Breakthrough apply directly, partner, subcontract, or act as an operator?
5. What evidence is missing before applying?
6. Which grants are worth pursuing first?

## 4. Priority Outreach Sequence

### Step 1 — APEX Accelerator

**Purpose:** Determine whether Breakthrough should pursue grant funding, government contracting, subcontracting, or public-private partnership structures.

**Suggested outreach message:**

> We are evaluating federal, state, county, and Tribal funding pathways for a circular carbon infrastructure project involving methane reduction, anaerobic digestion, e-waste recovery, urban mining, AI-enabled MRV, and county-level infrastructure. Can your team help us determine whether this is best positioned as a grant, procurement, subcontracting, or public-private partnership opportunity?

**Questions to ask:**

- Should Breakthrough apply directly or partner with a public-sector applicant?
- Are there relevant federal or state contracting opportunities?
- Does this fit better as grant funding, procurement, or technical assistance?
- What registrations are required?
- What partner structure would be strongest?

---

### Step 2 — SBDC

**Purpose:** Strengthen the business model, commercialization plan, and capital-readiness narrative.

**Suggested outreach message:**

> We are preparing a non-dilutive funding strategy for an environmental infrastructure and AI-MRV platform focused on methane reduction, circular economy, e-waste recovery, and carbon asset generation. Can an advisor help review our business model, financial assumptions, commercialization plan, and grant-readiness materials?

**Questions to ask:**

- Is the business model clear enough for SBIR and infrastructure grants?
- What financial projections should be prepared?
- What commercialization evidence is needed?
- What local or state funding sources should be considered?
- Can SBDC help review pitch materials?

---

### Step 3 — SBIR / STTR Program Offices

**Purpose:** Validate whether the AI-MRV and feedstock intelligence platform fits EPA, DOE, NSF, or USDA SBIR/STTR programs.

**Suggested outreach message:**

> We are developing an AI-enabled MRV and feedstock intelligence platform for methane reduction, anaerobic digestion, e-waste recovery, urban mining, and carbon asset generation. Would this type of project potentially fit any current or upcoming SBIR/STTR topics, and what technical framing would make it most responsive?

**Questions to ask:**

- Is this concept responsive to current or upcoming topics?
- Should the project be framed as AI-MRV, methane reduction, recycling, critical materials, or environmental data infrastructure?
- Is a research institution partner required or helpful?
- What TRL is appropriate?
- What would reviewers expect in Phase I?

---

### Step 4 — County / Port / Economic Development

**Purpose:** Validate public benefit, site logic, partner structure, and potential letters of support.

**Suggested outreach message:**

> We are exploring a circular economy and methane reduction platform that could help public-sector partners reduce waste, recover materials, lower emissions, create jobs, and attract non-dilutive climate and infrastructure funding. We would like to understand whether this aligns with county, port, or regional economic development priorities.

**Questions to ask:**

- Is methane reduction or waste diversion a local priority?
- Are there existing plans or reports we should align with?
- Is there interest in a feasibility study?
- Could the county or port provide a letter of support?
- Which grants or infrastructure programs are already on the radar?

---

### Step 5 — University / Lab Partner

**Purpose:** Create technical credibility and validation support for SBIR/STTR and infrastructure grants.

**Suggested outreach message:**

> We are seeking a technical validation partner for an AI-enabled MRV and circular infrastructure platform focused on methane reduction, anaerobic digestion, e-waste recovery, urban mining, and carbon asset generation. We are looking for support around feedstock modeling, emissions calculations, lifecycle analysis, and methodology validation.

**Questions to ask:**

- Can the university support methane or feedstock modeling?
- Is there interest in STTR collaboration?
- Can researchers support lifecycle analysis or MRV validation?
- What IP or partnership rules would apply?
- Could a letter of support be provided?

## 5. Contact Preparation Checklist

- Pilot options

## 6. Recommended One-Page Summary Structure

Use this structure for initial advisor outreach:

1. **Project name:** Breakthrough Circular Carbon Infrastructure Platform
2. **Problem:** Communities produce methane-emitting waste and recoverable materials but lack infrastructure, MRV, funding pathways, and buyer access.
3. **Solution:** AI-enabled MRV + circular infrastructure packaging for methane reduction, AD, e-waste, urban mining, and carbon asset generation.
4. **Initial geography:** Whatcom County / Ferndale, WA as exploratory anchor center.
5. **Target funding:** SBIR/STTR, EPA, DOE, EDA, USDA, state, county, Tribal, circular economy, critical minerals.
6. **Current need:** Eligibility validation, partner structure, funding pathway selection, and technical assistance.
7. **Ask:** 30-minute advisory consultation or referral.
