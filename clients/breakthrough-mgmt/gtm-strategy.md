# Breakthrough Management — Grant-Focused GTM Strategy

**Project:** Breakthrough Circular Carbon Infrastructure Platform  
**Client:** Breakthrough Management  
**Prepared by:** KlickSmartAI  
**Date:** May 21, 2026  
**Status:** GTM Strategy Draft  
**Purpose:** Define a go-to-market strategy for securing grant funding, public-private partnerships, pilot partners, technical validation partners, and future corporate offtake pathways.

---

## 1. GTM Executive Summary

Breakthrough Management should not go to market as a carbon-credit broker or speculative carbon asset company.

The stronger GTM position is:

> Breakthrough helps counties, Tribal communities, rural regions, waste operators, recyclers, and industrial corridors convert waste liabilities into verified environmental assets through methane reduction, anaerobic digestion, urban mining, e-waste recovery, AI-enabled MRV, and circular economy infrastructure.

The initial GTM should be **grant-led**, not sales-led.

The objective is to use grant funding, no-fee technical assistance, public-sector relationships, and pilot partnerships to validate the platform before pursuing larger infrastructure capital and corporate offtake.

---

## 2. Strategic GTM Thesis

Breakthrough's early market is not a traditional commercial buyer market. The early market is a **grant-funded ecosystem** made up of public agencies, county stakeholders, technical partners, environmental infrastructure operators, and corporate sustainability buyers.

The GTM must therefore sell the project through four lenses:

1. **Public problem** — methane emissions, organic waste, e-waste, critical material leakage, landfill burden, and circular economy gaps.
2. **Fundable solution** — AI-enabled MRV, methane reduction, anaerobic digestion, urban mining, and infrastructure feasibility.
3. **Partnership structure** — county, Tribal, recycler, university/lab, environmental engineering, and corporate offtake partners.
4. **Commercialization pathway** — MRV software, carbon asset processing, recovered materials, infrastructure operations, and buyer offtake.

## 4. Beachhead GTM Positioning

The first GTM wedge should be:

> AI-enabled MRV and feedstock intelligence for methane reduction and urban mining pilots.

Why this is the best beachhead:

- Lower capital intensity than full infrastructure.
- Strong fit for SBIR/STTR.
- Supports methane, e-waste, MSR, and C&D use cases.
- Helps create evidence required for larger grants.
- Gives Breakthrough a technology identity beyond project development.
- Creates a repeatable data product for counties and infrastructure partners.

## 6. GTM Funnel

### Stage 1 — Intelligence and Positioning

**Goal:** Build credibility and a grant-ready narrative.

**Actions:**

- Publish internal PDD.
- Build grant eligibility matrix.
- Build no-fee assistance directory.
- Build program-specific concept papers.
- Create 2-page executive brief.

**Conversion event:** Advisor, agency, or partner agrees the project is fundable.

---

### Stage 2 — Partner Discovery

**Goal:** Identify the first anchor partners.

**Actions:**

- Contact APEX and SBDC.
- Speak with EPA/DOE/NSF SBIR contacts.
- Approach county / port / economic development stakeholders.
- Approach recyclers / waste operators.
- Identify university/lab validation partner.

**Conversion event:** Letter of support, MOU discussion, or technical validation interest.

---

### Stage 3 — Pilot Packaging

**Goal:** Convert the concept into a fundable pilot.

**Actions:**

- Select one beachhead pilot.
- Build project scope.
- Build budget.
- Confirm applicant/subrecipient structure.
- Draft concept paper.
- Secure support letters.

**Conversion event:** Grant application, LOI, or pilot proposal submitted.

---

### Stage 4 — Funding Capture

**Goal:** Secure non-dilutive funding.

**Actions:**

- Submit SBIR / grant application.
- Submit county/state feasibility proposal.
- Explore matching funds.
- Continue corporate buyer discovery.
- Prepare backup grants.

**Conversion event:** Award, finalist status, invited full proposal, or funded feasibility study.

---

### Stage 5 — Demonstration and Replication

**Goal:** Use the pilot to unlock larger grants and infrastructure capital.

**Actions:**

- Produce measurable pilot data.
- Publish non-confidential results.
- Build replication model.
- Approach additional counties and Tribal partners.
- Develop corporate offtake pipeline.
- Build infrastructure capital package.

**Conversion event:** Second site, larger grant, corporate offtake, or anchor center funding.

## 11. Sales Motion vs Grant Motion

Breakthrough should separate the GTM into two motions.

### Grant Motion

**Goal:** Secure non-dilutive funding and validation.

**Buyer / Audience:** Agencies, counties, public partners, SBIR reviewers, technical advisors.

**Output:** Concept papers, eligibility matrices, partner letters, grant applications, feasibility packages.

### Commercial Motion

**Goal:** Build future revenue pathways.

**Buyer / Audience:** Corporate sustainability teams, carbon buyers, ITAD firms, secondary materials buyers, infrastructure investors.

**Output:** Buyer briefings, offtake discussions, pilot results, recovered materials pipeline, MRV reports.

The grant motion should come first. The commercial motion should run in parallel as market validation.

## 13. Strategic Recommendation

Breakthrough should pursue a **grant-led GTM strategy** for the first 12–24 months.

The primary objective is not immediate commercial sales. The objective is to create enough technical evidence, partner support, and non-dilutive funding to de-risk a larger infrastructure platform.

The recommended GTM sequence is:

```text
No-fee assistance
    ↓
Grant eligibility validation
    ↓
Technical concept paper
    ↓
Partner letters / MOUs
    ↓
Pilot grant application
    ↓
MRV / methane / urban mining pilot
    ↓
County anchor center feasibility
    ↓
Infrastructure funding + corporate offtake
```

This approach gives Breakthrough the strongest chance of securing non-dilutive capital while building a credible path toward infrastructure-scale deployment.
