## Core Dynamic

AI infrastructure is creating a "super-grade" e-waste stream that is exponentially richer than traditional consumer electronics — fundamentally altering the economics of gold recycling.

## 1. Creation of "Super-Grade" Feedstock

**Yield comparison:**
- Consumer PC boards: ~40-100 g Au/ton
- AI/Telecom server boards: 200-500+ g Au/ton
- High-end CPUs and RAM: >1,000 g Au/ton

**Why AI hardware is richer:**
- Thicker gold plating on connectors/pins for thermal reliability and corrosion prevention under extreme processing loads
- Multi-layered motherboards with buried gold traces

**The "Sweet Spot":** 1 ton of server-grade circuit boards = 40 to 800× more gold than 1 ton of mined ore.

## 2. Accelerated Obsolescence and Volume

- Traditional server lifecycle: 3-5 years
- AI cluster lifecycle: **12-18 months** (NVIDIA H100 → Blackwell architecture upgrades)
- Generative AI hardware: **1.2 to 5.0M metric tons** cumulative e-waste by 2030
- **Batch availability:** Data centers decommission tens of thousands of identical, high-grade units simultaneously — uniform, high-yield stream vs. scattered consumer waste

## 3. High-Value Component Targets

- **GPUs** — heavily gold-plated fingers and connectors; recovering from memory modules is highly efficient (minimal interfering materials)
- **RAM (DDR5/HBM)** — high-speed memory modules with gold-plated connectors
- **Backplanes** — large, multi-layered server motherboards with significant copper and gold traces buried in layers

## 4. Economic and Strategic Drivers

- **TAM:** ~$10.22B annually (US+Canada high-grade PCB gold recovery)
- **"Green Gold" demand:** Tech hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google) committed to recycled materials; actively seeking "verified recycled gold" to offset carbon footprints; creates closed-loop demand
- **ITAD opportunity:** Data centers prioritize security over scrap value; recyclers can acquire high-quality scrap via service contracts

## 5. Key Insight

AI hardware decommissioned within 45 days fetches 35-50% of original purchase value. Beyond that window, rapid depreciation creates stranded liability. This 45-day urgency drives ITAD adoption and creates a forced-choice for organizations: implement aggressive ITAD or accept massive asset loss.
