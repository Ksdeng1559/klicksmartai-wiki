## 5. ITAD+ Operational Framework

Seven core steps for compliant ITAD (2026 standards):
1. **Assess** — Evaluate infrastructure and data sensitivity vs. CISO requirements
2. **Select** — Partners with R2v3 and e-Stewards certifications
3. **Standardize** — Consistent workflows for data destruction (NIST SP 800-88)
4. **Track** — Portal-based chain-of-custody tracking per asset
5. **Verify** — Serialized certificates of destruction and recycling
6. **Measure** — Quantify value recovery (targeting 35-50% of purchase value)
7. **Scale** — Extend standardized processes globally

**Case study:** Sims Lifecycle Services "RackRenew" — remanufactures hyperscale hardware for second-life use (startups, research orgs).

## 6. Carbon Inset Framework

**Carbon Insets vs. Offsets:**
- Offsets: emission reductions outside a company's value chain (e.g., reforestation)
- Insets: reductions within a company's own Scope 3 supply chain — directly address corporate climate targets

**The I-TEC™ and I-MAT™ frameworks** (Bloom ESG):
- I-TEC™: Recognizes climate benefits of IT asset reuse and recovery
- I-MAT™: Recognizes climate benefits of materials recycling and recovery
- Both independently verified against ISO standards and carbon market best practices

**Government incentives:**
- US DPA: funding for strategic material recovery
- Canada CTM-ITC: **30% refundable credit** for polymetallic extraction equipment CapEx
