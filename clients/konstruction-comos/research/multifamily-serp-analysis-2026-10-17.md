## The Paradox: Near-Zero Search Volume, But the SERP is Crowded

DataForSEO returned volume data for **only 2 of 42 multifamily seeds** (both at 10/mo, CPC $0). The other 40 had no measurable data at all.

**But the SERP tells a different story.** Across 5 SERP queries × 10 results = 50 organic positions, **12 (24%) are real lenders** — double the commercial-construction keyword SERP (which was 10%).

### Why the gap?
- Multifamily developers are a small, sophisticated, repeat-borrower pool. They have broker relationships. They don't Google to find a lender.
- When they DO search, they're researching **specific terms** (CMHC ACLP, PBR, specific brokers) that the broad seeds don't capture.
- The lack of Google Ads bidding (CPC $0) confirms this is a relationship/word-of-mouth channel, not a paid acquisition channel.

## Blue Ocean: `vancouver purpose built rental loan`

This is the **most striking finding.** Zero commercial lenders in the top 10 — only news (Daily Hive, Global News, Greenstreet), Reddit, and Facebook. The reason: the federal government announced **$15B in PBR financing** in 2025, and all the SEO juice got captured by news coverage of that announcement.

**A KommercialConnect landing page targeting `vancouver purpose built rental loan` + `vancouver purpose built rental financing` could rank top-3 within 6 months.** The searcher intent is highly commercial (developers + investors looking for PBR capital), and no one is currently serving it.
