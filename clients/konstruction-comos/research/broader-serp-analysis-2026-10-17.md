## Strategic Findings for Project Context

### 1. The Broker-Phrase Gap (NEW BLUE OCEAN)
**`commercial mortgage broker` (590/mo, $12 CPC, 5 brokers in top 10, 1 lender)** — and **`commercial mortgage broker vancouver` (20/mo, $10 CPC, 4 lenders, 6 other)** — both have ZERO lenders, ZERO BC credit unions, ZERO government at #1-3 organic. The SERP is dominated by individual brokers (capitalwest, impactcommercial, ribhurampersad) who are easy to outrank with structured content + AI Overview citation.

**The opportunity:** build `/commercial-mortgage-broker-vancouver/` (the 20/mo blue ocean) + `/commercial-mortgage-broker/` (the 590/mo high-value). Both are 0-BC-competitor at top of SERP.

### 2. Vancity is the ONLY BC Brand Across All 10 Broader Queries
Vancity appears in `commercial mortgage rates` (#6) and `commercial mortgage canada` (#2). No other BC credit union, broker, or lender has meaningful commercial mortgage SEO. **KommercialConnect has the field wide open** for any BC-focused commercial mortgage content.

### 3. The #1 national authority is `lendcity.ca` (9 of 100 slots) — beatable with BC-specificity
LendCity has 9 top-10 rankings across 10 queries. It owns CMHC ACLP guides + commercial mortgage guides. **But LendCity is national, not BC.** A BC-specific page that links to LendCity's national guides + adds local case studies + adds BC regulatory context (BC Mortgage Services Act) can outrank LendCity for BC queries.

### 4. CMHC mli select is all-govt (CMHC owns 3 of top 10)
`cmhc mli select` is the only query where govt dominates (3 of top 10). Not a commercial target — informational only. **Skip.**

### 5. `private lender` is the lowest-quality SERP (8 "other" in top 10)
1 lender, 0 broker, 0 govt, 1 news, 8 "other" — content quality is low across the board. AI Overview is at #6. **Beat this SERP with a structured `/private-lenders-canada/` page that cites Reddit + adds sourcing + adds a lender table.** 1300/mo, $14.80 CPC = highest commercial value in the broader set.

### 6. `commercial mortgage canada` is BC-friendly (Vancity #2, only 1 BC brand)
Vancity owns #2 for the "commercial mortgage canada" phrase. Beat Vancity on BC-specificity by adding developer case studies, BC Mortgage Services Act context, and broker relationships.

## Broader Strategic Recalibration

When the project was named "KommercialConnect" with construction-only focus, the SERP landscape was:
- Construction loans: 10-30% lender competition, 60-80% govt/edu + broker content
- PBR: blue ocean

The broader **commercial mortgage** landscape is harder but higher-value:
- Lender-dominated (60-100% of top 10 are lenders)
- AI Overview pushes organic to #2
- BC is structurally underserved (only Vancity shows up)
- Highest-value terms: `private lender` (1300/mo $14.80), `commercial mortgage` (880/mo $11.53), `commercial mortgage broker` (590/mo $12)

**The right strategic play for "lead generation for commercial mortgage financing opportunities" is NOT to compete with national lenders — it's to:**
1. Own BC-specificity (Vancity is the only competitor, easy to outrank on long-tail)
2. Build commercial-broker-focused content (5 of 10 SERPs for `commercial mortgage` are brokers, beatable)
3. Build private-lender + bridge + mezzanine content (high-CPC, low-competition-quality)
4. Use the PBR + multifamily + construction blue oceans (already identified) as the entry funnel
