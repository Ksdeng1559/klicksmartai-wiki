## What the Winning Page Needs

To rank #1, the landing page must:

1. **Be AI-Overview-citable** — direct answer at the top: "What is a private lender in BC? A private lender in BC is a non-bank individual or company that provides mortgages secured by real estate. BC private lenders typically charge 8-15% interest, approve in 5-14 days, and accept non-conventional applications that banks decline."
2. **Cover the buyer journey**:
   - What is a private lender in BC? (definition)
   - When to use a private lender (vs. CMHC, vs. bank, vs. mortgage broker)
   - BC private lender rates (2026 data)
   - BC private lender directory (curated)
   - How to qualify
   - How to apply
   - Risks and rewards
3. **Beat the existing BC content** — most current BC private lender pages are dated 2023-2025, no 2026 rates, no comprehensive directory, no BC Mortgage Services Act context
4. **Cite sources** — government sources, BCFSA, CMHC for contrast, BC Mortgage Services Act for compliance
5. **Include real BC case studies** — 3-5 anonymized deals (development, bridge, refinance) showing private lender in action
6. **Capture the lead** — apply form, broker call, rate sheet download
