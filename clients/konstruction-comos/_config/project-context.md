## 2. Goal

**Generate commercial mortgage financing opportunities for the BC market.**

The project exists to:
1. **Drive qualified inbound traffic** to a BC commercial mortgage lead-gen funnel
2. **Convert organic search interest** into commercial mortgage financing opportunities (loan applications, broker relationships, developer introductions)
3. **Build an authoritative content moat** in BC commercial mortgage + construction financing where no other BC player has meaningful SEO presence

The product KonstructionComOS provides the platform that captures the opportunity. KommercialConnect is the **content/SEO surface** that drives traffic to the platform.

## 4. The 3 Commercial Mortgage Realities (from organic research)

### Reality 1: BC is structurally underserved
- Only **Vancity** (1 BC credit union) appears in the broader commercial mortgage SERPs
- Only **Amansadfinancial** (1 BC broker) appears in commercial mortgage SERPs
- No BC lender has meaningful commercial mortgage SEO presence
- **Opportunity:** Own BC-specificity for any commercial mortgage term

### Reality 2: AI Overview is the new #1
- **9 of 10 broader commercial mortgage queries** return an AI Overview at #1
- The real #1 organic position is now rank #2
- Being cited in the AI Overview > ranking #1 organic
- **Implication:** Content must be AI-Overview-citable (direct definitions, tables, factual, scannable)

### Reality 3: Lender competition is real but beatable
- 60-100% of top 10 for commercial mortgage terms are lenders
- BUT: most lender content is generic, dated, not BC-specific
- BC-specific + 2026 + case studies + AI-citable = beatable
- The brokers (themortgageadvisors, capitalwest, impactcommercial) are the softest competition

## 6. The Lead-Gen Funnel

Each piece of content drives traffic to one of three lead-gen paths:

### Path A: Direct loan application
User reads: `/apartment-construction-loan-program-bc/`
Action: Apply for a commercial mortgage
Outcome: Loan application → underwriting → funded loan

### Path B: Broker partnership
User reads: `/commercial-mortgage-broker-vancouver/`
Action: Become a KommercialConnect broker partner
Outcome: Broker brings deals, earns referral fee (per PRD §15 — model TBD)

### Path C: Developer / GC subscription
User reads: `/construction-financing-vancouver/`
Action: Subscribe to KommercialConnect for project alerts + lead-gen
Outcome: SaaS subscription to KonstructionComOS platform

**All three paths feed into KonstructionComOS** — the construction intelligence + capital origination platform. KommercialConnect is the SEO/lead-gen surface, KonstructionComOS is the platform.

## 7. Compliance & Non-Goals

From the canonical PRD + AGENTS.md:

### Hard compliance gates
- **BC Mortgage Services Act** (in force 2026-10-13) — all BC lending activity
- **CASL** — no contact eligibility marketing, suppression handling, unsubscribe honored
- **PIPEDA** — applicable provincial privacy controls
- **Canadian securities / mortgage regulations** — consult counsel for regulated workflows

### Non-goals (do NOT do)
- ❌ Automated credit approval
- ❌ Funding guarantee
- ❌ Scraping behind access controls
- ❌ Non-consensual mass marketing
- ❌ Outbound to scraped public contacts without lawful authorization
- ❌ Source licensing violations (for paid municipal/provincial data)
- ❌ Fabricating project data, contact data, or company data

### Disclosure requirements
- All scraped public data: source URL, publisher, observation timestamp, license, verification status, confidence
- AI-generated content must be labeled
- Demo/illustrative projects must be clearly labeled
- Source provenance must be auditable
