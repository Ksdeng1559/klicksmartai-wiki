Definition

What is the Apartment Construction Loan Program (ACLP)?

The Apartment Construction Loan Program (ACLP) is a CMHC-backed construction financing program for 5+ unit residential buildings. The program was originally launched as a $4B federal initiative in 2024 to accelerate the construction of rental supply, then expanded. In 2026, ACLP funds projects through approved lenders (First National, MCAP, Merix, Lend City, others) with CMHC mortgage insurance backing the loan.

ACLP is distinct from CMHC MLI Select, but similar: both are insured by CMHC and offer low rates. ACLP focuses on construction financing (1-3 year term) for any 5+ unit residential project, while MLI Select also covers permanent financing and requires affordability/accessibility/energy criteria.

Process

How to apply for ACLP in BC (4 steps)

The ACLP application process takes 4-8 months from initial inquiry to first draw. Here's the typical BC flow.

1

Pre-qualification (1-2 weeks)

Submit project to ACLP-approved lender. Lender pre-qualifies against ACLP criteria: units, affordability, energy, developer track record. Get indicative term sheet.

2

Full application (4-8 weeks)

Submit full underwriting package: building permits, fixed-price GC contract, cost-to-complete analysis, appraisal of completed value, developer financials, project proforma.

3

CMHC insurance review (8-12 weeks)

Lender submits to CMHC for mortgage insurance approval. CMHC reviews project, environmental, construction docs. Issues commitment.

4

Closing + first draw (4-8 weeks)

Legal documents prepared and signed. Loan closed. First draw typically 4-8 weeks after closing, subject to permits and construction start.

📋

Documentation required

Project proforma, building permits, fixed-price GC contract, architectural drawings, environmental reports, appraisal, developer track record, project schedule.

💰

ACLP cost structure

CMHC insurance premium: 1.75-4.5% of loan (one-time, financed into loan). Lender fee: 1% of loan. Appraisal, legal, environmental: $30K-$80K total.

FAQ

Common questions about ACLP in BC

What is the Apartment Construction Loan Program (ACLP)?

ACLP is a CMHC-backed construction financing program for 5+ unit residential buildings. The program was launched in 2024 as a $4B federal initiative to accelerate rental construction, expanded in 2025-2026. ACLP offers up to 100% financing, 50-year amort, and prime + 1-2.5% rates for projects that meet affordability or energy criteria.

How is ACLP different from CMHC MLI Select?

Both are CMHC-insured and offer low rates. ACLP focuses on construction financing (1-3 year term) for any 5+ unit residential project with a BC affordability or energy angle. MLI Select covers both construction and permanent financing and requires scoring points on affordability, accessibility, or energy. In practice, ACLP is more accessible to mid-market BC developers.

What is the maximum LTV for ACLP in BC?

ACLP can fund up to 100% of project cost (land + construction + soft costs) in BC for projects meeting affordability criteria. Most lenders will require 5-10% developer equity for risk, but CMHC insurance covers the lender's risk on the rest.

How long does ACLP take from application to first draw?

ACLP in BC typically takes 4-6 months from application to first draw. Pre-qualification: 1-2 weeks. Full application: 4-8 weeks. CMHC insurance review: 8-12 weeks. Closing + first draw: 4-8 weeks. Most projects can be funded within 6 months if all documentation is ready.

Can first-time developers get ACLP?

Yes, but it's harder. ACLP-approved lenders prefer developers with 3+ years and 2+ completed projects. First-time developers typically need stronger pre-sales, larger equity contribution (15-20%), and a co-developer or guarantor with track record.
