Definition

What is a Vancouver purpose-built rental (PBR) loan?

A Vancouver purpose-built rental (PBR) loan is construction or permanent financing for a 5+ unit residential building built specifically for the rental market (not for-sale condos). Vancouver PBR projects qualify for special treatment under CMHC's MLI Select program because they add to the rental supply, which is a provincial and federal policy priority.

PBR loans typically fund 65-85% of total project cost, with 1-3 year construction terms and takeout at stabilization. Most Vancouver PBR projects built in 2026 use either CMHC MLI Select (cheapest) or a B-lender conventional loan (most flexible).

3 paths

The 3 paths for Vancouver PBR financing

Almost every Vancouver PBR project uses one of these 3 paths. The right choice depends on your project's affordability, energy efficiency, accessibility, and speed requirements.

Path 1: CMHC MLI Select

Best for: Affordable or energy-efficient PBR projects.

Rate: Prime + 1-2.5%

LTV: Up to 85%

Amort: Up to 50 years

Time to first draw: 60-90 days

Pros: Lowest rate, longest amort, 100% rental.

Cons: Must meet 1 of 3 criteria (affordability / accessibility / energy).

Path 2: B-lender conventional

Best for: Market-rate PBR without CMHC qualification.

Rate: Prime + 1.5-3%

LTV: 65-75%

Amort: 25-40 years

Time to first draw: 30-60 days

Pros: No affordability/energy requirement.

Cons: Higher rate than CMHC, lower LTV.

Path 3: Private PBR lender

Best for: Urgent closings, non-stated income, or bridge scenarios.

Rate: 10-14%

LTV: 55-70% of completed

Amort: Interest-only

Time to first draw: 21-42 days

Pros: Fastest, most flexible.

Cons: Highest rate, often used as bridge.

FAQ

Common questions about Vancouver PBR loans

What is the difference between a PBR loan and a condo construction loan?

A PBR (purpose-built rental) loan funds a 5+ unit residential building built for the rental market, with the developer retaining ownership and operating as a landlord. A condo construction loan funds a 5+ unit building where units are sold to individual buyers on completion. PBR loans typically have lower rates and longer amortizations because the lender holds a single asset (the building) rather than individual unit mortgages.

What is CMHC MLI Select?

CMHC MLI Select is a mortgage loan insurance program for 5+ unit residential projects that meet at least 1 of 3 criteria: affordability, accessibility, or energy efficiency. MLI Select offers up to 85% LTV, 50-year amortization, and prime + 1-2.5% rates. The program is designed to incentivize the construction of rental supply, affordable housing, and energy-efficient buildings.

How long does CMHC MLI Select take?

CMHC MLI Select typically takes 4-8 weeks for application review and 60-90 days from approval to first draw. Total time from application to first draw: 4-6 months. The process involves project underwriting, construction monitoring, and ongoing compliance reporting.

What is the minimum project size for Vancouver PBR financing?

Vancouver PBR financing minimums vary by lender: CMHC MLI Select requires 5+ units and typically $5M+ total project cost. B-lender conventional requires 5+ units and typically $3M+. Private PBR lenders can fund as low as $1M for 5+ unit projects.

Can I get CMHC MLI Select for a market-rate PBR?

Yes, if the project meets at least 1 of 3 criteria. Many market-rate Vancouver PBR projects qualify via energy efficiency (Step Code 3-4, which is required by City of Vancouver) or affordability (a portion of units at below-market rents, often required by municipal approvals).
