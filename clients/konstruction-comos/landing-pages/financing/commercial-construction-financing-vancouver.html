Definition

What is commercial construction financing in Vancouver?

Commercial construction financing in Vancouver is a short-to-medium term loan (typically 1-3 years) that funds the build of a commercial property: residential 5+ units, mixed-use, retail, office, industrial, hotel, or institutional. The lender advances funds in stages (draws) as construction progresses, with a draw inspector verifying completed work. Interest is charged only on funds drawn, not the total loan commitment. The loan is repaid at stabilization (takeout) through CMHC, a conventional lender, or sale of the completed project.

Vancouver construction financing is unique in Canada because of high land costs, complex zoning (Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Richmond all differ), seismic requirements, and tight construction labour market. Most Vancouver projects above $5M use a specialist construction lender rather than a generalist B-lender.

3 paths

The 3 paths for Vancouver construction financing

Almost every Vancouver construction project uses one of these 3 paths. Choosing the right one is worth 100-400 basis points in rate — and 4-12 weeks in time to first draw.

Path 1: CMHC MLI Select

Best for: 5+ unit residential with affordability, accessibility, or energy efficiency features.

Rate: Prime + 1-2.5%

LTV: Up to 85%

Time to first draw: 60-90 days

Min project: $5M

Pros: Lowest rate, longest amort (50yr), insured by CMHC.

Cons: Slowest, most admin, project must qualify on at least 1 of 3 criteria.

Path 2: B-lender (conventional)

Best for: Pre-sold residential, pre-leased commercial, or strong developer track record.

Rate: Prime + 1.5-3%

LTV: 65-75%

Time to first draw: 30-60 days

Min project: $3M

Pros: Mid-rate, mid-speed, flexible property types.

Cons: Requires strong pre-sales/pre-leases or developer experience.

Path 3: Private construction lender

Best for: Urgent closings, unconventional projects, non-stated income developers, bridge scenarios.

Rate: 12-15%

LTV: 55-65% of completed

Time to first draw: 21-42 days

Min project: $1M

Pros: Fastest, most flexible, accepts non-conforming deals.

Cons: Highest rate, lower LTV, often used as bridge to takeout.

FAQ

Common questions about Vancouver construction financing

How does commercial construction financing work in Vancouver?

Vancouver commercial construction financing advances funds in stages (draws) as construction progresses. A draw inspector verifies work completed, then the lender releases the next tranche. Interest is charged only on funds drawn, not the total loan. Most Vancouver construction loans are interest-only with the balance due at completion.

What is the typical LTV for Vancouver construction financing?

Vancouver construction financing LTV is typically 55-70% of completed value, depending on lender type. CMHC MLI Select offers up to 85% for affordable residential. Private construction lenders cap at 55-65%. Conventional B-lenders offer 65-75% with strong pre-sales or pre-leasing.

What is the cost of construction financing in Vancouver in 2026?

Vancouver construction financing rates in 2026: CMHC MLI Select prime + 1-2.5% (most affordable), conventional B-lender prime + 1.5-3%, private construction lender 12-15%. Lender fees are typically 1-2% of loan amount plus draw inspection fees ($300-500 per draw).

How long does it take to get construction financing in Vancouver?

Vancouver construction financing typically takes 4-8 weeks from application to first draw. CMHC MLI Select is the slowest (4-8 weeks for approval, 60-90 days to first draw). Private construction lenders are faster (3-6 weeks to first draw). B-lenders fall in the middle (4-6 weeks).

What documents are required for Vancouver construction financing?

Vancouver construction financing typically requires: building permits, fixed-price contract with qualified general contractor, cost-to-complete analysis, construction schedule, architectural drawings, environmental reports (Phase I/II if commercial), appraisal of completed value, presale agreements (if residential), pre-leasing letters (if commercial), and developer track record.
