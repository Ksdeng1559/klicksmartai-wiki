Definition

What does a Vancouver commercial mortgage broker do?

A Vancouver commercial mortgage broker is a BCFSA-registered mortgage professional who arranges commercial mortgages on behalf of borrowers. Unlike a bank mortgage specialist (who only offers that bank's products), a commercial broker works with 30-100+ lenders: Schedule I banks, B-lenders, credit unions, private lenders, CMHC-approved lenders, and institutional funds.

Vancouver commercial brokers handle loans of $500K to $100M+ across property types: multi-family rental, condo construction, mixed-use, retail, office, industrial, hotel, land, and specialty (care facilities, student housing, etc.). Most charge 0.5-2% of loan amount, paid by the borrower at funding.

How to choose

How to choose a Vancouver commercial mortgage broker

5 questions to ask before you sign with a Vancouver commercial mortgage broker.

1. Are you BCFSA-registered?

Mandatory. As of October 13, 2026, all BC mortgage brokers must be registered with the BC Financial Services Authority. Ask for the registration number and verify on BCFSA's public registry.

2. How many BC commercial deals have you funded in the last 24 months?

Track record matters. 5+ is the minimum for "active." 20+ is experienced. 50+ is specialist. Ask for references on deals similar to yours.

3. How many lenders do you have access to?

Minimum 10 for standard deals, 30+ for complex (private + bank + B-lender + institutional). More lenders = better rate, term, and structure competition.

4. What's your fee structure?

Standard is 1-1.5% of loan amount, paid at funding. Watch for upfront application fees over $2,500, success fees over 2%, or fees not credited back if the deal doesn't fund.

5. Can you handle my deal type?

Some brokers specialize (PBR, construction, private, large commercial). Others are generalists. For complex deals, a specialist often closes faster and at better terms.

6. Who owns the relationship after close?

Some brokers disappear after funding. Others stay for renewal, takeout, and future deals. Ask: "Will you be my broker for the next 5 years?"

Are commercial mortgage brokers worth the fee?

Yes, for most commercial deals. A good commercial broker saves 50-200 basis points in rate through lender competition, often paying for the fee 5-10x over the loan term. They also save time (banks decline 1 in 3 commercial applications), improve structure, and provide access to lenders you can't reach direct.

Is commercial mortgage brokering regulated in BC?

Yes. As of October 13, 2026, BC's Mortgage Services Act regulates all mortgage brokerages, sub-brokers, and individual mortgage brokers. All BC commercial mortgage brokers must be BCFSA-registered. Verify any broker's registration on BCFSA's public registry.
