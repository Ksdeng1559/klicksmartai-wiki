FAQ

Common questions from BC commercial brokers

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. There is no application fee, no monthly fee, and no annual fee. You only pay when a KommercialConnect-originated lead funds, and only the agreed split (70-85% to you, 15-30% to us). You keep your existing book of business and existing lender relationships — we don't interfere with that.

How many leads will I get?

Depends on your specialty, BC region, and tier. Standard brokers typically receive 2-4 qualified leads per month. Pro brokers: 4-8. Elite: 8-15. Partner: 15+. Lead volume is matched to your capacity and funded-deal velocity.

What if I don't close a lead?

No problem. You pay nothing on leads that don't fund. The economics work because we share the upside, not the risk. If your funded rate is below 10%, we'll work with you on qualification, deal support, or handoff to a co-broker.

Can I bring my own leads?

Yes. You keep 100% of fees on leads you bring to the deal yourself. The 70-85% split only applies to KommercialConnect-originated leads. This is a separate revenue stream, not a replacement for your existing book.

Do I need to be BCFSA-registered?

Yes. As of October 13, 2026, BC's Mortgage Services Act requires all mortgage brokers in BC to be BCFSA-registered. We verify your registration as part of the application. Sub-brokers working under a sponsoring brokerage are also welcome.

How does the fee split work in practice?

You close a KommercialConnect-originated deal. The borrower pays the standard broker fee (1-1.5% of loan). At funding, KommercialConnect invoices its share (15-30%). You keep the rest. Payout is within 14 days of funding via EFT.

What if I'm already at another brokerage network?

Most BC brokers are. We operate as a sub-brokerage or co-brokerage layer. You keep your existing brokerage, lender access, and book. We layer on top with deal flow and the KommercialConnect brand. No exclusivity required.

What about conflicts of interest?

We don't compete with you for leads — we generate them. We don't broker deals ourselves — we route them to partner brokers. Our only revenue is the platform split on funded deals. Aligned incentives by design.
