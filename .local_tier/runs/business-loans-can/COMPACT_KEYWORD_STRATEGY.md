## Strategic Recommendations

**1. Build the cornerstone first.** `/business-loans-canada` is the SERP hub. Without it, all province variants compete alone. With it, internal-link equity flows down.

**2. Two gap-play articles, both empty SERP in CA:**
   - `/bdc-vs-private-lender` — 2,400+590 = **2,990 monthly searches aggregate** across `bdc loan` + `small business loan bdc` cluster. Entire page 1 for `BDC vs private lender` (gl=ca) is empty — no Canadian content. **This is the single highest-ROI article target.**
   - `/bdc-tariff-loan` — small volume but the only private-lender position available. Trigger article for the next tariff spike.

**3. Skip tariff-loan category as planned.** 21 of 22 tariff seeds zero volume, zero CPC, no bidders. Park in `tariff-wave-playbook/drafts/` with Google Trends trigger.

**4. Differentiate on `unsecured business loan` and `working capital loan`** — both have broker + 1 alt-lender on page 1. Don't expect to overtake swoopfunding organically without 50+ backlinks; instead, target the long tail (`unsecured business loan bad credit`, `unsecured business loan start-up`).

**5. Geographic priority order:** Ontario first (60%+ of head-term volume + 880 dedicated `business loan ontario` searches), Alberta second (manufacturing/energy + 390 dedicated), BC third (port exposure for tariff tail).

**6. PAA-anchor every pillar page.** Top informational questions to embed as FAQ schema: `How to get a small business loan in Canada?`, `What is the easiest business loan to get?`, `How much would a $100,000 business loan cost?`, `Can I get a small business loan in Ontario?`.

**7. Paid channel allocation (Phase 5, $1,500 cap):**
   - Google Ads: $500-600 on commercial head terms + `bdc vs private lender` + `tariff loan` (private-lender angle)
   - Facebook/Instagram: $400-500 on lookalike audiences sourced from BDC-comparison page visitors
   - Bing Ads: $200-300 on `bdc loan` + `small business loan bdc` (lower competition than Google)
   - YouTube/Remarketing: $200-300 on-site retargeting
   - Reserve: $100-200
