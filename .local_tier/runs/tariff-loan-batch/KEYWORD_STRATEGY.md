## Strategic Recommendations

1. **Build the 7-page pillar cluster first** (highest commercial-intent density):  
   - `/business-loans-canada` (head terms, 14k+ vol)  
   - `/unsecured-business-loans` (Rail B core, 4.2k vol)  
   - `/working-capital-loans` (2.7k vol, $42 CPC)  
   - `/business-line-of-credit` (5.3k vol, $37 CPC)  
   - `/short-term-business-loans` (2.1k vol, $76 CPC)  
   - `/bdc-vs-private-lender` (comparison play, 25k vol aggregate)  
   - `/equipment-financing` (8.9k vol, $16 CPC)

2. **The `business loan online instant approval` cluster** (10 vol @ $302 CPC!) is the single highest-CPC commercial-intent term in the dataset. One fast-approval-optimized landing page targeting this query alone justifies the cluster.

3. **Geographic overlay** — every pillar page gets a province-modified variant. ON alone = 60% of head-term volume. Build `/business-loans-ontario`, `/business-loans-alberta`, `/business-loans-british-columbia` as derivative pillars.

4. **Skip tariff-loan category** (0 commercial-intent terms). Keep the single `BDC tariff loan vs private lender` article as a trigger piece for the next spike.

5. **Quick-win low-comp gaps** in commercial-intent set: terms with score ≥ 60 + LOW comp are SERP-claimable in <30 days. The Tier-2 top 7 (working capital lenders, quick business loans, fast business loan) are these.

6. **Lead capture forms** on every pillar — these are 100% commercial-intent terms, average CPC $102 means a single conversion pays for the page cluster multiple times over.
