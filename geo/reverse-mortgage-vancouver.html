What is a Reverse Mortgage for Vancouver Homeowners?

A reverse mortgage allows Vancouver homeowners aged 55+ to convert up to 55% of their home's equity into tax-free cash — without selling, moving, or making monthly mortgage payments. In Vancouver, where the average detached home is worth $1.8M+ and many seniors are house-rich but cash-poor, a reverse mortgage can unlock $500,000 to $1,000,000+ in tax-free liquidity.

The loan is repaid when you sell, move out, or pass away. You retain full ownership and title. Both Canadian reverse mortgage providers — HomeEquity Bank (CHIP) and Equitable Bank — are federally regulated Schedule I banks with a "no negative equity guarantee": your estate will never owe more than the home's fair market value.

Pros and Cons of a Vancouver Reverse Mortgage

✅ Advantages
  • No monthly payments — interest accrues but nothing is due while you live in the home
  • Tax-free cash — proceeds are not taxable income and don't affect OAS/GIS
  • You keep ownership — title remains in your name, you can stay as long as you want
  • Vancouver appreciation works for you — as your home value rises, your remaining equity grows
  • Flexible payout — lump sum, monthly payments, or a combination
  • No negative equity guarantee — your estate never owes more than the home's value
⚠️ Considerations
  • Higher interest rates — 2–3% above conventional mortgage rates
  • Interest compounds — the balance grows over time, reducing equity for heirs
  • Setup costs — appraisal, legal, and closing fees ($1,500–$2,500)
  • Reduced inheritance — the loan must be repaid from the estate
  • Prepayment limits — limited ability to pay down early without penalty
  • Not for short-term — best suited for those planning to stay 5+ years
