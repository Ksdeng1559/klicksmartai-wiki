Burnaby & New Westminster Mortgage Market

Burnaby's real estate market is defined by its four major town centres — Metrotown, Brentwood, Lougheed, and Edmonds — each with distinct housing stock and buyer profiles. The city's central location, SkyTrain connectivity, and ongoing densification make it a magnet for first-time buyers, investors, and downsizers alike.

New Westminster, just across the Fraser River, offers more affordable entry points with strong transit access and a growing stock of purpose-built rental. For investors, both cities present opportunities in pre-sale assignments, rental properties, and multi-unit developments. Dennis understands the lender appetite for each property type and neighbourhood — and can structure your mortgage accordingly.
