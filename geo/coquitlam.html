Coquitlam & Tri-Cities Mortgage Market

Coquitlam is the anchor of the Tri-Cities, with a population exceeding 150,000 and growing rapidly along the Evergreen SkyTrain extension corridor. The area attracts a mix of young families, professionals commuting to Vancouver, self-employed business owners, and investors drawn to relatively more affordable pricing compared to Vancouver proper.

Key mortgage considerations for Tri-City buyers: Burke Mountain new construction often requires progress draw mortgages; Port Moody's condo market demands quick pre-approvals in competitive bidding; and Port Coquitlam's townhouse developments frequently involve purchase-plus-improvement financing. Dennis has placed mortgages in every Tri-City neighbourhood and knows the lender nuances for each.
