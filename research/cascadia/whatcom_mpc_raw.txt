Whatcom Master-Planned Community Investment
We are excited to present to you an extraordinary investment opportunity in the Whatcom Master-
Planned Community, a transformative project that will redefine urban living and economic development
in the Pacific Northwest. This ambitious initiative spans 25–30 years, with a projected investment of $50
billion, and aims to establish a self-sustaining community designed to enhance quality of life while
driving substantial economic growth.
Project Overview
This comprehensive development will occur in phases, accommodating over 20,000+ residents and
serving as a cornerstone of regional revitalization. As a dedicated real estate developer and urban
planner, I am committed to creating a sustainable, inclusive, and thriving urban hub that benefits both
local and regional economies.
Development Scope and Objectives
Our vision encompasses an expansive array of facilities and infrastructure to ensure a vibrant,
connected, and resilient community:
1. Residential Developments
• Diverse housing options, including single-family homes, multifamily apartments, and affordable
housing units, to accommodate a wide range of residents.
2. 2,000-Acre Industrial Park
• A key economic driver, the industrial park will attract global businesses in technology, logistics,
and advanced industries, creating thousands of high-value jobs and establishing the region as a
hub for innovation.
3. Cascadia Rail Integration
• The community will leverage its proximity to the Cascadia Rail corridor, connecting Seattle,
Bellingham, Blaine, and Surrey.
• This high-speed rail network will attract tourism, facilitate cross-border commerce, and make the
Whatcom community a central destination for global travelers and businesses.
4. IT and Financial Hubs
• State-of-the-art zones dedicated to IT and financial services, fostering innovation, attracting
multinational corporations, and creating a robust, future-ready economic ecosystem.
5. Commercial and Retail Spaces
• Retail shopping malls, office buildings, and entertainment districts will create a vibrant business
environment and diverse employment opportunities.

6. Tourism and Hospitality
• A world-class resort and hotel, featuring luxury accommodations, an iconic convention center,
and curated experiences, will make the community a global tourism hub.
• The resort will host international conferences, trade shows, and exclusive events, driving high-
value tourism with extended stays.
7. Recreational and Educational Facilities
• Parks, schools, gyms, a community center, and family-friendly entertainment districts to enhance
livability.
• Green spaces and community gardens to promote environmental sustainability and foster a
strong sense of community.
8. Healthcare and Services
• Daycares, a hospital, and essential services to support the growing population and ensure well-
being for all residents.
Economic and Employment Impact
1. Job Creation
• The industrial park, IT and financial hubs, and tourism infrastructure will generate thousands of
jobs, benefiting sectors like technology, construction, logistics, hospitality, and retail.
2. Stimulating Economic Growth
• Attracting 20,000+ new residents, the project is designed to stimulate local businesses, support
infrastructure development, and drive long-term economic activity.
3. Cascadia Rail Synergy
• High-speed rail connectivity will amplify growth by attracting business travelers, tourists, and
residents, making the community an epicenter of commerce and tourism.
4. Supporting Local Businesses
• Retail and office spaces will foster entrepreneurship and create opportunities for small
businesses to thrive within the community.
