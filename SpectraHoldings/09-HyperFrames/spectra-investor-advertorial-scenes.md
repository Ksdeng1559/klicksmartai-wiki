# Spectra Holdings Investor Advertorial — HyperFrames Scene Architecture

## Scene 01 — National Housing Pressure

### Intent
Establish urgency around housing shortages, infrastructure strain, affordability pressure, and underserved regions.

### Visuals

- County maps
- Aerial housing footage
- Infrastructure imagery
- Headlines
- Workforce displacement visuals
- Disaster recovery footage

### Motion

Slow documentary pacing with subtle overlays.

### Narration Direction

America faces a growing housing shortage while traditional construction systems struggle to keep pace with affordability, infrastructure, and resilience demands.

---

### Visuals

### Motion
