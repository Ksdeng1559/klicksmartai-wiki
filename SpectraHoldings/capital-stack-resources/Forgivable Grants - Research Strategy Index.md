# Forgivable Grants — Research Strategy Index
## Spectra Holdings

This file organizes NotebookLM research prompts for public-benefit funding, non-dilutive capital, municipal partnership support, housing programs, infrastructure programs, workforce development, and community development opportunities.

---

## Strategic Purpose

Forgivable grants and related public-benefit funding programs should be researched as part of Spectra Holdings' non-dilutive capital formation strategy.

These programs may support:

- Housing delivery
- Workforce housing
- Infrastructure improvements
- Site preparation
- Community development
- Municipal partnership initiatives
- Job creation
- Workforce development
- Manufacturing expansion
- Disaster recovery or resilience initiatives
- Energy-efficient construction

## Recommended Usage Workflow

1. Upload program documents into NotebookLM.
2. Identify relevant funding programs.
3. Extract program requirements.
4. Map funding to the Spectra capital stack.
5. Identify gaps.
6. Build public benefit narrative.
7. Prepare evidence checklist.
8. Draft application materials.

## Core NotebookLM Starter Prompt

```text
Act as a public-benefit funding research analyst.

Using only the uploaded sources, identify funding programs that may support Spectra Holdings' housing, infrastructure, workforce housing, manufacturing, economic development, and community revitalization initiatives.

For each program, summarize:

1. Program purpose
2. Eligible applicants
3. Eligible uses
4. Required public benefit
5. Key documentation required
6. Strategic fit for Spectra
7. Recommended next action

Use clear, evidence-based language and identify any missing information that must be confirmed before proceeding.
```
