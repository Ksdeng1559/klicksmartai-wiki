# CMF and CDFI Bond Programs

## Purpose

This document summarizes the core CDFI-related federal financing tools most relevant to Spectra Holdings' affordable housing, workforce housing, disaster-resilient housing, and community redevelopment strategy.

These tools should be treated as catalytic capital instruments, not isolated funding sources.

---

## 1. Capital Magnet Fund (CMF)

The Capital Magnet Fund is a competitive grant program designed to attract private capital into affordable housing and related economic development in low-income and distressed communities.

### Strategic Use Cases for Spectra

CMF can support:

- affordable housing production,
- preservation or rehabilitation,
- revolving loan funds,
- loan loss reserves,
- loan guarantees,
- risk-sharing loans,
- subordinate financing,
- infrastructure connected to affordable housing.

### Why CMF Matters

CMF is powerful because it can be used as catalytic capital. A relatively small grant can help attract a much larger capital stack.

The defining requirement is the 10-to-1 leverage rule:

**Every $1 of CMF award must support at least $10 of eligible project costs.**

### Spectra Application Logic

CMF should be evaluated when a project has:

- clear low-income or workforce housing purpose,
- strong municipal alignment,
- eligible geography,
- defensible affordability strategy,
- measurable community impact,
- credible developer execution capacity,
- and a layered capital stack that can meet the leverage requirement.

## 2. CDFI Bond Guarantee Program

The CDFI Bond Guarantee Program provides long-term, patient capital to CDFIs through federally guaranteed bonds.

### Core Characteristics

- Designed for large-scale community development finance.
- Bonds are federally guaranteed.
- Minimum bond issue size is generally $100 million.
- Potential maturity may extend up to 30 years.
- Funds must be repaid; this is not a grant program.

### Potential Uses

Bond proceeds may support:

- rental housing,
- commercial real estate,
- mixed-use community development,
- healthcare facilities,
- charter schools,
- infrastructure connected to community development,
- loans to secondary borrowers.

### Spectra Relevance

This program may become relevant where Spectra has:

- a large enough pipeline,
- a CDFI partner or qualified issuer relationship,
- a repeatable project deployment system,
- strong collateral or repayment logic,
- and sufficient reporting discipline.

The program is most useful at scale, especially when Spectra has multiple county or multi-state projects that can be aggregated into a larger financing program.

---

## 3. CDFI Program Financial Assistance

CDFI Program Financial Assistance can provide grants, loans, deposits, equity investments, or secondary capital to certified CDFIs.

### Spectra Relevance

Spectra itself may not directly receive this capital unless it qualifies through an eligible entity or partner structure. However, certified CDFI partners may use this funding to support Spectra-aligned projects.

Potential structures:

- CDFI loan to a project SPE.
- CDFI subordinate debt in a housing project.
- CDFI-supported revolving facility.
- CDFI credit enhancement for senior lenders.
- CDFI bridge capital prior to public or private funding drawdown.

## 4. Decision Framework

### INPUT

- Project location.
- Census tract eligibility.
- AMI targets.
- Housing type.
- Capital requirement.
- Target population.
- Municipal support.
- CDFI partner availability.
- Eligible use of funds.

### MODEL

- Base project capital stack.
- CMF-enhanced capital stack.
- Bond program financing scenario.
- CDFI loan/subordinate debt scenario.
- Leverage ratio calculation.
- Funding gap analysis.
- Blended cost of capital.

### VALIDATION

- Confirm CDFI eligibility.
- Confirm eligible use of funds.
- Confirm leverage requirement.
- Confirm affordability requirements.
- Confirm repayment source.
- Confirm reporting and compliance capacity.

### DECISION

Proceed when the program materially improves feasibility, lowers risk, reduces cost of capital, or strengthens community impact credibility.

Do not proceed if the project becomes dependent on uncertain awards, unclear eligibility, or compliance obligations that exceed the project benefit.

### OUTPUT

- CMF / CDFI program memo.
- Capital stack impact analysis.
- Compliance checklist.
- County-level funding map.
- Go / No-Go recommendation.
