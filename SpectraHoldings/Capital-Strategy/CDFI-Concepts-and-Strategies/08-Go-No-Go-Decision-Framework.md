# Go / No-Go Decision Framework

## Purpose

This framework standardizes how Spectra evaluates CDFI-related opportunities, county deployment strategies, municipal partnerships, and capital stack structures.

The objective is to eliminate ad hoc decision-making and ensure every opportunity follows a repeatable underwriting process.

---

# Core Principle

Every opportunity must follow:

**INPUT → MODEL → VALIDATION → DECISION → OUTPUT**

No strategy is complete unless it produces a defensible decision.

---

# 1. INPUT

## Required Inputs

### Project Inputs

- Project location.
- Housing type.
- Number of units.
- Construction cost.
- Timeline.
- Absorption assumptions.
- Presale assumptions.

### Market Inputs

- Housing shortage.
- Wage growth.
- Migration trends.
- Vacancy rates.
- Workforce demand.
- Utility costs.

### Capital Inputs

- Senior debt.
- CDFI participation.
- Grants.
- Tax credits.
- Municipal participation.
- Investor capital.
- MCF deployment.

### Municipal Inputs

- Zoning.
- Infrastructure support.
- Bond programs.
- Incentives.
- PPP interest.

---

# 2. MODEL

## Required Modeling

### Base Case

Standard operating assumptions.

### Downside Case

Stress-test:

- absorption delays,
- cost overruns,
- interest rate increases,
- incentive loss,
- and slower deployment.

### Upside Case

Stress-test:

- faster deployment,
- reduced costs,
- stronger absorption,
- and additional incentives.

---

# 3. VALIDATION

## Required Validation Checks

### Financial Validation

- IRR.
- DSCR.
- Payback period.
- Cash flow timing.
- Capital sufficiency.

### Strategic Validation

- Municipal alignment.
- Housing need.
- Incentive eligibility.
- Workforce demand.
- Economic impact.

### Operational Validation

- Supply chain readiness.
- Construction capacity.
- Escrow controls.
- Reporting systems.
- Presale readiness.

### Legal and Compliance Validation

- Securities compliance.
- Use-of-funds restrictions.
- CDFI eligibility.
- Municipal authority.
- Reporting obligations.

---

# 4. DECISION

## GO Conditions

Proceed when:

- the project is financially viable,
- the capital stack is defensible,
- deployment risk is controlled,
- municipal alignment exists,
- and the project advances long-term strategic positioning.

## NO-GO Conditions

Do not proceed when:

- the project depends on uncertain incentives,
- the capital stack is unstable,
- the county lacks alignment,
- the repayment logic is weak,
- or operational capacity is insufficient.

---

# 5. OUTPUT

## Required Deliverables

- Executive decision brief.
- Project pro forma.
- Capital stack package.
- Sensitivity analysis.
- Municipal briefing.
- Investor presentation.
- Incentive impact memo.
- County intelligence report.

---

# Strategic Objective

The purpose of this framework is to ensure Spectra evolves into:

**A repeatable institutional housing and capital deployment system capable of operating across multiple counties and states with standardized underwriting logic.**
