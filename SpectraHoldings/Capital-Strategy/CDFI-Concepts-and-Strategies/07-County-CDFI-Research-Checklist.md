# County CDFI Research Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist standardizes county-level research for Spectra housing and community development opportunities.

The goal is to determine:

- whether the county is strategically aligned,
- whether CDFI-related capital may apply,
- and whether the county supports scalable housing deployment.

---

## 1. Housing Need Analysis

Research:

- housing shortage,
- workforce housing demand,
- vacancy rates,
- affordability pressure,
- population growth,
- migration trends,
- housing waitlists,
- and renter burden.

Key Questions:

- Is there measurable unmet housing demand?
- Is workforce displacement occurring?
- Is affordability deteriorating?

---

## 2. Economic Analysis

Research:

- major employers,
- wage growth,
- unemployment,
- manufacturing activity,
- logistics corridors,
- economic development plans,
- and municipal growth priorities.

Key Questions:

- Does the county need workforce retention?
- Is there economic expansion pressure?
- Are industrial or logistics opportunities emerging?

---

## 3. Infrastructure and Resilience Analysis

Research:

- disaster exposure,
- insurance instability,
- infrastructure condition,
- flood/fire/tornado risk,
- utility costs,
- and resiliency initiatives.

Key Questions:

- Does the county require resilient infrastructure?
- Are insurance costs becoming a problem?
- Would resilient construction reduce long-term risk?

---

## 4. Municipal Alignment

Research:

- public-private partnership activity,
- redevelopment plans,
- zoning flexibility,
- municipal bond activity,
- incentive programs,
- and housing initiatives.

Key Questions:

- Is the municipality developer-friendly?
- Is there political support for workforce housing?
- Are incentives available?

---

## 5. CDFI and Incentive Mapping

Research:

- CDFIs operating in the region,
- CMF recipients,
- Opportunity Zones,
- NMTC eligibility,
- HUD-related programs,
- workforce housing initiatives,
- and disaster funding programs.

Key Questions:

- Are there active mission-capital participants?
- Is the geography incentive-eligible?
- Are there local or regional CDFI partners?

---

## 6. Strategic Fit Assessment

Evaluate:

- anchor hub potential,
- manufacturing viability,
- logistics viability,
- landowner partnership potential,
- workforce availability,
- and long-term scalability.

---

## 7. Decision Framework

### INPUT

County demographic, economic, housing, infrastructure, and municipal data.

### MODEL

- Feasibility score.
- Demand forecast.
- Incentive impact.
- Capital stack potential.
- Economic impact model.

### VALIDATION

- Verify sources.
- Validate incentives.
- Validate zoning.
- Validate infrastructure assumptions.

### DECISION

Proceed only if the county demonstrates:

- scalable housing demand,
- municipal alignment,
- financing viability,
- and long-term economic relevance.

### OUTPUT

- County intelligence report.
- Executive decision brief.
- Incentive map.
- Housing demand analysis.
- Capital stack recommendations.
