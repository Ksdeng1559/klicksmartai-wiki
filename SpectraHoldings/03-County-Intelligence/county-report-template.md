# County Intelligence Report Template

## Purpose

This template supports investor advertorials, municipal presentations, landowner outreach, and regional housing deployment analysis.

## County Overview

- County name
- State
- Population
- Population growth
- Median household income
- Major industries
- Workforce profile
- Regional economic drivers

## Housing Pressure Indicators

- Housing shortage estimate
- Median home price
- Median rent
- Rent burden percentage
- Vacancy rate
- Workforce housing demand
- Building permit trends
- Housing affordability trends

## Infrastructure and Risk Factors

- Disaster exposure
- FEMA risk categories
- Insurance pressure
- Utility infrastructure strain
- Transportation access
- Water and energy considerations
- Aging infrastructure indicators

## Economic Development Potential

- Manufacturing opportunities
- Logistics opportunities
- Construction labor demand
- Regional tax base growth potential
- Public-private partnership opportunities
- Industrial land availability

## Municipal Alignment

Document:

- County strategic plans
- Housing goals
- Infrastructure priorities
- Economic revitalization initiatives
- Workforce development initiatives
- Zoning and development constraints

## Landowner and Development Considerations

- Available land
- Utility access
- Transportation access
- Development readiness
- Regional growth corridors
- Opportunity zones
