# SBIR and Grants Workflow: Funding Intelligence Operating System

## Purpose

Organize SBIR/STTR opportunities, federal grants, county needs, tribal priorities, and capital-stack planning into one repeatable workflow.

The goal is not to chase individual grants. The goal is to build a reusable intelligence process that converts community problems into fundable projects, grant applications, procurement opportunities, and blended capital stacks.

```text
Community Problem → Technology Fit → Funding Source → Readiness Score → Proposal Package → Capital Stack → Execution
```

## 3. Workflow Stage Details

### Stage 1 — Community / County Intelligence

Inputs:
- County name
- City / region
- Tribal communities
- Housing data
- Energy burden
- Waste burden
- Infrastructure gaps
- Economic development priorities
- Environmental justice indicators

Output:

```text
County Opportunity Profile
```

Example outputs:
- Housing shortage score
- Waste-management pressure
- Energy resilience need
- Tribal alignment potential
- Public agency partner map

---

### Stage 2 — Problem Definition

Convert loose opportunity into a fundable problem statement.

Example:

```text
Whatcom County has overlapping housing, waste-management, energy resilience, and tribal infrastructure needs. A modular housing + waste-to-energy + solar/storage pilot could address multiple federal and local priorities.
```

Deliverable:

```text
Problem Definition Document
```

---

### Stage 3 — Technology Mapping

Map the solution to funding language.

| Technology | Funding Language |
|---|---|
| Waste-to-energy | Waste diversion, renewable energy, circular economy, resilience |

| Modular housing | Affordable housing, disaster resilience, workforce housing |
| AI grant engine | Decision support, public-sector modernization, innovation infrastructure |

### Stage 5 — Eligibility and Fit Review

Questions:

- Does the project need a research partner?
- Is matching funding required?
- Is the technology mature enough?
- Does the project align with agency priorities?


---

### Stage 6 — Readiness Scoring

Suggested model:

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Community need alignment | 15 |
| Funder priority alignment | 15 |
| Technology maturity | 15 |
| Partnership strength | 10 |
| Pilot readiness | 10 |
| Matching funds / leverage | 10 |
| Logic model quality | 10 |
| Regulatory readiness | 5 |
| Post-award execution capability | 10 |

Readiness tiers:

| Score | Tier | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 80–100 | Strong | Build proposal package |
| 65–79 | Moderate | Fill gaps before submission |
| 45–64 | Early | Build evidence and partners |
| 0–44 | Not ready | Reframe or defer |

Deliverable:

```text
Grant Readiness Scorecard
```

---

### Stage 7 — Logic Model Generation

Structure:

```text
Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Long-Term Impact
```

Example:

```text
Inputs:
Technology, capital, county partner, tribal partner, site, research support

Activities:
Pilot installation, monitoring, workforce training, energy conversion, data reporting

Outputs:
Waste diverted, energy generated, homes supported, jobs created

Outcomes:
Lower infrastructure burden, improved resilience, reduced environmental impact

Impact:
Replicable county-scale resilience and housing model
```

Deliverable:

```text
Logic Model
```

## 5. Minimum Viable Workflow

```text
Input: County + Technology + Partner + Objective

Step 1: Generate county problem profile
Step 2: Search SBIR and grant sources
Step 3: Match agencies and programs
Step 4: Score readiness
Step 5: Generate logic model
Step 6: Build capital stack
Step 7: Produce proposal outline
Step 8: Save to wiki / database
```

Example input:

```text
County: Whatcom County
Technology: Waste-to-energy + solar/storage + modular housing
Partners: Spectra, Tiyo Energy, tribal partners, university partner
Objective: Build a county-scale resilience and community development pilot
```

Example output:

```text
Funding path:
SBIR Phase I
+ DOE community energy
+ EPA environmental justice
+ USDA rural development
+ County infrastructure support
+ CDFI / impact capital
```

## 6. AI Agent Roles

### Research Agent
Finds opportunities from SBIR, Grants.gov, SAM.gov, agencies, and state/local sources.

### Eligibility Agent
Extracts requirements, applicant type, deadlines, and restrictions.

### Readiness Agent
Scores fit and identifies missing requirements.

### Logic Model Agent
Creates inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impact model.

### Capital Stack Agent
Maps grants, debt, impact capital, and private capital.

### Proposal Agent
Creates executive summary, narrative, budget narrative, and checklist.

### Knowledge Graph Agent
Stores entities and relationships for future reuse.

---

## 7. Operating Principle

Do not chase grants. Build a funding intelligence workflow.

```text
Problems ↔ Technologies ↔ Communities ↔ Agencies ↔ Partners ↔ Capital Sources ↔ Proposals ↔ Outcomes
```

This turns SBIR and grants into part of a larger venture studio and capital formation system.
