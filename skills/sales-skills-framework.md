# Sales Skills Framework

21 AI skill prompts covering the full B2B sales motion — from prospecting infrastructure through deal execution and pipeline management. All skills reference `sales-context.md` (built with the `sales-context` skill) for company-specific tailoring.

## Foundation First: sales-context

Before using any other sales skill, build `.agents/sales-context.md`. This is the single source of truth for ICP, value proposition, sales motion, and proof points. The setup conversation flows through 5 rounds:

1. Company & Market — what you sell, stage, team size, target customer
2. ICP & Value Prop — trigger events, core promise, differentiators, before/after outcomes
3. Sales Motion — channels, deal size, cycle length, stages, buying committee
4. Proof & Challenges — case studies, metrics, tools used
5. Anti-ICP & Boundaries — who you don't sell to, hard disqualifiers

**Red flags in your context**: ICP defined too broadly, no trigger events identified, vague differentiators (features not outcomes), no proof points, contradictions between stated ICP and actual deal economics.

## Key Frameworks Embedded in the Skills

### SPIN Selling (discovery-call)
Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-payoff. Talk less than 30% of the time. Dig into business consequences, not feature gaps.

### PACT Qualification (replacing BANT)
Pain, Authority, Consequence, Timeline — score each 1–3. Total 8+ = qualified lead.

### ACRC Objection Handling
Acknowledge → Clarify → Respond → Confirm. First stated objection often masks the real blocker.

### Pipeline Scoring (pipeline-review)
Score 1–10 across: pain ID, authority mapping, timeline confirmation, champion engagement, next-step clarity, competitive knowledge. Above 40 = accelerate. Below 20 = disqualify.

## Key Rules

- **Discovery**: "Discovery is where deals are won or lost — not in the demo." Talk < 30% of the time.
- **Pipeline**: "A deal without a next step is not a deal." Every opportunity needs a calendared action.
- **Cold email**: Follow-up IS the campaign. Most replies come from touches 2–5.
- **Objections**: When 60%+ of prospects raise the same objection, it's a positioning/pricing/product problem — not a handling problem.
- **Pipeline health**: Need 3–4x quota in pipeline coverage. Below this = prospecting problem, not deal optimization problem.

## Performance Benchmarks (cold email)
- 40–60% open rates
- 3–8% reply rates
- 0.5–1.5% meeting rates
Low opens = deliverability issue. Low replies with good opens = copy or targeting problem.
