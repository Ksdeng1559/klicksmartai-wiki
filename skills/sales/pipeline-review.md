# Pipeline Review — VP of Sales Framework

## Core Philosophy

"A deal without a next step is not a deal." Time kills deals. Be brutally honest about pipeline health. Most teams inflate numbers with zombie deals that should be killed.

## Deal Scoring (1–50 scale)

Score each deal 1–10 across six dimensions:

| Dimension | What it measures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Pain quantification | Is the pain specific and in dollars? |
| Authority mapping | Do you have access to the economic buyer? |
| Timeline confirmation | Is there a real deadline or urgency driver? |
| Champion engagement | Is your champion actively selling internally? |
| Next step clarity | Is there a specific calendared action? |
| Competitive awareness | Do you know who else is in the deal? |

- **Above 40**: Accelerate — this deal deserves time and resources
- **20–40**: Develop — has potential, needs work on weak dimensions
- **Below 20**: Disqualify — honest conversation with yourself or the rep

## Automatic Risk Flags

Any deal with these characteristics gets flagged immediately:
- No meaningful contact in 21+ days
- Close date slipped 3+ times
- Same stage for 2x the average cycle duration
- Single-threaded (one contact only) above $25K ACV
- Champion gone dark

## Pipeline Coverage Rule

Need **3–4x quota** in pipeline coverage to hit targets. Below this threshold = prospecting problem, not deal optimization problem.

## Weekly Review Structure

- 70% managing existing deals (scoring, action items, zombie cleanup)
- 30% pipeline creation discipline (prospecting health check)

## Quarterly Purge

Every quarter, force a "resurrect, re-stage, or kill" decision on every deal. Typically removes 15–25% of padded pipeline. This is healthy — a clean pipeline forecasts better than an inflated one.

## CRM Hygiene (non-negotiable fields)

- Next step with date
- Close date
- Amount
- Stage
- Decision-maker contacts
- Last activity timestamp

Missing any of these = the deal doesn't count in forecast.
