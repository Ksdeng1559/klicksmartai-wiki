# LinkedIn Outreach — B2B Prospecting Framework

## Core Principle

"Connection request is not a pitch." LinkedIn is a relationship-building channel. Engagement precedes sales asks. Your profile is your landing page — optimize it first.

## Profile Essentials

- **Headline**: What you do + who you help + outcome (not your job title)
- **About section**: Problem you solve, who you serve, proof points, CTA
- **Featured content**: Best post, case study, or resource

## Performance Benchmarks

| Metric | Baseline | Strong |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Connection acceptance | 20–30% | 40–50% |
| First DM reply rate | 5–10% | 15%+ |
| InMail response | 10–15% | 20%+ |

## Weekly Connection Strategy (~100 requests/week)

- 60% targeted prospects (ICP-matched)
- 25% content engagement targets (people engaging with relevant content)
- 15% inbound interactions (people who viewed your profile, engaged with your posts)

Spread across 15–20 daily sends. Never blast 100 in one day.

## DM Sequencing (Value-First)

1. **Connect** (no note or short context-specific note, no pitch)
2. **Engage** (comment on their content, share relevant resource)
3. **Bridge** (reference a signal: job change, post, company news)
4. **Ask** (meeting request after demonstrated interest)

Never pitch before touch 3. Not ever.

## Comment Framework (within 1 hour of publication)

- Add data or a stat that extends their point
- Respectfully disagree with evidence
- Share a brief personal story that reinforces the theme
- Ask a follow-up question that adds value for other readers

## Critical Warnings

- **No automation for messaging** — triggers account restrictions
- **Spread outreach** — LinkedIn throttles accounts with spiky activity
- **No copy-paste identical messages** — suppression risk
- **Sales Navigator** — worth it for serious outbound ($99/mo). Gives 50 InMail credits, advanced filtering, lead lists, account mapping.

## The Patience Rule

LinkedIn compounds slowly. 3 months of consistent engagement > 3 weeks of volume. Read the signals — someone who views your profile after engaging with your content is much warmer than a cold connection.
