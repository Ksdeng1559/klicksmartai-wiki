# Cold Email — B2B Outbound Framework

## Infrastructure First (Non-Negotiable)

"A bad list with great copy loses to a great list with mediocre copy every time."

Setup checklist:
- [ ] Separate sending domain (never your primary)
- [ ] SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records configured
- [ ] New mailboxes warmed for 2–3 weeks before cold sending
- [ ] 2–3 mailboxes rotating per domain
- [ ] Bounce rate and domain reputation monitored weekly

## Copy Framework

Structure: **Opening line → 1–2 sentences (problem/proof) → single CTA**

Rules:
- Under 100 words for the body
- Plain text only — no HTML, images, or attachments
- No tracking pixels (hurts deliverability)
- One clear ask per email

**Subject lines**: Under 6 words, lowercase often wins, no clickbait.

Examples:
- `quick question about [topic]`
- `[company] + [your value prop]`
- `saw your [specific thing]`

## Personalization That Works

Substantive only: referencing a recent hire, funding, product launch, job change, or specific pain signal. Mentioning their alma mater is not personalization.

## Performance Benchmarks

| Metric | Acceptable | Strong |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Open rate | 25–40% | 40–60% |
| Reply rate | 1–3% | 3–8% |
| Meeting rate | 0.3–0.5% | 0.5–1.5% |
| Bounce rate | < 5% | < 2% |

Low opens = deliverability issue. Low replies with good opens = copy or targeting problem.

## Follow-Up Sequence (5 touches)

| Touch | Day | Angle |
|-------|-----|-------|
| 1 | 0 | Lead with value/pain |
| 2 | 3 | Different angle, shorter |
| 3 | 7 | Social proof / case study |
| 4 | 14 | Challenge / insight |
| 5 | 30 | Breakup email |

"The follow-up IS the campaign." Most replies come from touches 2–5.

## Reply Handling

- **"Not interested"**: Acknowledge + ask if timing is the issue or if there's someone else
- **"Not now"**: Set a reminder, ask what would need to change
- **Pricing request**: Schedule a call instead of quoting in email
- **Referral**: Immediate thank-you + ask for warm intro

## Timing

Send Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am in recipient's timezone.
