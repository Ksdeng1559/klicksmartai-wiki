# Discovery Call — B2B Sales Diagnostic Framework

## Core Philosophy

"Discovery is where deals are won or lost — not in the demo." The moment you start selling on a discovery call, you lose. Diagnose first. Talk less than 30% of the time. Dig into business consequences, not feature gaps.

## Call Structure (30–60 min)

| Phase | Duration | Focus |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Opening & rapport | 4–6 min | Frame as peer evaluation, not vendor pitch |
| Situation assessment | 5–7 min | Understand current state |
| Pain discovery | 8–12 min | Layered questions, real problems |
| Impact quantification | 3–5 min | Numbers on the pain |
| Vision & priority | 3–5 min | Urgency and timeline |
| Next steps | 3–5 min | Specific calendared follow-up |

## SPIN Selling Methodology

- **Situation**: What's your current setup/process for X?
- **Problem**: What's not working? Where do you feel pain?
- **Implication**: What does that cost you / what happens if this continues?
- **Need-payoff**: If you solved this, what would that mean for the business?

## Qualification: PACT (replaces BANT)

Score each 1–3. Total 8+ = qualified opportunity.

| Dimension | What to probe |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Pain** | Specific, quantified problem |
| **Authority** | Access to decision-makers |
| **Consequence** | Real cost of inaction |
| **Timeline** | Urgency drivers |

## Critical Techniques

- **Capture exact language** — Prospect phrasing becomes your sales ammunition later
- **Ask follow-up questions** — Real insights come from curiosity, not scripts
- **Control the process** — Set agenda, manage time, drive next steps
- **Send post-call summary within 2 hours** — Prove you listened, create accountability

## Multi-stakeholder dynamics

Above $25K ACV: identify all stakeholders in the first call. Single-threaded deals fail at this size. Ask: "Who else would be involved in evaluating a decision like this?"

## Post-call

Send a 3-paragraph email within 2 hours:
1. What you heard their situation to be
2. What you agreed to explore / next step
3. Calendar link or specific proposed time

This email becomes the champion's internal sell document.
