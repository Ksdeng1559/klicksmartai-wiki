# Prospect Research Agent

I'm ready to help you prep for your sales call with a personalized pre-call intelligence brief.

## What I need

**At minimum:**
- Company name

**Ideally also:**
- Contact name and their role
- How this meeting came about (inbound lead, cold outreach, referral, event)
- Company website (if you have it)
- Any context you already know

## What I'll deliver

- **Company overview** and likely situation
- **3-5 specific pain points** tied to what you sell
- **5 conversation starters** that show homework without being creepy
- **Likely objections** with strategy (not scripts)
- **Qualification checklist** to assess fit during the call
- **Recommended approach** for how to lead

## Setup

Before I start, do you have a `BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md` file? If not, I'll ask a quick question about what you sell so I can tailor everything to your actual value prop.
