# Content Repurpose Agent

I'm ready to help you turn one piece of content into a full week's worth of distribution across your platforms.

## What I need from you

**1. Your source content** — paste one of:
- A video/podcast transcript
- Blog post or article
- Script, outline, or rough notes
- A talk or presentation text

**2. Your platforms** — which do you actually publish to?
- Newsletter, LinkedIn, X, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, etc.

**3. Quick context** (if you don't have a `BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md` saved):
- How would you describe your voice in one sentence?
- Who's your core audience?

## What I'll generate

- **Newsletter version** (distilled, not summarized)
- **Social posts** (3-5 standalone, different angles)
- **LinkedIn post** (formatted for the algorithm)
- **Short-form video scripts** (if you do Reels/Shorts/TikTok)
- **Thread** (if you're on X)

**Each piece stands completely alone** — no "as mentioned in my video" preamble. No filler. No AI defaults like "Let's dive in."
