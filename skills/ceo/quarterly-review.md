# Quarterly Review Framework

This guide is designed to help founders conduct meaningful 60-90 minute quarterly reviews that move beyond execution tracking to strategic evaluation.

## Core Purpose

The framework distinguishes quarterly reviews from weekly ones: rather than monitoring task completion, these sessions address the fundamental question of "Are we building the right thing?" Weekly reviews keep you on track; quarterly ones ensure you're on the right track.

## Key Process Sections

**Preparation:** Before beginning, locate any existing `BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md` file and prior reviews. This ensures grounding in actual company goals rather than vague reflection.

**The Eight Steps:**
1. Quarter Scorecard — Evaluate 3-5 specific goals against actual outcomes
2. Revenue & Growth Review — Assess trajectory and sustainability
3. What Worked — Identify 2-3 wins and their repeatability
4. What Didn't Work — Name disappointments and extract lessons
5. Strategic Priority Assessment — Categorize initiatives into Keep/Kill/Start buckets
6. Team & Capacity Review — Evaluate people alignment with direction
7. Next Quarter's Big Bets — Define exactly three priority initiatives with success metrics
8. Annual Goal Check-In — Verify alignment with larger trajectory

## Critical Enforcement Rules

The framework emphasizes constraint: "Three bets, not five" prevents priority overload. It requires removal of old priorities before adding new ones. Founders must specify measurable goals—"grow revenue" fails; "$50K MRR by end of Q2" succeeds.

The avoidance question—what should have been done but wasn't—often reveals the quarter's most valuable insight.

## Documentation

Output is saved as `reviews/quarterly-review-[YYYY]-Q[N].md` for longitudinal tracking across quarters.
