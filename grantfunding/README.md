# GrantFunding Intelligence Layer

The GrantFunding Intelligence Layer is the non-dilutive funding intelligence module inside the KlickSmartAI Knowledge Wiki and RIOS architecture.

## Purpose

Connect county needs, tribal priorities, agency programs, company capabilities, and relationship intelligence into one repeatable opportunity system.

```text
Community Need
→ Technology Fit
→ Agency Alignment
→ Funding Path
→ Relationship Map
→ Proposal Strategy
→ Execution
```

## Core Questions

1. What funding opportunity exists?
2. Why does it matter now?
3. Which company or client fits?
4. Which county, tribe, or agency is aligned?
5. Who should be contacted?
6. What proposal narrative should be used?
7. What supporting research is required?
8. What is the next best action?

## RIOS Role

GrantFunding feeds opportunity signals into RIOS. RIOS then maps relationships, warm introduction paths, engagement history, and next-best actions.
