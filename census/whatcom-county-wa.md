## 8. The Affordability Gap

**$200,000+** separates what Whatcom County's workforce can afford from the median home price. 50%+ of renters are cost-burdened. This is structural, not cyclical — population growth + constrained supply + near-zero vacancy.

The "missing middle" — duplexes, triplexes, townhomes, cottage courts, ADUs — is critically undersupplied. Kulshan CLT targets exactly this product type with permanently affordable homeownership.

---

## 9. MCF Intervention Thesis

**Why Whatcom County is the primary MCF pilot target:**

1. **Demand is structural** — 4.2% population growth, near-zero vacancy, $200K+ affordability gap
2. **Crisis is locally generated** — 69% lost housing in-county; intervention dollars stay local
3. **Proven local partner** — Kulshan CLT has LaFreniere Court, Thornton Road, Telegraph Townhomes in pipeline
4. **Faith-aligned impact is measurable** — 972 homeless students; 47% cite affordability as cause
5. **CDFI-aligned** — CLT model is textbook CDFI target: permanently affordable, community-controlled, high-impact
