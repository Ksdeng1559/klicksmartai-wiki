# Enterprise Community Loan Fund (ECLF)

**What it is:** One of the largest nonprofit Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs) in the country. Operates as a subsidiary of Enterprise Community Partners. Provides high-impact loan products to finance affordable housing, healthcare facilities, schools, grocery stores, and community infrastructure in underserved markets.

## CDFI Loan Products

### By Type

| Loan Type | Max Amount | Term | Key Use |
|-----------|-----------|------|---------|
| Predevelopment | $2M | Up to 24 mo | Design, permits, due diligence prior to construction |
| Acquisition | $8M | Up to 24 mo | Land/building acquisition, critical repairs |
| Construction | $10M | Up to 24 mo | New construction or rehabilitation |
| Bridge | $10M | Up to 48 mo | LIHTC equity timing gaps, anticipated funding bridges |
| Mini-Permanent / NMTC Leverage | $8M | 5–7 yrs | Acquisition/refinance of operating properties |
| Permanent | $10M | 7+ yrs | Long-term financing (up to 30-year amortization) |

### By Focus Area

- **Rental Housing** — Preservation and development of affordable multifamily rental
- **For-Sale Housing** — Homeownership for low-income residents
- **Health Care** — FQHCs and community health center facilities
- **Food Security** — Healthy food outlets in food deserts
- **Education** — Charter schools, early childcare facilities
- **Community Services** — Mixed-use, retail, manufacturing, office for job creation
- **Solar Energy** — PV systems for rural and low-income communities
