# CDFI Underwriting Framework
## Risk Mitigation Guide for Affordable Housing Finance

**Purpose:** Transform CDFI loan product structures and underwriting criteria into a practical risk-mitigation framework for evaluating capital sources, structuring deals, and benchmarking developer borrowers in the Spectra Holdings MCF county pipeline.

**Intended users:** Dennis / KlickSmartAI — evaluating CDFI partners and structuring deals with faith-aligned developers.

---

## 1. The Five Underwriting Pillars

CDFIs evaluate borrowers across five risk dimensions. Every loan product sits on all five — weakness in any one pillar increases default probability and triggers covenant enforcement.

| Pillar | What It Measures | Primary Risk |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| **1. Capacity** | Repayment ability from project cash flows | Income/volatility risk |
| **2. Collateral** | Asset quality and liquidation value | Asset concentration risk |
| **3. Capital** | Borrower skin-in-the-game and leverage | Moral hazard / flip risk |
| **4. Compliance** | Eligibility and regulatory standing | Mission drift / default risk |
| **5. Covenants** | Ongoing monitoring and intervention triggers | Early warning / escalation risk |

---

## 2. Pillar 1 — Capacity (Repayment Ability)

**The Rule:** CDFIs underwrite to actual cash flow, not projections. Historical performance dominates; forward projections are discounted 30–50% unless corroborated.

### A. Income Verification Hierarchy

| Priority | Source | Weight |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 1st | Trailing 2–3 years tax returns / audited financials | 60–70% |
| 2nd | Current rent rolls with executed leases | 20–30% |
| 3rd | Pro forma with vacancy stress-test | 10–20% |
| 4th | Sponsor guaranty (personal recourse) | Tiebreaker |

### B. Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

Standard CDFI minimums:

| Loan Type | ECLF Min DSCR | Stress DSCR |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Predevelopment | 1.00x (interest-only) | 0.85x |
| Acquisition | 1.15x | 1.00x |
| Construction | 1.20x | 1.05x |
| Bridge | 1.10x | 1.00x |
| Mini-Permanent | 1.15x | 1.05x |
| Permanent | 1.25x | 1.10x |

**ECLF-specific:** Construction loans capped at 65–75% of stabilized NOI. Bridge loans sized to bridge anticipated equity gap (LIHTC timing, NTMC closing gap).

### C. Capacity Risk Flags

| Flag | Signal | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Negative cash flow 2 of 3 prior years | Operational distress | Require operating reserve escrow |
| Tenant concentration >40% of income | Single-tenant default |Diversification covenant |
| Vacancy >25% at underwriting | Soft market / overimprovement | 12-month seasoned income average |
| Interest-only request with no fallback | Speculative deal | Require bridge commitment letter |
| Construction DSCR <1.20x | Overleveraged | Cut loan-to-cost ratio to 70% |

## 3. Pillar 2 — Collateral (Asset Quality)

**The Rule:** CDFIs underwrite to liquidation value, not appraised value. Stress scenario (30–40% haircut) determines actual advance rate.

### A. Advance Rate by Asset Type

| Asset Type | Standard Advance | ECLF Ceiling | Stress Value |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------|-------------|
| Multifamily (affordable) | 75–80% of LTV | $10M max | 55–60% LTV |
| Commercial (stabilized) | 70–75% of LTV | — | 50–55% LTV |
| Land (entitled) | 50–65% of raw land value | — | 35–45% LTV |
| Health care / community facility | 65–70% of FMV | — | 45–50% LTV |
| Construction (in-progress) | 80% of hard costs | $10M max | 65% |

### B. Collateral Quality Metrics

**Loan-to-Cost Ratio (LTC):**
- New construction: ≤80% LTC
- Substantial rehab: ≤75% LTC
- Predevelopment: ≤70% of land/option value

**Debt Yield:**
- Permanent: ≥10% (NOI / outstanding balance)
- Bridge: ≥8% (bare minimum)

### C. Collateral Risk Flags

| Flag | Signal | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| No independent appraisal | Value unsupported | Mandatory MAI appraisal |
| Environmental contamination | Liability waterfall | Phase I ESA required; Phase II if flagged |
| Title defects / mechanics liens | Clear title not established | Title insurance + escrow holdback |
| Cross-collateralization request | Overleverage | Separate deals / separate collateral pools |
| Personal guarantee absent | Limited sponsor recourse | Required on construction and bridge |

## 4. Pillar 3 — Capital (Borrower Skin-in-the-Game)

**The Rule:** A borrower with capital at risk behaves differently than one with none. CDFIs require minimum equity injection and monitor loan-to-value ratios throughout the loan term.

### A. Minimum Equity Requirements by Loan Stage

| Loan Stage | Min Equity Injection | Notes |
|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| Predevelopment | 15–20% of total project cost | Covers due diligence, permits, option |
| Acquisition | 25–30% of purchase price | Plus closing costs + carrying costs |
| Construction | 25–30% of total development cost (TDC) | Hard costs + soft costs + contingency |
| Bridge | 10–15% of loan amount | LIHTC equity expected to close gap |
| Mini-Permanent | 25–30% | Typical LIHTC gap financing |
| Permanent | 30–35% of TDC | 65–70% max LTV on permanent |

### B. Capital Risk Flags

| Flag | Signal | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Seller financing exceeding 15% of equity | Price inflation risk | Independent cost analysis |
| Gift funds in equity stack | Undue influence | Verify source and maintain minimum |
| Deferred developer fee >30% of equity | Borrower cash-out priority | Require fee paid at construction close |
| Loan-forfeitance right | Borrower exit without penalty | Eliminate flip provisions |
| ECLF sub-debt in stack | Complex capital stack | Confirm ECLF is not displaced by senior |

## 5. Pillar 4 — Compliance (CDFI Eligibility & Mission Alignment)

**The Rule:** Every CDFI-financed transaction must serve a CDFI Certification Target Market. For Spectra's pipeline, this means low-income census tracts, Opportunity Zones, or CDFI Fund-defined investment areas.

### A. Target Market Verification

**Three pathways to eligibility:**

1. **Geographic:** Property located in a low-income community (≤80% AMI) or Qualified Census Tract (QCT)
2. **Population:** Serves low-income individuals (≤80% AMI) — measured by rent affordability
3. **Activity:** Community-serving use (health care, food security, education, affordable housing)

**For Spectra's MCF pipeline:**
- Whatcom County (WA) and Bexar County (TX) census tracts must be verified against HUD AMI data
- Properties in Opportunity Zones receive automatic compliance preference
- Faith-framed developments serving low-income tenants = strongest alignment

### B. CDFI Compliance Risk Flags

| Flag | Signal | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Mixed-income building (>20% market-rate) without carve-out | Target market dilution | Confirm sq ft / unit allocation to income-restricted |
| No DEI/ Section 3 documentation | Federal funding incompatibility | Require Section 3 plan for any public subsidy |
| No affordability covenant on title | 15-year covenant not recorded | Record covenant at closing; monitor annually |
| Out-of-service-area developer | Limited relationship / oversight | Require development services agreement |

## 6. Pillar 5 — Covenants (Ongoing Monitoring & Early Warning)

**The Rule:** Covenants are contractual triggers that give the CDFI intervention rights when borrower financial standing deteriorates. They are the lender's early warning system.

### A. Standard Covenant Package by Loan Type

| Covenant | Trigger | Enforcement |
|---------|---------|------------|
| **Financial reporting** | Annual audited statements + quarterly rent roll | 30-day cure period |
| **DSCR maintenance** | DSCR falls below 1.10x (permanent) | Cash sweep to reserve |
| **Vacancy cap** | Average occupancy <85% for 90 consecutive days | Increase reserve deposit |
| **Debt yield minimum** | NOI / outstanding balance <9% | Acceleration / additional collateral |
| **Change of ownership** | Any transfer without lender consent | Due-on-sale clause |
| **Environmental compliance** | Any contamination discovered | Escrow hold + remediation |
| **Insurance maintenance** | Lapse in property insurance | Lender force-placed insurance + fee |

### B. Watch List Criteria (CDFI Standard)

Loans are placed on the Watch List when:

- 60+ days delinquent
- DSCR falls below 1.05x for two consecutive quarters
- Collateral deterioration >15%
- Borrower enters financial distress (bankruptcy, judgment)
- Material tenant loss (>20% of income)

**Watch List consequences:**
- Increased reporting frequency (monthly vs. quarterly)
- Cash sweep to reduce principal balance
- Requirement to provide additional collateral
- Acceleration if uncured within 90 days

### C. Covenant Risk Flags

| Flag | Signal | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Covenant-lite request | Lender has limited recourse | Standard covenant package required |
| Cross-default provisions absent | Borrower can default to other lenders without triggering ECLF | Cross-default clause required |
| No escrow for real estate taxes | Property tax lien risk | Tax and insurance escrow required |
| No reserves established | No buffer for underperformance | 3–6 months operating reserve at closing |

## 7. Loan Product Risk Map

Each ECLF loan product carries a distinct risk profile. Use this to match the product to the project stage and borrower risk level.

| Product | Primary Risk | Secondary Risk | Key Mitigation |
|---------|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Predevelopment** | Entitlement failure; permitting delays | Borrower walking away | Equity injection; option agreement; contingency |
| **Acquisition** | Overpurchase; market softening | Title defects | Independent appraisal; title insurance; environmental |
| **Construction** | Cost overruns; contractor default | Timeline delays | Cost audit; performance bonds; draw schedule |
| **Bridge** | LIHTC timing gap; equity not closing | Market interest rate risk | commitment letters; DSCR floor; cash sweep |
| **Mini-Permanent** | Refinance risk; NMTC allocation failure | Permanent financing not arranged | Take-out commitment required before bridge close |
| **Permanent** | Refinance risk; NOI underperformance | Asset deterioration | DSCR covenant; reserve requirements; occupancy floor |

## 8. ECLF-Specific Underwriting Benchmarks

Extracted from Enterprise Community Loan Fund loan product terms and CDFI industry standards:

### A. Loan Sizing Grid

| Loan Type | ECLF Maximum | Term Cap | Typical Leverage |
|-----------|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| Predevelopment | $2M | 24 mo | 65% of land/option value |
| Acquisition | $8M | 24 mo | 75% of purchase price |
| Construction | $10M | 24 mo | 80% of hard costs |
| Bridge | $10M | 48 mo | 90% of anticipated equity gap |
| Mini-Permanent | $8M | 7 yrs | 75% of stabilized value |
| Permanent | $10M | 30-yr amort | 65–70% of stabilized value |

### B. ECLF Borrower Eligibility Markers

From CDFI Fund and ECLF documentation, borrower qualification criteria:

- **Experience:** Minimum 2 completed affordable housing developments (or comparable)
- **Financials:** Audited statements for prior 2 years; no bankruptcies in last 5 years
- **Capacity:** DSCR ≥1.15x on a stressed basis (25% vacancy stress)
- **Capital:** Minimum 25% equity in deal (15% for nonprofits with government grants)
- **Compliance:** Entity in good standing; no debarment; CDFI territory eligible

### C. Geographic Investment Signals

ECLF partnership fund presence = confirmed affordable housing activity and CDFI pipeline. Active presence in target counties validates capital stack viability:

| Partnership Fund | Geographic | Implication for Spectra |
|-----------------|-----------|----------------------|
| NYC Acquisition Fund | New York | High-activity CDFI market; reference case for structure |
| Golden State Acquisition Fund (GSAF) | California | Strong affordable housing underwriting norms |
| Bay Area Transit Oriented Fund | SF Bay Area | Transit corridor = transit-oriented development (TOD) standard |
| Energy Independence Fund | National | Solar integration in affordable housing = standard practice |
| Equitable Path Forward Growth Fund | National | Directly relevant to Spectra's developer network in MCF counties |

## 9. Risk Mitigation Checklist

Use this checklist before closing any CDFI-financed deal in the Spectra pipeline.

### Capacity
- [ ] Trailing 2–3 year tax returns / audited financials reviewed
- [ ] Rent roll with executed leases verified
- [ ] DSCR meets ECLF minimum for loan type (see Section 2B)
- [ ] Stress test applied (25% vacancy, 10% expense increase)
- [ ] Sponsor guaranty obtained (construction + bridge minimum)

### Collateral
- [ ] Independent MAI appraisal obtained
- [ ] Phase I ESA completed (Phase II if flagged)
- [ ] Title insurance in place, CDFI as mortgagee
- [ ] LTC within ECLF ceiling for loan type
- [ ] Construction draws scheduled with inspection protocol

### Capital
- [ ] Equity injection confirmed and wired
- [ ] Developer fee structure verified (deferred fee at construction close)
- [ ] Capital stack documented (no seller carry-back >15%)
- [ ] LIHTC/ NMTC commitment letters attached

### Compliance
- [ ] Target market verification (census tract AMI or QCT)
- [ ] Affordability covenant recorded in land records (15-year minimum)
- [ ] Opportunity Zone certification if applicable
- [ ] Section 3 plan in place for any public subsidy

### Covenants
- [ ] Standard covenant package confirmed (Section 6A)
- [ ] Watch list triggers defined and documented
- [ ] Reserve accounts established (operating + replacement reserve)
- [ ] Tax and insurance escrow set up
- [ ] Cross-default provisions confirmed

## 10. Framework Limitations & Notes

1. **ECLF is one CDFI model.** Other CDFIs (Clearinghouse CDFI, Hope Credit Union, Self-Help) have different underwriting tolerances. This framework reflects ECLF-specific terms where available; cross-check against each capital source's published guidelines.

2. **CDFI loans are relationship-driven.** Soft information (sponsor character, community standing, developer reputation) can override hard metrics. The framework captures the hard criteria but cannot fully encode relationship risk.

3. **Faith-framed deals add complexity.** Developments framed as mission-aligned to Christian foundations (Spectra's investor thesis) must balance CDFI compliance with faith-values criteria. Some CDFIs have explicit impact measurement frameworks; others rely on standard financial underwriting only.

4. **Loan performance data is limited.** CDFI loan portfolios have above-normal default risk and lack comparable historical performance data (Congressional Research Service). Loan pricing does not fully reflect embedded risk — this framework does not attempt to price risk but rather to identify and mitigate it structurally.

5. **Layered capital stacks require coordination.** Deals using ECLF + NMTC + LIHTC + private equity need intercreditor agreements. This framework addresses each product individually; deal-level coordination requires separate legal review.
