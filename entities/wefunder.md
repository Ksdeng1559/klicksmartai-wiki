# Wefunder

**Wefunder** is a Public Benefit Corporation and FINRA-regulated crowdfunding platform that allows anyone to invest in startups starting at $100.

## Overview

- **Founded:** 2012
- **CEO:** Nick Tommarello
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, CA (Gun Barrel City, TX for Wefunder Portal LLC)
- **Legal structure:** Public Benefit Corporation
- **Mission:** Fund 20,000 more founders in all 50 states, backed by everyday angels
- **Tagline:** "Fighting to keep America defined by optimism, innovation, and opportunity"

## Products

- **Vault** — launched October 2025, makes it easier for investors to back top startups
- **Community Rounds** — Reg CF raises any amount from anyone
- **VC-Backed Community Rounds** — post-2021 reforms allowed venture-backed startups (Substack, Replit, Mercury) to raise community rounds
- **Syndicates & Funds** — SPV and fund structures for larger rounds

## Charter / Beliefs

I. Helping more founders increases all of our wealth
II. The people are wiser than banks or VCs — "wisdom of the crowd" beats gatekeepers
III. Everyone has the right to invest in what they believe in — laws limiting to wealthy are paternalistic and perpetuate structural racism
IV. Investing in dreams — win or lose — benefits us all
