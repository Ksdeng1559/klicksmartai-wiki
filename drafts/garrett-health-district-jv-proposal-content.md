**Date:** May 30, 2026  
**Parties:** Landowner Partnership (Garrett Street Parcels) & Development Partner

**Location:** 417, 419, [Adjacent Parcel] Garrett Street, Sapperton, New Westminster, BC  

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This proposal outlines a joint venture structure between the Landowner Partnership (owners of three contiguous parcels on Garrett Street) and an experienced development partner to realize **Garrett Health District Residences**, a 6-8 storey purpose-built rental building targeting healthcare workers at the adjacent Royal Columbian Hospital.

**Key Advantages:**
- **Site Control:** Assembled three parcels (~15,000 sqft, ~150 ft frontage)
- **Location:** 550m from Sapperton SkyTrain Station (TOD Tier 3), 400m from RCH
- **Policy Alignment:** BC TOD legislation (3.0 FSR / 8 storeys), healthcare workforce housing priority
- **Demand Anchor:** 2,000+ RCH staff with acute housing need
- **Financing Eligibility:** CMHC MLI Select, BC Housing co-investment, DCL waiver potential
- **Planning Narrative:** Healthcare framing reduces NIMBY risk, accelerates municipal approval

**Development Concept:**
- 6-8 storey purpose-built rental building
- 55-75 units (studios, 1BR, 2BR)
- Gross floor area: 45,000–54,000 sqft (3.0 FSR)
- Target tenant: RCH healthcare workers
- Parking: 22-30 spaces (0.4/unit under TOD reduced requirement)

##   2. JOINT VENTURE STRUCTURE

### 2.1 Partnership Composition

| Party | Contribution | Equity Share | Role |
|-------|-------------|-------------|------|
| **Landowner Partnership** | Contributes assembled land at appraised value (~$5,000,000) | 30% | Land contribution; representation in planning process; partnership oversight |
| **Development Partner** | Contributes all construction capital (~$25,000,000); brings development expertise; manages rezoning, financing, construction, leasing, operations | 70% | Development execution; financial management; project delivery |

### 2.2 Legal Vehicle

**Proposed:** Development Limited Partnership (LP)  
- General Partner: Development Partner (or subsidiary)  
- Limited Partners: Landowner Partnership (collectively)  
- Alternative: Development Agreement with completion guarantees if LP not preferred

### 2.3 Capital Contributions

| Item | Landowner Partnership | Development Partner | Timing |
|------|---------------------|-------------------|---------|
| Land Contribution | ~$5,000,000 (appraised value of assembled parcels) | — | At closing following rezoning approval |
| Equity Capital | — | ~$25,000,000 (construction + soft costs) | Phased per construction draw schedule |
| Total Contribution | $5,000,000 (in-kind) | $25,000,000 (cash) | |
| **Total Project Capital** | **$30,000,000** | | |

### 2.4 Profit Distribution

**Waterfall Structure:**
1. **Return of Capital:** 100% of distributions go to repay each party's contributed capital proportionally
2. **Preferred Return:** 8% annual cumulative preferred return to both parties on contributed capital
3. **Catch-up:** Development Partner receives 100% of distributions until it achieves 15% IRR on its capital
4. **Promote Split:** Thereafter, profits split **70% Development Partner / 30% Landowner Partnership**

5. **Financial Management:** Maintain project accounts, handle distributions, provide quarterly reporting
6. **Exit Strategy:** Execute hold/sell decision per partnership agreement (typical 5-7 year hold)

## 5. KEY AGREEMENT TERMS

### 5.1 Governance

- **Management Committee:** 3 members (2 Development Partner, 1 Landowner Representative)
- **Decision Authority:** Development Partner has day-to-day operational control
- **Consent Rights:** Landowner Partnership has consent rights on:
  - Budget variances >15%
  - Change in project scope or unit count
  - Sale/refinance of the asset
  - Partnership dissolution
- **Reporting:** Quarterly financials, monthly construction updates

### 5.2 Land Contribution Mechanism

1. Independent appraisal at rezoning approval date (MAI appraiser, mutually agreed)
2. Land value contributed as equity to JV entity
3. Transfer via sale at appraised value to JV entity (tax-deferred rollover possible)
4. Title insurance and environmental Phase I completed prior to transfer

### 5.3 Development Partner Guarantees

- **Completion Guarantee:** Development Partner guarantees project completion within budget (+10% contingency)
- **Financing Guarantee:** Development Partner guarantees construction financing at terms no worse than CMHC MLI Select
- **Time Guarantee:** Rezoning application submitted within 90 days of LOI execution

### 5.4 Exit Scenarios

1. **Hold Strategy:** 5-7 year hold period; distributions from net operating income
2. **Sale Strategy:** Asset sale after stabilization (Year 5-7); profit split per waterfall
3. **Refinance Strategy:** Cash-out refinance to return capital; partnership continues
4. **Default/Dissolution:** Trigger events and remedies defined
