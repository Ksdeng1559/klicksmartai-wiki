Problem & Solution

Why intake is broken — and how we fix it

✕ The Problem

  • 01. Generic forms capture bare minimum data, killing qualification accuracy
  • 02. Manual follow-up creates 24–72 hour delays that kill urgency
  • 03. No firm-wide visibility into lead pipeline or intake status
  • 04. Human bias in qualification leads to missed opportunities

✓ The Solution

  • 01. Dynamic AI conversations adapt questions based on role and context
  • 02. Instant qualification the moment a lead submits their info
  • 03. Real-time pipeline dashboard with complete lead visibility
  • 04. Consistent, bias-free scoring based on firm-defined criteria

How It Works

From prospect to qualified in 4 steps

1

Capture

Lead scans QR or taps link. Branded intake page loads instantly on any device.

2

Qualify

AI asks dynamic questions. Branching logic adapts to role and firm type for maximum relevance.

3

Score

Firm-defined criteria applied. Lead receives match score and routing tag automatically.

4

Route

Qualified leads routed to assigned advisor. Notification sent. CRM updated in real-time.

User Flows

Two primary journeys, zero friction

Prospect Intake Flow

For leads entering the funnel
Primary
1

Landing page loads — Branded, mobile-optimized intake form appears. Timer starts (avg. 90s).

2

Role detection — "What best describes you?" initial question branches to CPA, RIA, Attorney, or Exec path.

3

Dynamic Q&A — 5–8 role-specific questions. AI skips irrelevant sections. 73% fewer fields than traditional forms.

4

Instant results — Fit score displayed. "You're a 94% match!" CTA to book call or add to waitlist.

Advisor Review Flow

For your team qualifying leads
Internal
1

Notification lands — Slack/email alert with lead summary, score, and key signals. No app switching required.

2

Quick review — Lead card shows full profile, conversation transcript, and flagged items needing attention.

3

One-click actions — "Accept & Book", "Request More Info", or "Nurture" buttons. CRM updates in real-time.

4

Auto-escalation — Unreviewed leads after 4 hours auto-escalate. No hot lead goes cold.

Requirements

Non-negotiable — all must ship

⚡ Performance

  • Intake form loads in < 1.5s on 4G
  • Qualification completes in < 800ms
  • API response time < 200ms p95
  • 99.9% uptime SLA

🔒 Security

  • SOC 2 Type II certified at launch

  • TLS 1.3 for all connections
  • Configurable data retention per jurisdiction
