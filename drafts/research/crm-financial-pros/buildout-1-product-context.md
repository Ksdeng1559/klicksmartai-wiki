## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Stop re-entering the same client data into 4 different systems.

**What it does:** AI Intake Fast Lane replaces email-chain client onboarding with a structured digital intake flow — one client data entry, auto-populated to every carrier portal and AMS/LOS system. Mortgage brokers and insurance agents go from 5–10 days to under 15 minutes per new client.

**Product category:** Workflow automation / AI-powered data entry for financial services
**Product type:** SaaS (setup fee + monthly subscription)
**Business model:** $1,500–$3,000 setup + $199/user/month

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** After a client says yes, financial advisors spend the next 5–10 days in an email chain collecting documents and re-entering the same client data into carrier portals, LOS systems, and AMS platforms — manually, one field at a time, multiple times per file.

**Why current solutions fall short:**
- AMS platforms (AMS360, Applied Epic, EZLynx) were built to store data — not to collect it from clients or sync it to carrier portals automatically
- LOS platforms (Point, SimpleNexus, Blend) require manual data entry from the advisor, even when the data already exists in email or a prior system
- Email remains the default client communication channel — unstructured, ungoverned, and impossible to auto-populate from
- Intake forms from carriers are separate from the CRM — data collected in one doesn't flow to the other

**What it costs them:**
- Per file: 45–90 minutes of manual re-entry per client (3–5 systems × 15 minutes each)
- Per year: 100+ hours for an advisor doing 2 new clients per week
- At renewal: Data from intake never got into the AMS — renewal review requires reconstructing the file from scratch
- At E&O claim: Incomplete onboarding records = gaps in coverage that the agent can't prove were addressed

**Emotional tension:**
- "I'm a [mortgage broker / insurance agent], not a data entry clerk"
- "The client already gave me this information — why am I typing it again?"
- "I lose more clients in the 5 days it takes to open a file than I lose at the closing table"
- "Every time I re-enter data, I introduce an error that slows down the entire file"

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:** Generic intake form builders (JotForm, Typeform, Google Forms) — fall short because data collected stays in the form; no AMS/LOS/carrier portal integration

**Secondary competitors:**
- Standalone CRM systems with basic intake (HubSpot free, Zoho) — no financial services workflow templates, no carrier portal sync
-VAS/mortgage-specific intake tools (Roota, Altfein) — niche, US-only, no insurance vertical
- In-house IT builds — expensive, maintenance-heavy, not built for independent brokers

**Indirect competitors:**
- Clients using carrier direct portals — advisor loses control of the relationship and client data stays in the carrier's system
- "Just use email" — current default, tolerated because no better option has been presented

**How each falls short for customers:**
- Intake form builders: Nice UX, zero integration. You still manually copy the data into the AMS.
- CRMs: Built for tracking, not collecting. The CRM becomes another place to re-enter data.
- Carrier portals: Client goes direct to the carrier, advisor is cut out of the onboarding relationship.
- In-house builds: $20K–$50K, 6-month timeline, requires ongoing IT support, not built for the advisor's actual workflow.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
1. Financial services-specific intake templates (mortgage + insurance) — not generic forms
2. Auto-population of AMS platforms (AMS360, Applied Epic, EZLynx) and LOS systems (Point, SimpleNexus, Blend) — not just data collection
3. AI-assisted document parsing — driver license, pay stubs, policy docs auto-read and field-populated
4. E&O-safe client interaction log — timestamped proof of what was collected and when

**How we do it differently:** Most intake tools are form builders. We're a data pipeline. The form is just the front door — the value is what happens after: data that flows automatically into every system the advisor actually uses.

**Why that's better:**
- Advisor enters data once, in 15 minutes, at the point of intake
- AMS/LOS updated in real-time — no more "I'll enter it when I get back to the office"
- Client receives a professional intake experience, not a PDF in an email
- Compliance-ready audit trail built automatically

**Why customers choose us over alternatives:**
- "Because I stop being the middleware between my client and my AMS"
- "Because I onboard clients in 15 minutes, not 10 days"
- "Because when E&O calls, I have a complete record of exactly what the client provided and when"

| "My AMS already has an intake form" | Yours collects data. Ours populates your AMS and your carrier portals — automatically. Two completely different things. |
| "My clients won't fill out another form" | They're already filling out carrier-specific forms, one per carrier, with zero guidance. One streamlined form with auto-fill is objectively easier. |
| "We tried something like this before and it didn't stick" | What you're describing is a form builder. We're a data pipeline — it connects to the systems you actually use. |
| "It's one more thing to set up" | $1,500 setup is us doing it for you. You approve the templates, we build and deploy. |
| "My IT team needs to review it" | Independent brokers don't have IT teams. That's why this exists. |

**Anti-persona:**
- Carrier captive agents (data stays in carrier system by design)
- Large enterprise brokerages (50+ reps) with dedicated ops teams who already have custom integrations
- Advisors who are happy re-entering data and view it as "part of the job"
