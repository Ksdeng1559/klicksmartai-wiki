## PPP Model (Public-Private Partnerships)

Spectra partners with municipalities to provide:
- Land or development rights
- Zoning approvals and permitting support
- Access to State and Federal funding (CDBG, HOME, SHIP)
- Community needs assessment and planning input
- Revenue from property sales and rentals
- Increased property tax base
- Job creation and economic development

In return municipalities provide land, zoning support, and funding access.
