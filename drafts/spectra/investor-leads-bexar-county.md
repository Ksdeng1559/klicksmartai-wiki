## Notes

- **TDHCA / TSAHC** should be engaged at the city/county level before approaching investors — having municipal alignment signals project viability
- **LIHTC investors** (Hunt, Advantage Capital) typically require a developed project site + local LIHTC allocation from TDHCA — sequencing matters
- **FHLB Dallas AHP** is a non-recourse grant that stacks on top of any deal — apply early, decision cycle is ~6 months
- **Enterprise FBDI Texas** is a capacity-builder for faith organizations — Spectra could position as their development operating partner rather than approaching them as a capital source directly
