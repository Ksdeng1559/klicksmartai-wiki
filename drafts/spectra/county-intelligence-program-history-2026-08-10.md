## 3. REPORT-BY-REPORT HISTORY

### 3.1 Whatcom County, WA — 2026-05-13 (Primary MCF Pilot)

**Files:**
- `clients/spectra-holdings/deliverables/whatcom-county-wa-county-intelligence-report-2026-05-13.md` (27,269 bytes, 420 lines)
- Project folder: `clients/spectra-holdings/projects/whatcom-county-wa/` (12-doc structure)
- Partner: Kulshan Community Land Trust (`projects/kulshan-clt/`)

**Key metrics:** Pop 236,392 (+4.2% 2020–25); MHI $85,471; median home $585,800–$750,000; **$153K–$365K affordability gap**; 5,000-unit county-acknowledged shortfall; corridor (Blaine/Birch Bay/Semiahmoo) growing 3× county rate; Birch Bay 26.3% STR-driven vacancy.

**Core thesis:** *Bridge the affordability gap.* Workforce earning $85K can finance ~$385–432K — median home is $585–750K. Household must earn 167% of AMI to buy the median home. CLT (Kulshan) + MCF Pay-as-We-Sell + $6.6M county co-investment precedent + HB 1110 mandate (June 2026 deadline) = rescue-the-dream narrative.

**Why NOW (3 windows):** HB 1110 compliance deadline; 7-0 county council political will; sub-2-month inventory seller's market below $750K.

**Status:** Investor-ready. Active primary pilot county. Recommended narrative built for family offices, county officials, landowners.

**Files:**
- `clients/spectra-holdings/deliverables/craig-county-ok-county-intelligence-report-2026-05-20.md` (19,419 bytes, 279 lines)

## 7. LESSONS LEARNED / PATTERN RECOGNITION

1. **Every county reframed the thesis.** Whatcom = bridge gap in hot market; Bexar = targeted intervention in structural deficit; Craig = arrest decline + tribal braiding. The 12-section skeleton is constant; the thesis is never templated.
2. **Bexar started as a blank slate** (zero wiki data on 05-14) — census build-out had to precede the report. Whatcom had rich data; the 6-report batch was pure synthesis, zero new pulls.
3. **Confidence scoring appeared once** (Bexar 82/100) — not standardized across the program. Candidate for retroactive standardization.
4. **Risk registers matured with Bexar** (12 risks, L×I priority, owners, dates) — the Whatcom folder lacks the equivalent formal register.
5. **Net-new research (Craig) exposed the cost of rural data gaps** — 7 explicit gaps logged. Rural counties need a distinct source playbook (USDA, Oklahoma Works, Cherokee Nation Housing Division, OSU rural housing research).
6. **Capital stack research overtook county expansion** after May 21 — recoverable grants, GrantFundingAI, Breakthrough — the pipeline shifted from new-county intelligence to capital formation depth.
7. **Known process pitfalls encoded in the skill:** never skip social intelligence; never run deliverables before census is complete; note data vintage; investor leads before outreach; HITL for all external delivery.
