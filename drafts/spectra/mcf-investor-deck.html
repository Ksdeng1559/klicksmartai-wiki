Financial Mechanics

110% Pre-Funding

Each home fully funded before ground break — includes 10% contingency reserve. Zero schedule disruption.

12% Flat Interest

Interest accrues only on drawn & outstanding capital. No punitive prepayment penalties.

Pay-As-We-Sell

Interest paid directly from home sale proceeds. No quarterly payment burden on the borrower.

Capital Recycling

Sale proceeds → escrow → interest paid → principal returns to revolving pool instantly.

Risk Mitigation: SPE Structure

  • Each county operates its own Special Purpose Entity — legally isolated from all others
  • A challenge in County A cannot compromise collateral in County B or the master facility
  • Vertically integrated subsidiaries control manufacturing, distribution & construction
  • Local General Contractors employed by county via Vertikal Builders Alliance
  • Strict control over timelines, costs, and quality — no reliance on third-party developers
  • ~1,200 homes targeted simultaneously across multiple states
