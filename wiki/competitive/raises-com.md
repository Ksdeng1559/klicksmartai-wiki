## Process (4 steps, "protocol")

1. **Structure** — vehicle (PPM, sub-docs, operating agreement). Reg D 506(b) & 506(c).
2. **Package** — pitch deck, financial model, investment summary, diligence room.
3. **Connect** — warm intros to family offices, HNW, institutional.
4. **Close** — wiring before exclusivity expires.

## Customer segments

- **Aspiring Principal** — doctor/lawyer/exec going deal-ready (sourcing → diligence → structure)
- **Experienced Operator** — friends-and-family track record, scaling to institutional for $10M+ tickets
- **Capital Seeker** — has signed LOI, network tapped, needs fresh debt/equity

## Services (full list)

- Fund structuring (LP/LLC, PPM, sub agreements, 506(b)/(c))
- Investor packaging (decks, summaries, models, projections, data rooms)
- Capital connections (family offices, HNW, institutional equity, acquisition & construction debt, MCA refinance, SBA, seller notes/earnouts, DSCR/portfolio refi)
- Affiliate / white-label program (referral → commission → white-label the platform)
