# projects/ — Validated Source of Truth

**Purpose:** Promoted, HITL-approved deliverables. These are the source-of-truth artifacts for <client_name> work — once a draft passes review, its final source `.md` lands here.

**Rule:** Nothing lands here directly. All content originates in `drafts/` and is promoted here **only** after Dennis approves. See `../CONTEXT.md` Stage 04→05.

## Files (start empty)
_Empty by default. Promote from drafts/ as Dennis approves._

## Promote a draft
1. Copy the approved `.md` from `drafts/` into `projects/`.
2. Remove its row from `drafts/VALIDATION_QUEUE.md`.
3. Produce the client-ready export in `deliverables/`.

## Graduate to subdirectories
When a project accumulates multiple files (cards, briefs, memos, reports), move it into `projects/<slug>/` with its own `README.md`. Do this at a natural review point, not mid-pipeline.
