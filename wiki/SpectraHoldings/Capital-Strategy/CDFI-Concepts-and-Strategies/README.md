# CDFI Concepts and Strategies

## Purpose

This folder is the operating knowledge base for Spectra Holdings' CDFI-related capital strategy.

It organizes concepts, research prompts, capital stack logic, public-sector alignment, CDFI financing tools, municipal bond opportunities, National Christian Foundation / donor-advised fund alignment, and Master Credit Facility integration.

## Strategic Objective

Position CDFI-aligned capital as a catalytic layer inside Spectra's broader housing finance system:

**Mission Capital → Credit Enhancement → Project Financing → Housing Delivery → Capital Recycling → Community Impact**

## Folder Structure

- `01-CDFI-Strategy-Overview.md` — core CDFI strategy and role in Spectra's capital system.
- `02-CMF-and-CDFI-Bond-Programs.md` — Capital Magnet Fund, CDFI Bond Guarantee Program, and related financing tools.
- `03-Master-Credit-Facility-Alignment.md` — how CDFI capital aligns with the MCF and Strategic Capital Network.
- `04-Municipal-Bond-Alignment.md` — county, city, workforce housing, disaster resilience, and public-purpose financing.
- `05-NCF-and-Donor-Advised-Capital.md` — faith-based and donor-advised fund alignment.
- `06-Capital-Stack-Templates.md` — reusable blended capital stack structures.
- `07-County-CDFI-Research-Checklist.md` — research checklist for county-level feasibility.
- `08-Go-No-Go-Decision-Framework.md` — underwriting and decision framework.

## System Logic

Every CDFI-related opportunity should follow Spectra's financial intelligence workflow:

**INPUT → MODEL → VALIDATION → DECISION → OUTPUT**

No CDFI concept should remain theoretical. Each item should connect to one of the following decisions:

- Should Spectra pursue this capital source?
- Does it reduce the cost of capital?
- Does it improve project feasibility?
- Does it improve affordability compliance?
- Does it improve investor/lender risk profile?
- Does it strengthen municipal alignment?
- Does it create a repeatable county-level deployment model?
