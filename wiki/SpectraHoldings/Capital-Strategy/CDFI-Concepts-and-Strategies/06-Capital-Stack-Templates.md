# Capital Stack Templates

## Purpose

This document contains reusable capital stack concepts for Spectra housing and community development projects.

These templates are designed for:

- affordable housing,
- workforce housing,
- public-private partnerships,
- resilient infrastructure,
- and county-level redevelopment initiatives.

---

## Core Capital Stack Philosophy

Every project should optimize:

- cost of capital,
- deployment speed,
- lender confidence,
- community impact,
- and scalability.

The goal is not merely to finance projects.

The goal is to engineer repeatable, defensible, institutional-grade capital structures.

---

## Template 1 — Standard Workforce Housing Stack

### Components

1. Senior construction debt.
2. Master Credit Facility construction deployment.
3. Developer equity.
4. Municipal incentives.
5. Infrastructure participation.

### Best Used For

- market-rate workforce housing,
- suburban redevelopment,
- and municipal workforce shortages.

## Decision Framework

### INPUT

- Project type.
- County profile.
- Affordability requirements.
- Capital requirement.
- Municipal support.
- Investor profile.
- Construction timeline.

### MODEL

- Base case.
- Downside case.
- Upside case.
- Weighted cost of capital.
- DSCR.
- IRR.
- Payback period.

### VALIDATION

- Confirm eligible funding sources.
- Confirm affordability compliance.
- Confirm repayment structure.
- Confirm investor suitability.
- Confirm deployment timing.

### DECISION

Proceed with the stack that maximizes feasibility, scalability, resilience, and community impact while maintaining defensible economics.

### OUTPUT

- Capital stack package.
- Executive summary.
- Sensitivity analysis.
- Investor briefing.
- Municipal presentation.
