## Executive Purpose

This document defines the integrated capital architecture for Spectra Holdings' recoverable grant, CMF, nonprofit, tax credit, investor, and Master Credit Facility strategy.

It connects six operating modules:

1. Detailed capital stack math.
2. Legal document architecture.
3. Compounding reinvestment flywheel.
4. Investor entry points.
5. Compliance calendar.
6. Capital flow architecture.

The core purpose is to create a repeatable system where restricted public-purpose and donor capital can be deployed into affordable housing and community development projects, recovered where permitted, and redeployed into future hubs.

---

## Core Capital Architecture

```text
Federal / Donor / Investor Capital
        ↓
Propel CDC or qualified nonprofit partner
        ↓
Recoverable Grant / Program Capital
        ↓
Project SPV
        ↓
Anchor Hub
        ↓
Housing + Jobs + Operations
        ↓
Revenue / Recovery Trigger
        ↓
Propel Revolving Fund
        ↓
Next Hub
```

The system is designed to move capital through a controlled, auditable, and repeatable pathway.

## Module 1 — Detailed Capital Stack Math

### Capital Stack Objective

The capital stack solves the gap between what senior debt and tax credits can support and what a project actually costs.

### Sample $30M Anchor Hub Stack

| Layer | Amount | Percentage | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Senior Debt / CDFI / CRA Bank | $9,000,000 | 30% | First-lien debt / institutional leverage |
| LIHTC Equity | $11,400,000 | 38% | Tax credit equity |
| NMTC Net Subsidy | $3,600,000 | 12% | Community development gap subsidy |
| OZ Equity | $3,000,000 | 10% | Long-horizon Opportunity Zone equity |
| CMF Recoverable Grant | $2,100,000 | 7% | Soft subordinate mission capital |
| Developer / Sponsor Equity | $900,000 | 3% | Sponsor alignment / skin in the game |
| **Total** | **$30,000,000** | **100%** |  |

### Leverage Verification

```text
CMF Requirement: 10:1
Formula: (Senior Debt + LIHTC + OZ + Developer Equity) / CMF

Calculation:
($9.0M + $11.4M + $3.0M + $0.9M) / $2.1M
= $24.3M / $2.1M
= 11.6x
```

This exceeds the 10:1 leverage target in the illustrative structure.

### Subsidy Coverage Check

```text
Total subsidy = LIHTC + NMTC + OZ + CMF
= 38% + 12% + 10% + 7%
= 67%

Total stack coverage:
67% subsidy + 30% senior debt = 97%

Remaining gap:
3% developer equity
```

### Internal Validation Note

Before external use, validate any hard-cost assumptions. One source assumption references approximately $44.4M in hard construction cost for 200+ units, while the illustrative stack uses a $30M anchor hub. This should be reconciled by defining whether the $30M stack is phase-specific, subsidy-specific, or total development cost for a smaller initial tranche.

## Module 2 — Legal Document Architecture

### Document Hierarchy

```text
CDFI Fund / Public or Donor Capital Source
        ↓
Grant Award Agreement / Donor Agreement
        ↓
Propel CDC or qualified nonprofit awardee
        ↓
Recoverable Grant Agreement
        ↓
Project SPV
        ↓
Construction + Operating Agreements
        ↓
Spectra operating subsidiaries / vendors / project delivery partners
```

### Core Agreements

| Agreement | Standard Meaning | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| CDPA | Community Development Partnership Agreement | Master relationship, governance, public/private or nonprofit partnership terms |
| RGA | Recoverable Grant Agreement | Governs recoverable grant deployment, triggers, repayment, reporting, and remedies |
| UFA | Use of Funds Agreement | Defines eligible uses, restricted accounts, draw controls, and project-level reporting |
| RAA | Restricted Account Agreement | Controls segregated capital accounts |
| Escrow Instructions | Escrow release controls | Governs milestone-based construction and funding draws |
| Reinvestment Policy | Revolving capital controls | Defines how recovered principal must be redeployed |

### Acronym Standardization

To avoid confusion:

| Acronym | Use |
|---|---|
| CDPA | Community Development Partnership Agreement |
| RGA | Recoverable Grant Agreement |
| UFA | Use of Funds Agreement |
| UCA | Unfunded Commitment Authority |

Do not use UFA to mean Unfunded Commitment Authority. Use UCA instead.

### Restricted Accounts

| Account | Purpose | Control Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| CMF Revolving Fund | Holds recovered principal | CDFI Fund / program oversight and reinvestment rules |
| NMTC Program Account | Tracks NMTC allocation and QLICI activity | CDE compliance requirements |
| LIHTC Capital Account | Holds LP equity contributions | Investor / syndicator controls |
| OZ Fund Account | Holds OZ equity contributions | Opportunity Zone compliance rules |
| Project Escrow | Controls construction draws | Senior lender / escrow agent |

## Module 3 — Compounding Reinvestment Flywheel

### Flywheel Logic

```text
CMF / Recoverable Grant Capital
        ↓
Deployed into eligible project SPV
        ↓
Project produces homes, revenue, or refinancing event
        ↓
Recovery trigger activates where applicable
        ↓
Recovered principal returns to nonprofit revolving fund
        ↓
Recovered capital redeployed into next eligible project
```

The objective is not one-time funding. The objective is repeated deployment of the same capital into multiple eligible projects.

### Illustrative 15-Year Reinvestment Progression

| Period | Event | Illustrative Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Year 0 | Initial CMF / recoverable grant award | $6.3M deployed into 3 hubs |
| Years 1–2 | Construction phase | Capital deployed; no recovery yet |
| Years 3–4 | First revenue event | Partial recovery begins |
| Years 4–5 | Second deployment round | Recovered capital plus new sources fund additional hubs |
| Years 6–10 | Additional recoveries and deployments | Revolving fund grows through repeated cycles |
| Years 11–15 | Expansion phase | Capital base supports broader hub deployment |

### Recovery Rate Scenarios

| Scenario | Recovery Range | Strategic Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Full forgiveness | 0% | Mission-first outcome where affordability is preserved and return threshold is not met |
| Partial recovery | 50%–75% | Balanced mission and capital recycling outcome |
| High recovery | 80%–100% | Strong financial outcome or compliance trigger recovery |

## Module 4 — Investor Entry Points

### Foundation / Programmatic Grant

```text
Foundation
      ↓
Grant or recoverable grant
      ↓
Propel CDC / nonprofit partner
      ↓
Project SPV
```

Best for:

- Mission-aligned affordable housing.
- Predevelopment.
- Homebuyer readiness.
- Workforce programs.
- First-loss or soft capital.

Return expectation:

- Usually 0% financial return.
- Recovery, if applicable, often returns to the revolving fund rather than back to the donor.

### Donor-Advised Fund / DAF

```text
DAF sponsor
      ↓
Recommended grant
      ↓
Propel CDC / nonprofit partner
      ↓
Restricted use deployment
```

Best for:

- Faith-based capital.
- Philanthropic housing initiatives.
- Programmatic grants.
- Mission-aligned recoverable grants where permitted.

### Family Office

```text
Family office
      ↓
Equity / debt / tax-credit participation
      ↓
Spectra / Project SPV / QOF / tax-credit structure
```

Best for:

- OZ participation.
- Tax-credit investments.
- Impact-aligned project equity.
- MCF participation.

### Impact Investor

```text
Impact investor
      ↓
Debt or preferred equity
      ↓
Project SPV
      ↓
Affordable housing and community metrics
```

Best for:

- Below-market or market-rate impact capital.
- Patient capital.
- Project-level participation.

### Faith Capital

```text
Faith capital / church endowment / religious network
      ↓
Grant, PRI, low-interest loan, or recoverable grant
      ↓
Propel CDC / nonprofit partner
      ↓
Housing, health, and community development projects
```

Best for:

- Mission-aligned housing.
- Ethical capital deployment.
- No-windfall / no-penalty recoverable grant logic.

### CRA-Motivated Bank

```text
CRA bank
      ↓
Loan / participation / qualified investment
      ↓
Project SPV or nonprofit partner
      ↓
LMI housing / community development outcome
```

Best for:

- Senior debt.
- Construction bridge.
- Acquisition and infrastructure.
- CRA-qualified community development activity.

## Final Positioning Statement

Spectra's recoverable grant capital architecture is designed to convert public-purpose, philanthropic, donor, tax-credit, CRA, CDFI, and impact capital into a controlled deployment system for affordable housing and community development.

The nonprofit layer administers mission capital and compliance. The Project SPV receives and deploys restricted capital. The MCF and Spectra operating platform execute housing delivery. Recoverable grant triggers allow principal to recycle into future projects, creating a compounding community development capital system.
