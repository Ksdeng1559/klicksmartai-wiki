# Client Acquisition Roadmap
## Zero to First 5 Customers

**Source:** Growth Strategy 101 — Sabrina  
**Core premise:** The only validation that matters is five people paying for your solution.

---

## The Survival Mandate

Most startups die before reaching 10 customers. Until you hit 5, you are in the **danger zone**.

Two growth paths — **not mutually exclusive:**

| Path | Cost | Speed |
|------|------|-------|
| **Organic** | Time + sweat equity | Slow (months) |
| **Paid** | Capital to compress time | Immediate |

Iterate through both until you find signal in the noise.

---

## Organic Growth — Sweat Equity

### Direct Outreach

| Method | Primary Benefit | Golden Rule |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| **Warm network** (friends, ex-colleagues) | Leeway to fix kinks, build case studies | Be honest you're early-stage — lowers pressure, builds trust |
| **Cold DMs** (LinkedIn, X, IG) | Exact targeting of decision-makers who feel the pain today | Never just ask to sell — send a sample/before-and-after first |

> **Mentor note:** Skipping the warm network is a mistake. Friends provide "leeway" to build the first testimonial.

### Social / Community Strategies

1. **Niche Facebook/School Groups** — AI Automation, industry-specific groups
   - Copy-paste help docs/templates into the group
   - If it solves a common headache, the group pulls you in naturally
2. **Product Forums** (Airtable, Zapier, Notion) — Bottom-of-Funnel goldmines
   - Search pain-point terms: "How do I post to TikTok via API?"
   - Show your tool solves it in one click
3. **Product Hunt** — Launch every 6 months on major feature updates
   - Not a one-and-done; capture new waves of visitors per launch

### Value-First Content — Hand-Raisers

**Hand-raiser:** Someone who comments or DMs for a resource — highest-quality lead signal.

- Use lead magnets to surface them (e.g. free playbook, database, template)
- Tools like ManyChat automate the response sequence
- 3,000 comments = 3,000 qualified leads ready for a DM

**Viral hooks:** Don't try to be clever — copy hooks that already have 250k+ likes. Data-backed formats outperform originality until you have a tested voice.

## Paid Growth — Capital Compression

> **Warning:** Lost 100K+ on negative ROI ads. Do not start unless you have $500+ to burn on testing.

| Channel | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| **Apollo** (lead lists) | Scraping by specific criteria + automated sequences |
| **Facebook/Instagram ads** | Targeting control — segment by interest (e.g. "people who like Zapier") |
| **Micro-influencer/newsletter sponsorships** | Pre-vetted audiences |

### The 80/20 Upwork Rule
- Use reverse job boards to find people posting about your problem
- AI automates 80% of proposals
- Add 20% human touch (reference their specific niche) to stand out

### AI SEO — Competitor Keywords
- Target "Competitor + Alternative" keywords
- Searchers are bottom-of-funnel buyers ready to switch

### Vibe Coding Micro-Tools
- Build a free simple tool (e.g. LinkedIn Headline Generator)
- Drive traffic, embed main product naturally within

## Sabrina's Success Framework

1. **Identify 2-3 fitting methods** that match your personality
   - Introverts → Cold Email
   - Extroverts → Webinars
2. **Run 30-day experiments** — look for hand-raisers and engagement spikes
3. **Double down on the winner** — kill the rest, focus 100% energy on what shows life

---

## Organic vs Paid Summary

| Dimension | Organic | Paid |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Primary cost | Time + effort | Capital |
| Speed of results | Slow (months) | Immediate (minutes) |
| Targeting precision | Low (algorithm) | High (user-defined) |
| Scalability | Hard to "turn up" | Easy — increase budget |

---

## Lean Growth Mindset

- You will almost never "tap out" a channel — always more groups, keywords, influencers
- **Start free → validate people actually want it → then use capital to scale**
- Move fast, stay pragmatic, don't stop until you find winners
