## The Anti-Cynicism Stance

Wefunder's charter explicitly names the enemy: *caution, cynicism, credentialism*. Dennis has expressed the same orientation — the incumbents who screen out good candidates because they don't fit a mold, the "experts" who gatekeep access to capital and clients.

HUBERT-X is built on this:
- 80% of life insurance applicants eliminated by resume keyword filters before a human reads the application
- Advisors earning $65K+/year in commission income — untapped because no one built the tool to find them
- The solution is not "more credentialism" — it is better signal, faster signal, wider net
