# Delivery — PROFILE

The Delivery department produces the actual work the client pays for: audits, websites, AI Employees.

## Mission

Deliver every engagement on time, within scope, and to a quality that earns referrals.

## Current state

- **Hired but idle.** Two AI employees (`site-auditor`, `proposal-writer`) ready to work, but no signed engagements yet.
- **Site-auditor** produces per-business audit JSONs used internally by Lead Generation today.
- **Proposal-writer** produces per-business audit summaries (drafts) for the future sales cycle.

## What the Delivery department will produce (per engagement)

1. **Audit deliverable** — a comprehensive technical + AI-Search-readiness + UX/conversion audit (PDF or web)
2. **Rebuilt website** — Next.js + Tailwind, schema.org, OpenGraph, structured data
3. **CMS** — for the client to maintain content themselves
4. **AI Employees** — receptionist / intake / booking / review workflows (per PRD Stage 12)
5. **Handoff document** — what we did, what they own, how to measure success

## What the Delivery department does NOT do

- Finding prospects (Lead Generation)
- Outreach / pricing (Sales + CEO)
- Defining the service offer (CEO)
