# Compliance — Veritas Developments

Applies to any Veritas deliverable that touches **capital raising, securities, or investor marketing**.

## Key constraints

- **Reg D / Rule 506(b):** These private placement rules restrict how and to whom securities can be offered. Only accredited investors may be solicited; there are limits on advertising and general solicitation. If a deliverable describes a private offering or investor raise, it must not use general public solicitation language, and the audience must be qualified/accredited.
- **No forward-looking guarantees.** Do not promise specific returns, yields, or capital appreciation. Do not use "guaranteed", "risk-free", "certain return", or similar.
- **No unverified issuer claims.** Do not assert that a particular investor or institution has committed capital, is "in talks", or will invest — unless Dennis has explicitly provided that fact. Mark any such claim `[VALIDATE: <contact>]`.
- **No legal/regulatory advice.** Hermes is not a securities attorney. Anything touching Reg D 506(b), state Blue Sky laws, or SEC filing obligations must be flagged for review by Dennis and a qualified professional before use.
- **Targeted, not general.** Investor flywheel / webinar copy should be framed as educational material for qualified investors, not a public offer of securities.

## Flags to check in any draft
- Words: "invest in", "returns", "yield", "guaranteed", "raise", "offer", "accredited".
- Any named individual tied to a capital commitment (mark `[VALIDATE]`).
- Any claim about a specific dollar figure or expected rate (must be sourced, else `[VALIDATE]`).

## When in doubt
Stop and flag to Dennis. Do not let securities-touching language ship without a review gate.
