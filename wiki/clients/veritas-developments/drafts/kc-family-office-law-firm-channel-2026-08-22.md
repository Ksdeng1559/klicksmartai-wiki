### Why this beats cold email
- **Pre-vetted:** lawyers only refer families they already represent → no compliance/fit risk
- **Distribution multiplier:** one dinner → 12-20 KC FFOs in one evening
- **Co-sponsor-ready:** lawyers arrange LP/GP structures, so the conversation is technical-level, not pitch-level
