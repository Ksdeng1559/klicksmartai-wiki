# [MIRROR] Investor White Paper Research — Veritas

> **NOTION IS THE CANONICAL SOURCE.** This is a read-only mirror.
> **HITL rule:** Nothing becomes public or enters investor outreach until BOTH David Poole AND Daniel Bailey approve. This is non-negotiable.

## Purpose

Build a repeatable, evidence-backed white-paper program that positions **Veritas Development Group** as an authority in Greater Kansas City real estate development and produces credible investor education assets.

Hermes Agent conducts research and prepares drafts. David Poole and Daniel Bailey provide mandatory human review. Nothing becomes public or enters investor outreach until both reviewers approve it.

## Strategic Positioning

> **Veritas does not speculate on dirt. Veritas manufactures development certainty.**

The program explains how Veritas converts uncertainty involving land, entitlement, infrastructure, capital and buyer demand into documented evidence and staged investment decisions.

## Primary Audiences (6)

- Private real estate investors and qualified high-net-worth investors
- Family offices, real estate investment groups and co-GP partners
- Landowners considering sale, seller financing or land contribution
- Builders seeking finished lots or development inventory
- Commercial and development lenders
- Municipal and economic-development stakeholders

## Paper 1 Brief — Manufacturing Development Certainty

**Subtitle:** How disciplined site control, entitlement, infrastructure planning and buyer validation can transform uncertain land into investable real estate.

**Core question:** How can a sponsor create measurable real estate value before assuming the full capital risk of land ownership and horizontal construction?

**Required sections (14):**
1. Executive summary and investment thesis
2. The development uncertainty problem
3. Why raw acreage is not automatically an investment
4. Green Acres: Buy, Convert, De-Risk, Monetize
5. Control before ownership
6. Residual land-value underwriting
7. Seven-stage parcel lifecycle
8. Six evidence-based investment gates
9. Demand-led design and buyer evidence
10. Entitled, shovel-ready, finished-pad exits
11. Capital-at-risk ladder and staged capitalization
12. Risks, stress tests, walk-away discipline
13. Example labeled hypothetical unless project evidence is verified
14. Veritas positioning and next-step invitation

**Investor questions to answer (7):**
- What information will investors receive at each gate?
- What creates value at each stage?
- What evidence is needed before more capital is released?
- What can cause Veritas to terminate a project?
- What exits may exist before vertical construction?
- How are entitlement, infrastructure, market, financing risks monitored?
- How does Veritas avoid paying a de-risked price for uncertain land?

## Paper 3 Brief — Six Investment Gates That Protect Development Capital

1. **Control:** residual basis, contract terms, diligence budget, at least two exits
2. **Fatal flaws:** title, access, utilities, physical conditions, policy, market
3. **Municipal signal:** dated staff feedback, policy path, process, studies
4. **Application:** concept, stakeholder plan, budget, schedule, downside
5. **Entitlement and close:** priced conditions, refreshed value, buyers, alternatives
6. **Horizontal notice to proceed:** permits, reconciled bids, contingency, capital, takeout evidence

**Required stresses (5):**
- 12- and 24-month delays
- 10 and 20 percent cost overruns
- Density or saleable-yield loss
- Lower pricing and slower absorption
- Reduced debt proceeds and higher capital costs
- Documented walk-away decision as a valid capital-preservation outcome

## Hermes Agent Research Protocol

**Mission:** Produce a source-backed research package and draft that David and Daniel can audit efficiently.

**Research sequence (12 steps):**
1. Read the governing Veritas sources
2. Build the claim register before drafting
3. Research each claim using primary sources first
4. Record source, publisher, URL, publication date, data period, retrieval date
5. Capture exact supporting evidence and its limitations
6. Record geography and methodology
7. Assign High, Medium or Low confidence with a reason
8. Identify source conflicts and unresolved questions
9. Separate project facts from regional context
10. Draft only from supported claims
11. Mark unsupported statements as **OPEN ITEM**; never fill gaps with assumptions
12. Prepare a reviewer change log

**Source hierarchy (6):**
1. Government, municipality, planning authority, official economic-development sources
2. Project approvals, contracts, studies, plans, verified internal records
3. Recognized brokerage, appraisal, institutional research
4. Academic and industry-association research
5. Credible business media for context
6. Aggregators only as leads to stronger evidence

**Claim register fields (8):** Claim ID, Proposed claim, Claim class (market fact / project fact / legal-or-tax / forecast / opinion / hypothetical), Source and date, Geography, Confidence (H/M/L with reason), Reviewer (David / Daniel / specialist), Status (unverified / supported / disputed / revised / removed).

## Human-in-the-Loop Workflow (8 steps)

1. Hermes prepares the research plan and claim register
2. Dennis conducts evidence-quality review and routes gaps back to Hermes
3. Hermes prepares Draft v1 using supported claims
4. David and Daniel review Draft v1 independently
5. Hermes consolidates comments and produces Draft v2 with a change log
6. Legal, tax, engineering, appraisal or financing specialists review triggered claims
7. David and Daniel jointly grant or withhold final publication approval
8. The approved paper is converted into a PDF, landing page, investor email, LinkedIn content, presentation

**David Poole reviews:** strategy, operating capabilities, intended positioning, development process and construction practicality, project descriptions/quantities/status/sponsor representations, land control/entitlement/horizontal-delivery logic, whether any statement exceeds what Veritas can prove or execute.

**Daniel Bailey reviews:** real estate, brokerage, transaction language, landowner/builder/buyer assumptions, comparable-property and market-context relevance, sourcing/acquisition/disposition pathways, pricing/demand/liquidity/buyer-class claims, whether the CTA supports qualified conversations.

**Specialist triggers (5):**
- **Attorney:** securities, solicitation, legal structure, zoning, contracts
- **CPA or tax attorney:** tax rates, gains, incentives, tax benefits
- **Engineer or planner:** utilities, infrastructure, yield, entitlement, readiness
- **Appraiser:** valuation, cap rate, residual-value conclusions
- **Licensed financing professional:** lending terms, capital stacks, availability
