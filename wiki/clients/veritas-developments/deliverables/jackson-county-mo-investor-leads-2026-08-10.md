# Jackson County, MO (Kansas City) — Investor Leads

> **Scope:** Prepared for Veritas Developments (David Poole) — **Prime Lee's Summit** (Hwy 291, Lee's Summit) & **Stonehaven Estates** (Cyclone School Rd, Lee's Summit), Jackson County, MO.


> Prepared for the Veritas Developments MCF pipeline. Jackson County's partner ecosystem is the deepest of any pipeline county: a $100M regional housing fund is forming, the city has a Housing Trust Fund, and a faith-based church-land housing movement (AG/KC, United Believers, PEDC) is actively building. The MCF's entry window: assemble the CDFI/foundation stack before the Regional Housing Fund absorbs the available philanthropic capital.

## Priority Outreach Sequence (8–12 week cadence)

1. **Weeks 1–4 — Faith tier first:** United Believers (Edwards — active project, needs gap capital), AG/KC (church-land pipeline), PEDC (senior housing expansion), Catholic Charities (NHCH)
2. **Weeks 5–8 — CDFI + CRA banks:** AltCap (fresh NMTC allocation — natural co-investor), Central Bank (CDFI bank), UMB (LIHTC equity + construction), Commerce, KeyBank
3. **Weeks 9–12 — LIHTC + Grants:** Hunt Capital (proven KC syndicator), Monarch, MHDC (LIHTC + state trust), FHLB Des Moines AHP (2026 round), KC Regional Housing Fund (align before it launches at $40M)

**Timing flag:** The KC Regional Housing Fund is the single most important external clock — it launches around October 2026. Aligning the MCF as a deployment partner *into* the fund, or positioning KCCLT projects to draw from it, captures leverage that disappears after launch.
