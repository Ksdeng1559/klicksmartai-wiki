## Summary
Eng and Company is a BC land-acquisition and development principal focused on **Missing Middle infill** and **high-density transit-oriented (TOA Tier 3) land assemblies** in Metro Vancouver. Current active deal thread is the **417 & 419 Garrett Street, Sapperton, New Westminster** assembly (lot area ~10,000–11,000 sqft, frontage ~100ft combined, within 800m of Sapperton SkyTrain, adjacent to the $2B Royal Columbian Hospital Phase 2–3 redevelopment).

Strategy: use **wholly assignable option agreements** to tie up adjacent lots along Garrett Street, run the rezoning/permitting, then assign the contract to an institutional REIT, pension fund, or JV partner for a lift premium — capturing spread without holding final land title (and avoiding double PTT).
