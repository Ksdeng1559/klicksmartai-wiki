# Strategic Plan: Leading the Circular Economy in Precious Metals Recovery and Environmental Restoration
**Source:** NotebookLM | **Ingested:** 2026-05-22

## Vision

To redefine the resource industry by transforming environmental liabilities into high-value assets, establishing a new global standard for zero-waste precious metals production.

## Mission

Deploy proprietary, environmentally benign technologies to recover precious metals from e-waste and legacy mine tailings — generating superior financial returns while delivering verifiable environmental restoration and supporting domestic critical material supply chains.

## 2.0 Market Imperative

### 2.1 Urban Mine — AI E-Waste Tsunami
- 62M tonnes e-waste (2022); only 22.3% formally recycled
- Economic loss: USD $62B/year in recoverable resources
- US+Canada PCB gold TAM: **$1.70B – $10.22B annually**
- AI infrastructure creates 200-500+ g Au/ton in server boards (40-800× mined ore)
- Generative AI hardware projected to add **1.2 to 5.0M metric tons** of e-waste by 2030

### 2.2 Legacy Mine — Colorado Brownfields
- Colorado "richest square mile": >50M tons, 3-10 g/ton → **~$10B stranded value**
- Permitting gridlock resolved by non-toxic processing technology
- First new toll mill permit in Colorado in 20 years: Gladstone Toll Mill

## 3.0 Technological Platforms

### Electro-Winnowing (Urban Mining)
- Gold recovery rate: **86%**
- Processing capacity: ~300 lbs/hour (~3 tons/day)
- Cycle time: ~20% of cyanide-based processes
- Output: mixed precious metal concentrate (~70% Au, 20% Ag/Platinum/Pd, 10% base metals)
- Zero cyanide/strong acids

### Physical Separation (Mine Reclamation)
- Zero-toxicity: uses gravity and centrifugal force instead of chemicals
- EPA validated: TCLP, SPLP, ABA — **1,000+ year environmental stability**
- Non-toxic output: inert sand and gravel
- Breakthrough: Gladstone Toll Mill permit — precedent-setting regulatory acceptance

## 4.0 Commercial Strategy

### Feedstock Acquisition
- **Urban Mining:** Long-term agreements with ITAD firms; municipal e-waste programs; corporate "Green Kiosk" collection systems
- **Pricing:** USD $2.00/kg base for PCBs with ≥0.2 g Au/kg
- **Mine Reclamation:** Direct contracts/leases with property owners; **>$200M material access secured**

### Off-Take
- **Ocean Partners** term sheet: purchase all mixed precious metal concentrate at spot minus USD 5/g
- **Green Gold Premium target:** 5% above spot price from ESG corporate partners

### Value-Add Revenue Streams
- Silver + Palladium recovery: ~$1M/site/year
- Base metals (copper, tin): ~$500k/site/year
- Waste-to-Energy partnership (WtE) for non-metallic PCB fraction
- Bio Roc partnership: convert mine residuals into carbon-enriched fertilizer
- **Technology licensing (Year 4+):** USD $500k upfront + 5% royalty per licensee
