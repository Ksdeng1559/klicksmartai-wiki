## 1.0 Introduction

Two converging non-traditional gold recovery opportunities:

1. **Urban Mining** — extraction from end-of-life electronics (PCBs)
2. **Tailings Reprocessing** — profitable remediation of historical mine waste

Core thesis: Technologies that unlock stranded gold value while solving ESG challenges.

## 2.0 Macro-Economic Landscape

Gold's strategic resurgence driven by:
- **Safe-haven demand** — hedge against inflation, geopolitical instability, fiscal risk
- **Central bank purchases** — ~1,000 tons/year (doubled from 500 tons avg 2010-2021); emerging market banks diversifying from USD
- **Investment performance** — 10% annualized return over 20 years vs. 8% US Equities, 0% US Bonds

Higher gold prices = operational leverage for extraction companies.

### Market Accelerator: AI Infrastructure Boom

AI hardware creates a "super-grade" e-waste stream:
- **Accelerated obsolescence** — 12-18 month cycles (vs. 3-5 years traditional)
- **Higher ore grades** — Server/Telecom boards: 200-500+ g Au/ton
- **Thicker gold plating** — for thermal reliability under extreme loads
- **"Green Premium"** — ESG-driven demand from Apple, Microsoft, Google under SEC climate disclosure rules

## 4.0 Mine Reclamation Opportunity

**Colorado case study:**
- 1859-1917 production: equivalent of **>$3B in gold** (today's dollars)
- Above-ground material (waste rock + tailings): **>50M tons**
- Conservative gold content: **>150 tons recoverable** at 3 g/ton avg → ~**$10B at current prices**

**Grade comparison:**
- Dump ore piles: 3-10 g/ton
- Mill tailings: 1-3 g/ton
- Typical new mine discovery: 1-2 g/ton

**Breaking permitting gridlock:**
- Historical mercury/cyanide contamination → environmental liabilities → regulatory skepticism
- Gladstone Toll Mill permit = first in Colorado in 20 years; proves zero-toxicity can clear regulatory hurdles

## 5.0 Competitive Landscape

### E-Waste Processing

| Competitor | Process | Au Recovery | Key Differentiators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sims Metal Management | Pyrometallurgy + Leach | 70-80% | High carbon footprint, longer cycles |
| ERI (USA) | Acid Leaching + Electrorefine | 75% | Hazardous reagents, high labor |
| Small Local Recyclers | Manual + Smelting | 65% | Low yield, high labor, environmental issues |
| IMG + Eng JV | Electro-winnowing | 86% | Low emissions, fast cycle, modular, dust-free |

### Clean Recovery Technologies

- **IMG Electro-Winnowing** — dust-free, benign reagent, electrochemical cell; 86% Au recovery; ~20% of cyanide cycle time
- **MineTeck Physical Separation** — zero waste, zero toxicity; gravity + centrifugal force; 1,000-year EPA validation (TCLP, SPLP, ABA)
- **Flinders University** — trichloroisocyanuric acid + salt water leaching; recyclable sulfur polymer sorbent; closed-loop, non-toxic

## 6.0 Market Outlook

**Regulatory & ESG:**
