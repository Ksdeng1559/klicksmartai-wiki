# Voice — GPC Development

Audience: **Tak Ho, Zulliy Alnahas, and the GPC team** — sophisticated Vancouver developers, GC operators, and finance people. They speak in plain English about deals, costs, schedules, and risks. No fluff, no jargon for jargon's sake.

## Tone

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Direct, evidence-led | Marketing fluff ("unleash potential") |
| Cost-aware | Hand-wavy ROI |
| Vancouver-specific | Generic "Pacific Northwest" |
| GC / construction fluent | Layman "construction worker" language |
| Cite specific projects (Mackenzie Heights, Inova, etc.) when relevant | "We build beautiful buildings" |
| Show numbers (volumes, KD, credits) | Hide them in service-speak |
| Brief, sentence-first | Long subordinate clauses |

## Per-artifact-type defaults

| Artifact | Voice |
|---|---|
| Audit reports | Editorial — direct, no executive summary fluff |
| Quote sheets | Editorial — cost-aware, line-item pricing |
| SEO content (blog posts for GPC) | Default — evidence-led, Vancouver-aware |
| Email / outreach (if added later) | Cold-outreach — short, specific |
| Deck / investor materials (future) | Editorial |

## Voice examples

### Bad (don't write this)
> "At GPC, we unleash the full potential of Vancouver's premier real estate landscape to deliver transformative construction experiences."

### Good (write this)
> "Multifamily residential development in Vancouver — five projects live, eight in pre-development, average unit count 60, average tenure 14 months from permit to CO."

### Cost narrative (quote sheets)

> "Phase 1 fixes all 110 audit issues in 7 working days. We charge $2,500 flat, not hourly, because we know exactly what needs to change."
