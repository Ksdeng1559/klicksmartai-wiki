## 1. Strategic Identity: Brand DNA & Market Positioning

**Core thesis:** 83% of consumers would purchase insurance if products were easier to understand. Transition from "Sales-First" → "Trust-First" identity.

**Pillars:**
- Insurability Preservation
- Underwriting Transparency

**The Volatility Tax:** KFF data — 17% average ACA claims denial rate, ranging from 2% to 49% depending on insurer. Without a transparency-driven advocate, choosing an insurer is a "coin-flip on claim validity."

## 2. Market Context: The "Transparency Gap"

**Key stat:** 59% of denials are upheld on appeal → reactive management is a losing game.

| Denial Reason | Frequency | Strategic Insight |
|---------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Excluded Services | 14% | Policy ambiguity creates coverage gaps |
| Lack of Pre-authorization | 8% | Administrative friction = financial loss |
| Medical Necessity | 2% | Clinical disagreements void coverage |

| Unsuccessful Appeals | 59% upheld | Pre-claim eligibility is the only defense |

_Source: KFF Data, 2021 Plan Year._

## 3. ICP Segmentation

### ICP #1: Young Family / New Homeowner

**Market:** $2.2T mortgage debt market (CMHC). 44% of millennials unprepared for death of breadwinner.

**Emotional drivers:**
- **Legacy Control** — Protect primary asset from bank seizure
- **Affordability Correction** — Reframe the 52% who think insurance is "too expensive"
- **Financial Burden Aversion** — Mitigate 40% risk of leaving debt to survivors

**Psychology:** Ownership vs. Bank Control — they fear the bank's power over their home more than death itself.

---

### ICP #2: Health-Aware Homeowner (Ages 45–65)

**Market:** Homeowners with stable income, home values $600K–$1.5M, early health markers (elevated BP/cholesterol).

**Emotional drivers:**
- **Qualification Anxiety** — "Insurability Window" is closing
- **Spousal Security** — Avert future stress for partner
- **Preservation of Status** — Lock in "Preferred" eligibility before diagnosis

**Psychology:** Fear of being uninsurable. Health is in evolutionary state; they want to preserve eligibility while they still have it.

---

## 4. Value Proposition: Life Insurance vs. Bank MPP

Bank Mortgage Insurance (MPP) relies on **Post-Claim Underwriting** — medical history investigated only after death. Individual life insurance uses **At-Application underwriting** — eligibility locked in now.

| Feature | Bank MPP (The Trap) | Individual Life Insurance (The Solution) |
|---------|---------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Beneficiary | The Bank | Your loved ones |
| Benefit Amount | Decreases as mortgage paid | Remains level |
| Underwriting | Post-Claim | At-Application |
| Ownership | Bank-owned, non-portable | Individually owned, portable |
| Risk | "Russian Roulette" | Period of Incontestability |

**Critical stat:** RGA data — ~20% of life insurance claims are **rescinded** during the contestability period. Bank MPP effectively plays "Russian Roulette" with families.

## 7. Advisor Script Framework

**3-Step "Security Architect" Conversation (ICP #2):**

1. **Discovery:** "Has your doctor mentioned early health markers — cholesterol, blood pressure — in recent check-ups?"

2. **Risk Framing:** "Health evolves and carrier rules change. If your markers changed next year, would you rather have your family's security locked in at today's rates?"

3. **Solution:** "We're performing **Insurability Preservation**. Getting approved today starts the contestability clock and guarantees your coverage stays as a level asset."

**Tone rules:**
- ✅ "Health evolves" / "Qualification windows close"
- ❌ "You might die"
- Frame as: "Health changes over time; your eligibility doesn't have to."
