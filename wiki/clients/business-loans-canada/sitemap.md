## URL structure convention

- All under `/business-loans-canada/` subdirectory
- Slugs use full English words separated by hyphens (WordPress default)
- Province + city slugs: lowercase, no abbreviations (`british-columbia`, `calgary`, not `bc`, `yyc`)
- Property type slugs: lowercase (`restaurant-commercial-mortgage`, not `restaurant-commercial-mortgages-canada`)

## Internal linking architecture

**Hub-and-spoke pattern:**
- Cornerstone `/business-loans-canada/` links TO every other page
- Every page links BACK to cornerstone
- Every Vector 6 page links sideways to 2-3 related Vector 6 pages
- Every province page links up to the parent pillar
- Every vertical page links to the relevant product pillar

**Authority flow:**
- Root domain mortgagesbydenniseng.ca → /business-loans-canada/ → all pages
- /business-loans-canada/ → individual pillars (topical authority)
- T6 pages (Glasslake + Triple Queens) link to each other and to T2/T3 (creates topical cluster around commercial lending)

**Anchor text strategy:**
- Use exact match keywords as anchor text where natural (`business loans canada`, `bridge financing canada`, `unsecured business loan`)
- Avoid over-optimization — vary anchor text with branded + generic + naked URLs
- Sidebar/footer "Related guides" widget for cross-linking
