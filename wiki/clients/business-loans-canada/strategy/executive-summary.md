## The opportunity

**Build a Canadian business-loan comparison site on the existing Mortgagesbydenniseng.ca WordPress domain, subdirectory `/business-loans/`.** The site targets 124+ vetted commercial-intent keywords across 10 play groups, plus a parallel mortgage-lens cluster that exploits a structural gap in the Canadian market.

**The structural moat:** Swoop Funding is the dominant broker (757 indexable URLs, 3+ years of authority) but cannot publish HELOC, equity-takeout, refinance-for-business, or commercial mortgage content because their commission model excludes it. Business-loan alt-lenders cannot publish it because they don't have mortgage licenses. Only a mortgage broker who can advise on both consumer and business credit can own this lane. That's the differentiator.

**The lender anchor:** Glasslake Funding is the canonical referral partner for the commercial mortgage cluster. Dennis confirmed he can submit as a mortgage broker with them. Glasslake (Bayview Asset Management subsidiary) funds $200K-$5M small commercial mortgages, targets self-employed + alternative-to-bank borrowers, and covers the exact property types the emergency content cluster targets (mixed-use, multi-family, industrial, automotive, restaurants, daycares). Glasslake transforms the "emergency / declined / lender won't renew" content cluster from "informational with no destination" to "named-solution funnel with broker commission on funded deals."

**The unit economics:** Standard business-loan lead = $50-150. Commercial mortgage lead (with Glasslake as destination) = $5,000-30,000 per funded deal. The commercial mortgage cluster alone could fund the entire site operation multiple times over at conservative capture rates.

## Commission economics (corrected 2026-09-14)

Dennis confirmed: **commercial mortgage broker commission is min $5,000 OR 1.5-2% of transaction size, whichever is higher.**

| Deal size | @ 1.5% | @ 2% |
|---|---:|---:|
| $300K (small owner-occupied) | $5,000 (min) | $5,000 (min) |
| $500K (small-mid) | $7,500 | $10,000 |
| $1M (typical) | $15,000 | $20,000 |
| $2M (mid-large) | $30,000 | $40,000 |
| $5M+ (large) | $75,000 | $100,000 |

**Realistic avg per funded deal: $12,500.**

A single commercial mortgage funded = 167 standard business-loan leads at $75 each. The Vector 6B pillar doesn't need 1,000 sessions/mo to be profitable — it needs 8-15 qualified leads, of which 2-5 fund.

## The bottom line

**This is a 12-month, $7K-13K investment that has a realistic 30-95x first-year ROI at conservative capture rates, or 100-200x if the commercial mortgage cluster hits mid-range.**

The biggest risk is execution, not market. The market is real (35,600 vol/mo of addressable commercial-intent searches), the differentiator is structural (mortgage-broker license + business-credit lens that no one else has), and the lead economics are asymmetric (a single funded commercial mortgage = 167 standard business-loan leads).

**The single most important next action:** ship the cornerstone + Vector 6B owner-occupied commercial mortgage pillar before the next commercial mortgage renewal season (Q4 2026 / Q1 2027). Renewal is a forced-action event with 30-90 day decision windows — borrowers in renewal season are shopping right now.
