## 3. Market Assessment

### Housing Supply Situation
**Structural deficit.** Vacancy rates (~3% rental, ~1% owner) are well below healthy thresholds (5–7% rental). The market has essentially no buffer — any shock (job loss, rent increase, eviction) immediately pushes households into homelessness. Supply is constrained by:
- City of Bellingham has historically constrained growth to 1–2% annually
- WA HB 1110 (middle housing mandate) is still being implemented — duplex/triplex supply won't materialize for 2–4 years
- Semiahmoo–Birch Bay–Blaine corridor has 26% vacancy, but that stock is seasonal/STR — not available for workforce housing

### Demand Drivers
| Driver | Intensity | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Seattle remote workers | **High** | Keep Seattle salaries, buy at Bellingham prices; 20% of migration from Seattle metro |
| Canadian cash buyers | **High** | Visible in Blaine/Birch Bay corridor; pay above ask, cash |
| Amenity migrants | **Medium** | Coastal retirement, second-home buyers |
| In-migration (CA) | **Medium** | 15% of population growth from California |
| Natural increase | **Low** | Organic population growth is minor contributor |

### Competitive Landscape
- **Kulshan CLT** — only active CLT in Whatcom County; pipeline is full (LaFreniere Court, Thornton Road, Telegraph Townhomes)
- **Habitat for Humanity** — homeownership, slow throughput
- **Mercy Housing** — affordable rental, primarily federal/state funded
- **Opportunity Council** — services, not developer
- **No major national CDFI** has an active primary presence — Kulshan CLT is the clear entry point

### Political/Regulatory Environment
- **Zoning:** Bellingham is transitioning from exclusionary single-family toward missing-middle compliance with HB 1110
- **Rent control:** WA state preemption limits local rent control options — no meaningful rent stabilization possible
- **Council:** Divided between growth-constrain and growth-permit factions; WHAC pushing for inclusionary zoning
- **Risk:** Political fragmentation slows permitting; Kulshan CLT projects are in the Bellingham metro, which faces NIMBY opposition
