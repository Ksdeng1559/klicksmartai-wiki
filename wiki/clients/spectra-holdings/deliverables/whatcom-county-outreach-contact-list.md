## 1. County Officials

**Whatcom County Council — Housing Committee**
- Website: whatcomcounty.us/639/Housing-Advisory-Committee-of-Whatcom-Co
- Meets: Monthly, public meetings open to attendance
- Key reference: Resolution 2025-011 (March 2025, housing allocations by income band)

**Mayor Kim Lund — City of Bellingham**
- Signed executive order (Nov 2024) accelerating missing middle housing
- Housing Task Force lead
- Contact via: cob.org

**Whatcom County Housing Authority (BWCHA)**
- Serves low-income families, elderly, disabled, working families
- Partner with Bellingham Housing Authority (BHA) for county-wide coverage
- Website: whatcomcounty.us/441/Housing-Authority-of-Whatcom-County

**WA State Legislators — Housing Committee**
- Active on HB 1110 implementation oversight
- Contact via: app.leg.wa.gov
