# Whatcom County, WA — County Current State Report

> *"The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few."* — Matthew 9:37

Whatcom County sits at the Canadian border on the Salish Sea, anchored by the city of Bellingham. It is one of Washington State's fastest-growing mid-size counties, driven by remote-work migration, Canadian cross-border purchasing, and amenity-driven relocation. The result is a housing market under severe structural pressure — near-zero vacancy, a $200,000+ affordability gap, and a homeless population that is overwhelmingly local in origin.

**County co-investment signal:** Whatcom County invested **$750,000** in the Semiahmoo/Birch Bay/Blaine corridor (Telegraph Townhomes + LaFreniere Court) in September 2025 — a direct signal of local government commitment that de-risks MCF deployment.
