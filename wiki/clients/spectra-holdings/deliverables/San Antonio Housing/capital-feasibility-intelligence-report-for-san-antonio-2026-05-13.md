# Capital Feasibility Intelligence Report for San Antonio

## Executive Decision

**Recommendation:** Proceed to structured capital formation, subject to site-level underwriting and partner verification.

**Overall feasibility rating:** **82 / 100 — High feasibility, medium execution risk.**

Bexar County / San Antonio is a strong candidate for Spectra Holdings Group's Master Credit Facility because the market shows severe unmet housing need, sustained population growth, major affordability pressure, identified CDFI and faith-aligned partner pathways, and clear exit optionality through rental, for-sale, NOAH preservation, and missing-middle product types.

The core constraint is not demand. The constraint is capital structuring, public-sector alignment, entitlement speed, and execution discipline.

---

# 1. Capital Intelligence Stack Overview

This should not be presented as a single capital stack. It should be presented as a repeatable funding architecture that converts county housing need into financeable projects.

## Operating Logic

**INPUT → MODEL → VALIDATION → DECISION → OUTPUT**

### INPUT
County data, housing deficit, affordability gap, eviction concentration, CDFI ecosystem, faith-aligned capital, municipal incentives, project economics.

### MODEL
Master Credit Facility + project-level SPEs + blended capital stack + incentive overlays + impact reporting.

### VALIDATION
DSCR, IRR, absorption, construction cost, appraisal support, partner diligence, public incentive confirmation, resident affordability test.

### DECISION
Proceed, restructure, pause, or reject.

### OUTPUT
Capital stack package, investor memo, CDFI / NCF impact memo, municipal partnership brief, and project-level pro forma.

# 3. Highest-Probability Funding Pathways

## Pathway A — NOAH Acquisition-Rehab

**Best first use case.**

Bexar County has a highly actionable eviction thesis: concentrated eviction patterns allow capital to be deployed into acquisition-rehab and tenant stabilization before displacement becomes permanent.

### Why This Works

- Existing asset creates faster underwriting.
- No major zoning battle.
- Immediate social impact.
- Easier CDFI alignment.
- Stronger philanthropic / faith framing.
- Can preserve affordability before displacement occurs.

### Suggested Capital Stack

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| MCF equity | Acquisition deposit / control |
| CDFI debt | Acquisition-rehab loan |
| Faith PRI | Patient subordinate capital |
| City incentive | Tax abatement / fee support |
| Grants | Tenant stabilization / relocation / legal support |

**Feasibility rating:** **86 / 100**

This is the strongest near-term deployment path because it avoids the largest new-construction risks.

---

## Pathway B — Missing-Middle New Construction

San Antonio has a structural gap between single-family homes and large apartment complexes. Smaller 2-to-20 unit projects are difficult to finance but highly aligned with workforce housing needs.

### Target Products

- 4–12 unit courtyard buildings.
- 2–4 unit for-sale missing-middle product.
- ADU / cottage court models.
- Small workforce rental buildings.
- Starter ownership product for households earning approximately $50K–$75K.

### Suggested Capital Stack

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| MCF equity | Land control + predevelopment |
| Faith capital | Patient affordability layer |
| CDFI | Gap financing |
| Bank debt | Construction debt |
| City support | Zoning / permit acceleration / TIRZ / fee deferral |

**Feasibility rating:** **74 / 100**

Strong demand, but execution depends heavily on zoning, permitting, parcel assembly, and cost control.

---

## Pathway C — Anti-Eviction / Housing Stability Fund

San Antonio's homeless response and prevention infrastructure creates a measurable impact pathway. This can be structured as a Housing Stability Sidecar Fund connected to the MCF.

### Suggested Structure

- MCF creates a dedicated Housing Stability Sidecar Fund.
- Faith capital / DAF capital funds the concessionary layer.
- CDFI or nonprofit partner administers funds.
- Outcomes tracked by household, ZIP code, eviction risk, and stabilization result.

**Feasibility rating:** **80 / 100**

Strong impact case, but may produce lower direct financial return unless paired with acquisition, rehab, resident ownership, or portfolio preservation pathways.

# 5. Go / No-Go Decision

## Go

Proceed with capital formation if Spectra can secure:

1. One credible CDFI / nonprofit housing partner.
2. One faith-aligned capital conversation, preferably NCF-linked.
3. One pilot property or property cluster.
4. A project-level pro forma showing DSCR, IRR, cost-to-build, and absorption.
5. Municipal meeting pathway for permitting / incentive alignment.
6. Legal structure for MCF to SPE deployment.

## No-Go / Pause

Pause if:

1. No local operating partner is confirmed.
2. Site control cannot be obtained.
3. Senior debt requires guarantees Spectra cannot support.
4. Affordability commitments destroy DSCR.
5. Public incentive assumptions are required but not confirmed.
6. Construction costs cannot be fixed or controlled.

# 7. Final Confidence Rating

## Overall Confidence: 82 / 100

### Why Confidence Is High

- Housing need is severe.
- Demand fundamentals are strong.
- Faith and CDFI alignment is unusually strong.
- Eviction data creates a targeted intervention thesis.
- NOAH preservation offers a lower-risk entry point.
- Homeless response system already shows measurable ROI.
- MCF structure fits the market's need for patient, flexible capital.

### Why Confidence Is Not Above 90

- Texas has a lower public-subsidy environment.
- Missing-middle zoning reform is uncertain.
- Senior lenders will require hard collateral and underwriting.
- Actual site economics are not yet validated.
- Construction costs, insurance, taxes, and local operating partners must be confirmed.
- Public incentives should be treated as upside, not base-case dependency.

# 8. Best First Move

Do not start with a 5,000-unit narrative. Start with a capital-validating pilot.

The strongest first pilot is:

**NOAH acquisition-rehab + anti-eviction stabilization + CDFI / faith capital sidecar.**

That gives Spectra the fastest path to proof of capital, proof of impact, proof of local partnership, proof of underwriting, and proof that the MCF can recycle capital into future housing production.
