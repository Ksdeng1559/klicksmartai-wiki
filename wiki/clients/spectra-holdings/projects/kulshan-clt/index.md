# Kulshan CLT — Whatcom County MCF Pilot

> Primary local partner for Spectra Holdings Master Credit Facility in Whatcom County, WA.

## Overview

**Kulshan Community Land Trust (CLT)** is the primary MCF pilot partner in Whatcom County. The CLT model provides permanently affordable homeownership — land held in trust, home owned by the resident, resale restrictions keep affordability in perpetuity.

**Whatcom County context:**
- Population: 236,392 (2025 est.)
- Median home: $585,800 | Workforce can afford: ~$385,000
- Affordability gap: **$200K+**
- Rental vacancy: ~3% (healthy: 5–7%)
- 47% of homeless cite housing affordability as primary cause
- 69% of homeless individuals lost housing *within* Whatcom County

---

## MCF Structure for CLT Projects

```
Spectra MCF (patient capital)
    ↓
Kulshan CLT (developer/sponsor)
    ↓
Individual CLT homeowners (equity + mortgage)
```

- MCF provides construction financing at below-market rates
- CLT sells home to income-qualified buyer
- Buyer receives ground lease (owns home, rents land)
- Resale formula: buyer pays limited appreciation (typically 25% of gains)
- Home stays affordable in perpetuity regardless of market appreciation
