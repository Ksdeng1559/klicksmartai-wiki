# Bexar County — CDFI / CMF Eligibility Snapshot

## Purpose

This document provides a preliminary eligibility and alignment review for potential CDFI and Capital Magnet Fund participation in a Bexar County / San Antonio workforce housing pilot.

## Data Basis

This snapshot draws from:

- Bexar County Current State Report (2026-05-13)
- Bexar County Census/Housing Brief
- Capital Feasibility Intelligence Report for San Antonio
- Spectra Holdings Financial Intelligence System modules

## 1. CDFI Eligibility Indicators

### Workforce Housing Need

| Indicator | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| Housing deficit | 115,125 units (structural) |
| Affordability gap | $16,095 annual income gap for median renters |
| Households below $50K | ~40% of all households |
| Poverty rate | 17.1% (above national 12.5%) |
| Cost-burdened households | 95,000 (city 10-year plan target) |

**Assessment:** Strong CDFI target market alignment. The bottom 40% of households cannot sustain median market rents, and the income gap creates a clear intervention point for mission capital.

### Eviction Concentration

| Indicator | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| Eviction filings (2024) | 27,012 |
| Concentration | 12% from 30 identifiable properties |
| Pre-COVID rate | ~13,000/yr |
| Current vs. baseline | More than double |

**Assessment:** Highly addressable intervention point for CDFI loan loss reserve or subordinate debt against eviction-triggered default risk.

### Affordable Housing Waitlist

| Indicator | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| Opportunity Home SA waitlist | 65,920 households |
| Public housing waitlist | 48,101 |
| HCV waitlist | 17,124 |
| Housing authority acceptance rate | ~4% |

**Assessment:** Extraordinary demand signal. If only 4% of waitlist households are served, the unmet need is severe and ongoing.

### Target Market Fit

| CDFI Criterion | Bexar County Status |

| Underserved households | Yes — 40% below $50K, 17.1% poverty |

## 2. Capital Magnet Fund (CMF) Alignment

### CMF Priority Activities

CMF supports:

- affordable housing for low-income households,
- economic development and community revitalization,
- infrastructure in low-income communities,
- multi-family rental housing with income restrictions.

### Bexar County CMF Relevance

| CMF Priority | Bexar County Fit |
|--------------|------------------|
| Affordable rental housing | Strong — 115K unit deficit, 65K waitlist |
| Mixed-income housing with affordability restrictions | Strong — missing-middle gap |
| homebuyer assistance (low-to-moderate income) | Strong — median income $65K, median home $235K |
| infrastructure supporting affordable housing | Strong — Lane A infill infrastructure |
| community revitalization | Strong — Eastside Promise, SA Tomorrow corridors |

### Recommended CMF Use Cases

1. **CMF as subordinate debt** — reduces risk for senior lender and MCF construction capital.
2. **CMF as loan loss reserve** — improves CDFI lender appetite for NOAH acquisition gap.
3. **CMF as construction-to-permanent bridge** — provides rate-buydown for workforce ownership product.
4. **CMF as infrastructure grant** — Lane A infill infrastructure in target corridors.

## 6. Required Validation Before Capital Commitment

- Confirm specific CDFI's target market areas and AMI targeting requirements.
- Confirm CMF allocation cycle and intermediary partner availability.
- Confirm TDHCA LIHTC application timeline and underwriting criteria.
- Confirm City of SA HOME and CDBG allocation availability for target corridors.
- Confirm leverage requirements and matching capital availability.
- Confirm reporting obligations and compliance infrastructure requirements.
