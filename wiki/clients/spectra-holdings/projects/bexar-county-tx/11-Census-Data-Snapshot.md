# 5. MCF Intervention Thesis

Bexar County may be an MCF pilot target because:

1. It has a reported 115,125-unit structural housing deficit.
2. Missing-middle housing appears to be a key financing and delivery gap.
3. Eviction pressure is concentrated and measurable.
4. Affordable housing demand is evidenced by a large housing authority waitlist.
5. Faith-aligned and mission-oriented capital may have a clear community narrative.
6. The market scale may support repeatable construction and capital deployment.
