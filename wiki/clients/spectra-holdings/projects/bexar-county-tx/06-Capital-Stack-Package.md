## Capital Stack Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| CDFI gap not closed | Faith sidecar acts as bridge; parallel-track outreach |
| Interest rate spike at refi | Rate cap on construction loan; passthrough to permanent lender |
| Absorption delay | 50% pre-leasing required before CO; rents below market |
| Cost overrun | Fixed-price contracts; 10% contingency in budget |
| Affordability covenant default | CDFI monitors; grace period with cure rights |

## Faith-Capital Sidecar Logic

The faith-capital sidecar (NCF/DAF) serves three purposes:

1. **Community trust signal** — Faith-aligned capital signals long-term commitment to the community rather than speculative extraction.
2. **Below-market cost of gap capital** — 3% rate vs. market 6–8% for subordinate gap reduces total blended cost from ~6.5% to ~5.8%.
3. **Grant conversion incentive** — If affordability covenants are met for 15 years, the $420,000 converts from debt to grant, reducing the total obligation and improving the long-term capital position.
