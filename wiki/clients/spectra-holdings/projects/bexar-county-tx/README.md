# Bexar County, TX — Spectra Project Workspace

## Purpose

This folder is the live project workspace for evaluating Bexar County, Texas as a potential Spectra Holdings housing, CDFI, MCF, municipal, and community development deployment market.

This workspace follows the same operating logic as the Whatcom County project folder, but should be rebuilt with Bexar-specific data, partners, incentives, housing demand, infrastructure constraints, and municipal priorities.

## Current Decision Status

**Intake / Research Required**

Bexar County has not yet been validated as a Spectra pilot market. It should proceed through structured county intelligence, Census analysis, housing community sentiment review, CDFI / CMF eligibility screening, and MCF deployment feasibility.

# Operating Logic

Every Bexar County output should follow Spectra's financial intelligence workflow:

**INPUT → MODEL → VALIDATION → DECISION → OUTPUT**

## INPUT

Required inputs:

- Census / ACS data.
- Housing supply and demand data.
- Affordability metrics.
- Population growth and migration trends.
- Workforce and wage data.
- County and city housing plans.
- Infrastructure and land constraints.
- CDFI / CMF eligibility indicators.
- Municipal bond / public incentive opportunities.
- Faith-based and nonprofit housing ecosystem.
- Potential local development partners.

## MODEL

Required models:

- County Intelligence Report.
- Census / ACS Data Snapshot.
- Housing Community Sentiment Report.
- CDFI / CMF Eligibility Snapshot.
- MCF Pilot Application.
- Capital Stack Package.
- MCF Pilot Pro Forma.
- Risk Register.

## VALIDATION

Required validation:

- municipal priorities,
- county housing need,
- infrastructure readiness,
- partner availability,
- land opportunity,
- CDFI eligibility,
- incentive availability,
- buyer / renter demand,
- and capital stack feasibility.

## DECISION

Decision outputs should answer:

- Should Spectra proceed in Bexar County?
- What housing product is most needed?
- What capital stack is most appropriate?
- What improves the deal?
- What breaks the deal?
- Who are the likely local partners?
- What is the first pilot pathway?

## OUTPUT

Required outputs:

- executive decision brief,
- county intelligence report,
- housing sentiment report,
- CDFI / CMF snapshot,
- MCF pilot application,
- investor narrative,
- municipal briefing,
- risk register,
- and final Go / No-Go recommendation.
