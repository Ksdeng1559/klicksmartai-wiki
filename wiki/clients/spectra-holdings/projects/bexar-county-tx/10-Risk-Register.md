## 1. Overview

This risk register identifies and assesses the primary risks associated with the Bexar County NOAH pilot (20 units, $2.8M deployment) and the broader county-level MCF strategy. Risks are scored on a 1–5 scale for both **likelihood** and **impact**. Scores are multiplied to produce a **priority rank**. Mitigation actions are defined for all risks rated 12 or above.

---

## 2. Risk Scoring Matrix

| Score | Likelihood | Impact |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| 5 | Highly likely (>80%) | Critical — project fails |
| 4 | Likely (60–80%) | High — major delays/cost |
| 3 | Possible (40–60%) | Moderate — manageable impact |
| 2 | Unlikely (20–40%) | Low — minor disruption |
| 1 | Very unlikely (<20%) | Negligible |

**Priority:** Likelihood × Impact. Scores ≥12 require active mitigation.

## 3. Project Execution Risks

### Risk R-01: Property Acquisition Competition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk | NOAH properties attract competing bids from private equity buyers, delaying or preventing acquisition at target prices. |
| Likelihood | 4 |
| Impact | 4 |
| Priority | 16 |
| Trend | Increasing |
| Mitigation | Establish direct seller relationships with MLS-listed NOAH owners; partner with Opportunity Home SA to identify off-market pipeline; structure closings within 14-day window. |

### Risk R-02: Renovation Cost Overruns

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk | Construction cost inflation in San Antonio (labor shortage, material tariff impacts) drives per-unit rehab costs above $60K budget threshold. |
| Likelihood | 3 |
| Impact | 3 |
| Priority | 9 |
| Trend | Stable |
| Mitigation | Pre-qualified general contractors under fixed-price contracts; contingency reserve of 15% of rehab budget; scope negotiations before closing. |

## 4. Community & Regulatory Risks

### Risk R-05: Affordability Covenant Enforcement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk | Local enforcement mechanisms for income-qualified tenancy are legally untested in Texas; covenant breach without remedy. |
| Likelihood | 3 |
| Impact | 4 |
| Priority | 12 |
| Trend | Known |
| Mitigation | CLT structure (Kulshan-style) provides third-party enforcement right; recording affordability covenant at county property database; annual compliance audit clause. |

### Risk R-06: Texas SB 1443 (Landlord-Tenant) Preemption

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk | State law limits local rent control or eviction protections; federal preemption arguments untested and slow. |
| Likelihood | 2 |
| Impact | 3 |
| Priority | 6 |
| Trend | Low |
| Mitigation | Covenant enforcement via contract law (not rent control); tenant screening as primary stabilization tool; community land trust deed restriction survives property sale. |

### Risk R-07: Public / Community Opposition to Density

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk | NIMBY opposition to workforce housing in Eastside / Westside corridors stalls permitting. |
| Likelihood | 3 |
| Impact | 3 |
| Priority | 9 |
| Trend | Monitored |
| Mitigation | Early community engagement sessions (60-day pre-application); faith-based coalition co-signature on project application; council-adjacent partner (City NHSD) as project champion. |

## 5. Financial Risks

### Risk R-08: Interest Rate Sensitivity

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk | Rising rates reduce NOAH cap rate arbitrage; debt service coverage ratio tightens below 1.15x covenant minimum. |
| Likelihood | 3 |
| Impact | 3 |
| Priority | 9 |
| Trend | Declining (rates stabilizing) |

| Impact | 4 |

| Trend | Annual |

### Risk R-10: Appraisal Gap on NOAH Properties

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk | As-is appraisals come in below offer price, creating financing gaps and deal failure risk on specific properties. |
| Likelihood | 4 |
| Impact | 3 |
| Priority | 12 |

| Mitigation | Two-appraisal review on any property with >10% gap; seller concession negotiation clause in purchase contracts; acquisition price ceiling at 85% of neighborhood median sales price. |
