# Bexar County, TX — Municipal Briefing Deck Outline

## Purpose

This document provides the outline and speaking notes for a municipal briefing meeting with City of San Antonio officials, Bexar County staff, or elected representatives regarding a Spectra Holdings MCF pilot in Bexar County.

---

## Briefing Objectives

1. Introduce Spectra Holdings as a housing delivery partner (not a speculative developer).
2. Demonstrate alignment with City of San Antonio housing priorities and SA Tomorrow plan.
3. Identify specific geographic corridors (Council District 8 / Eastside Promise) for initial deployment.
4. Confirm municipal incentive and entitlement pathway availability.
5. Establish a municipal point of contact for ongoing coordination.

### Slide 3 — The Problem San Antonio Faces

**115,000+ unit housing deficit. Not a cyclical shortage. A structural crisis.**

Key facts:
- 27,012 eviction filings in 2024 (more than double pre-COVID rate)
- 65,920 households on affordable housing waitlist
- Median household income $65,056 — not enough to afford median rent or home
- 40% of households earn below $50K
- San Antonio rents +53% over 10 years; home prices +54%

---

### Slide 4 — What We Are Proposing

**A pilot workforce housing program for San Antonio**

- First use case: NOAH acquisition-rehab (20 units in targeted corridor)
- Target population: Workforce households at 60–80% AMI
- Product: 1BR and 2BR apartments at below-market rents
- Affordability: 15-year covenant restricting rents to 60–80% AMI bands
- Construction: Vertically integrated — faster delivery, controlled cost, consistent quality

---

### Slide 5 — Why This Works for San Antonio

**Matching the delivery gap**

San Antonio has a documented missing-middle financing gap:
- Market-rate developers build for high-income households.
- Affordable housing programs target very low income (below 30% AMI).
- The workforce band (60–80% AMI) falls between these two — too high for deep subsidy, too low for market-rate feasibility.

Spectra's product is designed precisely for this band.

## Key Messaging Points

### Core Narrative

> Spectra Holdings is not here to speculate on San Antonio's growth. We are here to help solve a housing problem that the market alone cannot solve — for the workforce households, families, and essential service providers that San Antonio needs to retain.

### If Asked About Speed

> We control our own construction and manufacturing. We can move from site control to certificate of occupancy faster than conventional developers because we remove the fragmented subcontractor and supply chain risk.

### If Asked About Affordability Enforcement

> Affordability covenants are held by our CDFI partners and recorded against the property. They are enforceable for 15 years minimum. If we violate them, the loan comes due. We have every incentive to maintain compliance.

### If Asked About Gentrification

> This product is designed for people who already live and work in San Antonio. We are not targeting external speculative buyers. We are targeting workforce households — teachers, nurses, tradespeople, municipal workers — who are being priced out of the market they serve.

### If Asked About Local Hiring

> We will commit to local hiring quotas, local vendor preferences, and workforce development programs in the target corridor. These will be included in the community benefit agreement with the City.
