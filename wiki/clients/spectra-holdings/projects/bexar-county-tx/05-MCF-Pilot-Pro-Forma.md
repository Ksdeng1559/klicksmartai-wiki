# Bexar County, TX — MCF Pilot Pro Forma

## Purpose

This pro forma models the financial structure for a first Bexar County / San Antonio MCF pilot project. It follows the Spectra Holdings Financial Intelligence System framework and uses San Antonio market data.

---

## Pilot Assumptions

| Parameter | Value | Basis |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Pilot product | NOAH acquisition-rehab (Lane A — infill cluster) | Fastest delivery, lowest lease-up risk |
| Unit count | 20 units | Manageable first batch; proof of concept |
| Unit mix | 1BR and 2BR apartments | Affordability targeting; fastest lease-up |
| Target AMI band | 60–80% AMI | Workforce housing; below-market rents |
| Estimated acquisition cost per unit | $95,000 | Below-median San Antonio home price; NOAH discount |
| Estimated rehab cost per unit | $45,000 | Moderate renovation for habitability and energy efficiency |
| Total cost per unit | $140,000 | |
| Total pilot project cost | $2,800,000 | 20 units × $140,000 |
| Target monthly rent (1BR) | $1,050 | Below market average ($1,081); affordability target |
| Target monthly rent (2BR) | $1,350 | Below market average ($1,377); affordability target |
| Weighted average rent | $1,150 | 40% 1BR, 60% 2BR mix |
| Gross monthly rental income | $23,000 | 20 units × $1,150 |
| Annual gross rental income | $276,000 | |

## Absorption Gate

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Lease-up timeline | 60 days from CO |
| Pre-leasing target | 50% before CO |
| Absorption rate | 3–4 units/month |
| Vacancy trigger | >15% vacancy for 90 days = pause next tranche |
| Rent-to-income ratio | ≤30% of target tenant income |

---

## Repayment Logic

| Exit Path | Conditions | Timeline |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Permanent financing refi** | Stabilized NOI, DSCR ≥ 1.25x, occupancy ≥ 90% | Month 18–24 |
| **NOAH syndication** | Sale to mission buyer (CLT, nonprofit) at below-market cap rate | Month 24–36 |
| **Pay-as-we-sell (ownership)** | If product converts to for-sale, proceeds fund MCF payback | Month 12–36 |
| **Public agency purchase** | City of SA or Bexar County acquires for affordable housing | If offered |

## Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Construction/rehab cost overrun | Medium | High | Fixed-price contracts; 10% contingency in budget |
| Lease-up delay | Low | Medium | 50% pre-leasing before CO; below-market rents |
| Interest rate increase on refi | Medium | Medium | Rate cap on construction loan; refi passthrough |
| Rent growth below model | Medium | Low | Affordability covenant doesn't require market rent |
| CDFI gap capital not closed | Low | High | Parallel-track CDFI outreach; faith sidecar as backup |
| Eviction and tenant displacement | Medium | Medium | Tenant stabilization services; housing authority coordination |
