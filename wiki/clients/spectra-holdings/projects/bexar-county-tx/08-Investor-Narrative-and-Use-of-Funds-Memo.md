# Bexar County, TX — Investor Narrative and Use-of-Funds Memo

## Purpose

This memo provides the investor-facing narrative and use-of-funds statement for a first Bexar County / San Antonio MCF pilot project. It is designed for family offices, CDFI partners, faith-aligned funders, and mission-driven investors.

---

## Investment Thesis

San Antonio is the 7th largest U.S. city with a structural housing deficit of 115,000+ units, an affordability gap that has widened 53% over 10 years, and a proven homelessness response system that has reduced unsheltered populations by 25% while national trends increased 30%. Yet it remains one of the most underpriced and underserved major metros in the country for workforce housing capital.

Spectra Holdings is deploying a vertically integrated construction and financing model to fill the missing-middle gap — producing workforce housing at lower cost, shorter timelines, and with permanent affordability controls that market-rate development cannot deliver.

## Risk Controls

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Construction cost overrun | Fixed-price contracts; 10% contingency |
| Lease-up delay | 50% pre-leasing before CO; below-market rents |
| Refi rate spike | Rate cap on construction loan; passthrough to permanent lender |
| Affordability covenant default | CDFI monitoring; recorded covenant with cure rights |
| Capital stack gap | Parallel-track CDFI outreach; faith sidecar as bridge |
| Eviction/tenant displacement | Tenant stabilization services; housing authority coordination |

---

## Affordability Controls

- 15-year recorded covenant restricting rents to 60–80% AMI bands.
- Annual compliance reporting to CDFI and municipal partners.
- Right of first refusal to City or designated affordable housing nonprofit upon sale.
- No tax abatement sought — property taxes paid in full.

---

## Faith-Capital Alignment

The faith-capital sidecar (3% NCF/DAF capital) serves three functions:

1. **Below-market cost** — Reduces blended cost of capital from 6.5% to 5.8%.
2. **Community trust signal** — Faith-aligned capital demonstrates long-term stewardship commitment.
3. **Conversion incentive** — If affordability covenants are maintained for 15 years, principal converts to grant, improving investor return profile.
