# Kulshan Community Land Trust Partnership Memo

## Purpose

This memo outlines the strategic rationale for a potential partnership between Spectra Holdings and Kulshan Community Land Trust in Whatcom County, WA.

## Strategic Alignment

Kulshan CLT provides:

- permanent affordability infrastructure,
- community trust,
- local housing credibility,
- buyer stewardship,
- and mission alignment with CDFI and faith-based capital.

Spectra provides:

- vertically integrated construction capability,
- MCF construction financing,
- scalable deployment systems,
- and capital stack engineering.

## Why the Partnership Matters

The partnership could bridge:

- public funding,
- mission capital,
- construction financing,
- and long-term affordability.

The CLT structure helps ensure affordability remains attached to the property across future resale cycles.

## Potential Pilot Areas

- Thornton Road
- Telegraph Townhomes
- LaFreniere Court
- future corridor expansion opportunities

## Proposed Strategic Roles

### Kulshan CLT

- community stewardship
- affordability administration
- local engagement
- buyer qualification
- land trust structure

### Spectra Holdings

- construction deployment
- MCF capital structure
- project delivery systems
- capital stack coordination
- investor reporting

## Required Validation

- confirm project pipeline alignment
- confirm geographic fit
- confirm infrastructure readiness
- confirm legal and governance compatibility
- confirm financing structure compatibility

## Recommended Next Step

Proceed to exploratory partnership discussions and pilot-level feasibility review.
