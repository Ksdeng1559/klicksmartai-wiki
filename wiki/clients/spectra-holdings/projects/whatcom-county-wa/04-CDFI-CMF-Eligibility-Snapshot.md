# Whatcom County — CDFI / CMF Eligibility Snapshot

## Purpose

This document provides a preliminary eligibility and alignment review for potential CDFI and Capital Magnet Fund participation in a Whatcom County workforce housing pilot.

## Preliminary Alignment Indicators

### Workforce Housing Need

The county demonstrates:

- documented housing shortage,
- affordability stress,
- workforce displacement,
- and permanent affordability demand.

### Community Impact Alignment

Potential alignment areas include:

- affordable housing,
- workforce housing,
- underserved households,
- community revitalization,
- and resilience-oriented housing.

### Public-Private Partnership Potential

County co-investment precedent and housing policy support strengthen alignment with community-development-oriented capital.

## Potential Eligible Capital Uses

- subordinate debt
- loan loss reserves
- affordable housing financing
- revolving loan support
- buyer-side financing enhancement
- infrastructure-related housing support

## Potential Capital Sources

- CDFI lenders
- CMF-aligned structures
- HUD-aligned programs
- state housing programs
- donor-advised mission capital
- faith-based impact capital

## Key Validation Requirements

- identify certified CDFI partner
- confirm target market eligibility
- confirm AMI affordability requirements
- confirm use-of-funds compliance
- confirm leverage structure
- confirm reporting obligations

## Current Status

**Preliminary alignment appears positive but requires project-level validation.**
