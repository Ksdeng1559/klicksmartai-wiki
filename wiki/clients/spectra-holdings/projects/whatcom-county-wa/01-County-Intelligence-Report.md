## Executive Summary

Whatcom County is a high-priority pilot candidate for Spectra Holdings because it combines:

- documented housing shortage,
- severe affordability gap,
- active county co-investment,
- missing-middle zoning urgency under HB 1110,
- a potential CLT partner through Kulshan Community Land Trust,
- and a strong fit for CDFI / MCF / faith-aligned capital deployment.

## Key Intelligence Findings

### Housing Need

- Whatcom County has a documented 5,000-unit housing shortfall over five years.
- Rental vacancy and owner vacancy are below healthy market thresholds.
- Homes under $750,000 remain supply-constrained.
- The market is bifurcating, with pressure concentrated in attainable workforce ownership.

### Affordability Gap

- Median household income is not sufficient to support median home prices.
- Conventional mortgage finance cannot bridge the gap for many workforce households.
- The target product should focus on attainable ownership and missing-middle housing.

### Growth Corridor

The Semiahmoo / Birch Bay / Blaine corridor is growing faster than the county average, but faces:

- short-term rental distortion,
- infrastructure constraints,
- NIMBY resistance,
- and a lack of affordable housing developer capacity.

### Political Window

The county has shown public-sector willingness through:

- housing and infrastructure co-investment,
- density approvals,
- missing-middle planning,
- and formal housing allocation policy.

### Manufacturing / Delivery Fit

Spectra's vertically integrated construction model may help address:

- high conventional construction costs,
- slow delivery timelines,
- missing-middle feasibility gaps,
- and long-term durability / resilience needs.

## Strategic Decision Implication

Whatcom County should move from intelligence collection to structured pilot validation.

The next stage should focus on:

1. Kulshan CLT partnership confirmation.
2. Specific project or phase identification.
3. Infrastructure capacity validation.
4. CDFI / CMF eligibility review.
5. MCF pilot pro forma.
6. County / municipal engagement strategy.
7. Investor-ready capital stack package.

## Decision Status

**Conditional Go**

Proceed to validation, but do not treat the project as fully approved until partner, site, infrastructure, capital, and repayment assumptions are confirmed.
