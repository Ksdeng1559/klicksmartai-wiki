## 8. Underwriting Implications

### Demand Signal

The population, vacancy, and affordability data support a strong workforce housing demand thesis.

### Product Signal

The most relevant product types are:

- missing-middle housing,
- townhomes,
- cottage courts,
- ADUs / carriage units,
- CLT-based affordable ownership,
- and workforce rental where infrastructure supports it.

### Capital Signal

The affordability gap supports the need for:

- CDFI capital,
- CMF-aligned catalytic capital,
- buyer-side credit enhancement,
- public-sector co-investment,
- and MCF construction financing.
