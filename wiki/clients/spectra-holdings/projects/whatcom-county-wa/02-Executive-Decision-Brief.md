# Whatcom County — Executive Decision Brief

## Decision Status

**Conditional Go**

Whatcom County should proceed to structured validation as a Spectra MCF / CDFI / CLT pilot market.

## Executive Summary

Whatcom County presents a high-priority pilot opportunity because it combines documented housing need, public co-investment, a time-sensitive regulatory window, and a credible local affordability partner through Kulshan Community Land Trust.

The opportunity is not a conventional real estate play. It is a county-level housing delivery and capital deployment pilot that can validate Spectra's broader model:

**County Intelligence → Partner Validation → Capital Stack → MCF Deployment → Housing Delivery → Capital Recycling**

## Why This Matters

The county has a structural workforce housing gap that conventional mortgage finance and traditional development economics cannot solve alone. Median-income households cannot afford the prevailing median home price, while homes below the upper-market threshold remain supply constrained.

This creates a compelling case for:

- Master Credit Facility construction deployment,
- CDFI / CMF capital layering,
- public-sector co-investment,
- CLT affordability controls,
- and mission-aligned / faith-based capital participation.

## Recommended Action

Proceed to validation and modeling before final capital commitment.

## Go Conditions

- Kulshan CLT confirms partnership interest.
- Specific pilot project or phase is identified.
- Infrastructure and entitlement feasibility are confirmed.
- CDFI / CMF eligibility is validated.
- MCF pilot pro forma supports repayment logic.
- Buyer pipeline supports presale or buyer-readiness threshold.

## No-Go Conditions

- No viable project site or phase is identified.
- Infrastructure capacity blocks execution.
- Political resistance prevents entitlement path.
- Buyer pipeline cannot support absorption.
- Capital stack depends on uncertain grants without fallback.

## Immediate Next Steps

1. Confirm Kulshan CLT discussion pathway.
2. Identify project-level pilot target.
3. Build MCF pilot pro forma.
4. Map CDFI / CMF eligibility.
5. Prepare county / municipal briefing.
6. Prepare investor use-of-funds memo.

## Decision Output

Move Whatcom County from intelligence stage into pilot validation stage.
