# Tiyo Energy

## Classification
**Status:** Active client
**Category:** Waste-to-Energy / Circular Economy / Biogas
**Jurisdiction:** Indonesia (Jakarta focus)

## Summary
Tiyo Energy is a waste-to-value / circular economy venture focused on converting organic waste into energy (biogas via anaerobic digestion). Aligned with Jakarta's documented journey toward circular waste management, as covered by SIPA UNDP Indonesia.

## Key Themes
- Anaerobic digestion (biogas/renewable energy from organic waste)
- Circular economy / waste valorization
- Municipal solid waste (MSW) management
- Jakarta metropolitan area waste infrastructure
- Carbon offset / ESG value propositions
