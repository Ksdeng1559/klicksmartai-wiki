## Content Architecture

### Publication Identity

**WealthWireRadar** is a signal-driven intelligence brief for HNWI-facing professionals — wealth advisors, family office directors, private bankers, and estate planners. Content bridges the gap between raw financial signals (market moves, regulatory shifts, trend data) and actionable client conversation points.

**Positioning Statement:** "The 5-minute signal brief that makes you the smartest person in every client meeting."

**Voice:** Analytical, concise, forward-looking. No cheerleading. No panic. Just *"here's what's happening and here's what it means for your clients."*

## Content Pipeline & Production Workflow

### Signal Detection (Ongoing — Hermes Automation)

Daily automated scan via cron across:
- SEC/IRS/Treasury filings and rulings
- Major PWM wealth-management publications (Barron's Penta, Bloomberg Wealth, FT Wealth)
- Family office research (FOX, Campden, UBS GFO, Citi Private Bank)
- Tax and estate law firm client alerts (Withers, McDermott, Kirkland, Holland & Knight)
- HNW consumer behavior indicators (Luxury Institute, Sotheby's, Christie's results)

### Production Cycle (Weekly)

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| **Friday** | Hermes signal sweep → produces "Signal Digest" for following week |
| **Monday morning** | Editor reviews Signal Digest, selects top Radar Brief and Deep Scan topics |
| **Tuesday 7 AM** | Radar Brief #1 publishes |
| **Wednesday 7 AM** | The Deep Scan publishes |
| **Thursday 7 AM** | Radar Brief #2 publishes |
| **Friday** | Signal Snapshot posts; next-week signal sweep runs |

### Content Templates

**Radar Brief (600-900 words):**
1. **The Signal** (50 words) — What happened, source
2. **Why It Matters** (150 words) — HNW client impact
3. **The Advisor's Play** (250 words) — Actionable conversation starter, 2-3 talking points
4. **What to Watch** (100 words) — Forward indicators
5. **Further Reading** (links)

**The Deep Scan (2,000-3,000 words):**
1. Executive Summary (100 words)
2. The Landscape (300 words) — Background/situational analysis
3. Signal Breakdown (500 words) — 3-5 discrete signals with source citations
4. Client Impact Analysis (400 words) — Segmented by client profile (UBHNW, HNW, mass-affluent)
5. The Advisory Playbook (400 words) — 5+ concrete action items for advisors
6. Forward Radar (200 words) — 6-12 month outlook
7. Source Index (links/references)
