## Persona A: "The Climate-First Family Office" ($50M+ AUM)

### Profile
- **Typical Role:** Principal or CIO at a single-family office
- **Portfolio Thesis:** Climate adaptation + energy resilience
- **Check Size:** $250K–$2M (Seed to Series A)
- **Decision Horizon:** 6–18 months
- **Information Diet:** S2G Ventures reports, ClimateTech VC newsletters, Bloomberg Green, CTVC

### Psychographic Signals
- Motivated by **impact metrics** not IRR alone
- Requires **tangible product validation** (units shipped, reviews, return rates)
- Will want to see **supply chain transparency** (LiFePO4 sourcing, tariff exposure)
- Responds to: "gigatons of CO₂ displaced" language

### Hook (Signal → Outreach)
> "The portable power market shipped 15M units in 2023 — nearly all lithium. Yet under 3% of brands disclose battery provenance. WattBricks could be the first D2C power station company with verified, conflict-free LiFePO4 supply chain. That story alone commands a green premium. Want to see what that looks like in a pitch deck?"

### Objection → Counter
| Objection | Counter |
|-----------|---------|
| "Too early — no revenue data" | "This is precisely when climate-first capital gets the best terms. EcoFlow's first $5M round was at a $15M cap." |
| "D2C hardware is capital-intensive" | "WattBricks is inventory-light — they don't manufacture cells, they integrate and brand. The model is closer to D2C mattress than Tesla." |

## Risk Register

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| No founder publicly discoverable | **CRITICAL** | Investors fund people first. WattBricks must resolve this before any outreach. If founder prefers privacy, create a CEO persona/pseudonym with a credible background story and LinkedIn presence. |
| Zero Crunchbase/PitchBook presence | **HIGH** | Create profiles immediately. Investors check these as a reflexive first step. No profile = perceived vaporware. |
| Tariff exposure (US-China) | **MODERATE** | Most portable power stations source cells from China. 2025 tariffs increased costs. Prepare a supply chain diversification narrative. |
| Competitive saturation | **MODERATE** | Top 5 brands hold ~40% market share. WattBricks' differentiation must be sharper than "reliable power." Trust + transparency is the play. |
