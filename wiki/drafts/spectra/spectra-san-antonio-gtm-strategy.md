## 1. Market Overview

**Bexar County / San Antonio, TX**
- Population: ~2.1M (Bexar County), ~1.5M (City of San Antonio)
- Median household income: ~$54,000; 17%+ households below poverty line
- Rental affordability crisis: 50%+ of renters cost-burdened (>30% income on housing)
- Job growth: healthcare, military, logistics, tech services — steady demand signal
- Key employer base: USAA, Valero, CPS Energy, Port San Antonio, Fort Sam Houston

**Affordable Housing Gap**
- Estimated deficit: 35,000+ units at 80% AMI or below
- LIHTC pipeline active but insufficient — financing gap persists
- Opportunity: workforce housing (60%–120% AMI) as bridge product
- CDFI and foundation ecosystem active and receptive to faith-framed investment

## 3. Competitive Positioning

### Differentiation
1. **Faith-framed investment narrative**: Rooted in CDFI ecosystem, Christian foundations, and mission-first capital partners — not generic developer pitch
2. **County-level integrated approach**: Spectra pairs investment with community systems (mercy kitchens, workforce development) — not pure real estate play
3. **MCF structure familiarity**: Kulshan CLT framework provides replicable county-level model with proven community integration

### Positioning Statement
> *Spectra Holdings partners with counties, CDCs, and mission lenders to develop workforce and affordable housing that is financially sustainable and community-integrated — where capital returns are measured alongside community outcomes.*

## 6. Risk Factors & Mitigation

| Risk | Probability | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| LIHTC award not received | Medium | Build multiple gap financing paths; CDFI and foundation alternatives |
| County CDBG allocation insufficient | Medium | Target multiple municipalities; explore adjacent counties (Comal, Guadalupe) |
| Investor capital not committed in timeline | Medium | Stagger project phases; start with smaller pilot unit count (40–60 units) |
| Construction cost escalation | Medium | Get GC quotes at sketch design; use cost-plus vs. fixed price structure |
| Market absorption risk | Low | Target near major employers and transit corridors; studio + 1BR focus |
