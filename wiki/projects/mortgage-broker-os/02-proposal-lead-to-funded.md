## Proposal A: Lead-to-Funded Automation System

**What it is:** A full-stack automation system that replaces the lead gen → no follow-up cycle with a lead gen → nurture → conversion pipeline.

**What it does:** Captures intent signals, auto-differentiates outreach, runs ghost-busting sequences, scores and triages leads, and manages the loan pipeline from lead → funded.

**What it replaces:**
- Lead gen tool (aggregator, Zillow, realtor.com)
- CRM (generic, doesn't map to mortgage)
- Follow-up sequences (manual, inconsistent)
- Pipeline management (spreadsheets, sticky notes)

### What's Included

**Phase 1 — Signal Capture (Week 1–2)**
- Daily scrape of target county: new listings, price drops, courthouse filings
- Enrichment: phone, email, LinkedIn, competitor data
- CRM entry with intent score, same-day

**Phase 2 — Positioning (Week 2–3)**
- Broker picks micro-niche persona (5 options)
- All outreach auto-personalizes: email subject lines, SMS body, landing page copy
- 35 sequence variants pre-built per niche

**Phase 3 — Sequences (Week 3–4)**
- 21-day ghost-busting sequence: 8 touchpoints, email + SMS
- Stage-triggered — no manual touch until borrower responds
- A/B variants for subject lines and offers

**Phase 4 — Scoring (Week 4–5)**
- Lead scoring model live: 20 weighted signals
- Daily score recalculation + broker morning digest
- Hot lead alerts: SMS + dashboard highlight

**Phase 5 — Pipeline (Week 5–6) — Elite only**
- Loan stage tracker: Lead → Pre-qual → App → UW → CTC → Funded
- Automated checklists per stage
- Stage transition triggers: document requests, deadline reminders
