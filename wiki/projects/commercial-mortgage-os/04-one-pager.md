## The Problem

You're managing $10M–$50M+ in commercial loan originations.
Your deals take 90–180 days to close.
Your pipeline system is a spreadsheet + Outlook.

Here's what's actually happening:

**1 deal in 4 stalls in underwriting.** Not because the deal was bad — because nobody was watching the UW conditions. Missing appraisal. Expired rate lock. Stale rent roll. One stalled deal = **$50K–$300K in lost origination fees.**

**Your best origination opportunity is already in your pipeline.** Loans you financed 3–5 years ago are hitting their maturity windows. Your borrowers don't know when their loans mature. Your competitors do — and they're calling first.

**CMBS loans in special servicing are flying under your radar.** Loans transferred to special servicers are the highest-probability origination opportunities in commercial real estate. But monitoring 5+ servicers manually isn't sustainable.

## What We Built

**Klick2Client OS for Commercial Mortgage Brokers** — a pipeline intelligence system that:

✅ Tracks every deal from pitch → proposal → application → underwriting → commitment → closing
✅ Alerts you 10 days before rate locks expire and UW conditions go overdue
✅ Monitors maturity walls on agency, CMBS, and life company loans — 12–18 month window
✅ Scrapes CMBS special servicer pools weekly — new transfer alerts same day
✅ Runs automated check-in sequences on every active deal — nothing stalls
