## Stage-Specific Check-In Sequences

### Application Stage — Borrower Check-In (Day 0, 7, 14)

| Day | Channel | Message |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 0 | Email | "Here are the documents we need to complete your application" — with checklist link |
| 3 | SMS | "Quick check — did you get our doc request? Happy to walk through what's needed." |
| 7 | Email | "Friendly follow-up on docs needed — here's what's outstanding" |
| 14 | SMS | "Just checking in — where are you on the rent roll and operating statements?" |

### Underwriting Stage — Borrower + Lender (Day 0, 14, 30)

| Day | Channel | Target | Message |
|-----|---------|--------|---------|
| 0 | Email | Borrower | "UW has started — here are the conditions we're tracking" |
| 7 | SMS | Borrower | "Any questions on the UW conditions? We're here to help." |
| 14 | Email | Lender | "Update on UW conditions — [X] cleared, [Y] in progress" |
| 21 | SMS | Borrower | "Appraisal back yet? Want to make sure we're on track." |
| 30 | Email | Borrower + Lender | "UW status update — [summary of cleared vs. pending conditions]" |

### Commitment Stage — Rate Lock Monitoring (Day -10, -5, -1)

| Day | Channel | Message |
|-----|---------|---------|
| -10 | Email | "Your rate lock expires in 10 days — here's the status" |
| -5 | SMS | "Rate lock expires [DATE] — do we need to extend? Let me know today." |
| -1 | Email | "Last chance — rate lock expires tomorrow. Call me if you need to act." |
