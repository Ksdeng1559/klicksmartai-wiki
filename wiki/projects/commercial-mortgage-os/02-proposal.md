## The Opportunity

Commercial mortgage brokers are leaving **$100K–$500K per year** in origination fees on the table — not because they can't source deals, but because their pipeline system doesn't match the complexity of their deals.

**The math:**
- Independent commercial broker, $30M/year in originations
- Average deal: $5M at 1% origination = $50K fee
- 10 active deals per year, 1–2 stall in underwriting = $50K–$100K in lost fees
- Maturity wall: 5 loans in broker's borrower network maturing in next 12 months
- Capturing 2 refinancing deals = $100K in new origination fees

**Total annual value at risk from pipeline failures: $150K–$200K+**

Klick2Client OS for commercial mortgage brokers solves this.

## Features

### Pipeline Tracking
- Deal tracker: Pitch → Proposal → Application → UW → Commitment → Closing → Post-Close
- Stage-specific document checklists (appraisal, environmental, rent roll, entity docs)
- Automated reminders at every stage gate and deadline
- Real-time pipeline dashboard — no more spreadsheets

### Deal Stalling Prevention
- Missing docs alert → borrower + guarantor notified automatically
- UW condition overdue → lender alert + escalation to broker
- Rate lock expiring → borrower + broker notification 10 days out
- Legal review stalled → internal alert + follow-up trigger
- Stalled deal = automated recovery sequence

### Maturity Wall Intelligence
- Track loans in broker's borrower network (not just own originations)
- Scan agency (Fannie/Freddie), CMBS, life company, bridge maturity windows
- 14-month alert window for agency loans, 12-month for CMBS, 18-month for life company
- Pre-loaded outreach template at each alert trigger
- One captured refinancing window = $50K–$250K in new origination

### CMBS Special Servicer Monitor
- Track loans transferred to special servicing across 5+ servicers
- Weekly scan → new opportunity alert → outreach sequence
- Property address, loan amount, servicer contact → deal entry
- Borrower needs new capital → broker provides solution → new deal origination

### Check-In Sequences
- 7 stage-specific email/SMS sequences for active deals
- Borrower check-in at application, UW, commitment, and closing stages
- Lender check-in at UW conditions and commitment stages
- No deal goes cold — automated touchpoints keep momentum
