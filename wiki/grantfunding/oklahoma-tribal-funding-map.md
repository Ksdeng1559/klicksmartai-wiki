# Oklahoma Tribal Funding Map

Oklahoma is treated as a scalable tribal development ecosystem for housing, energy, infrastructure, workforce development, and environmental recovery.

## Strategic Themes

- tribal housing
- energy resilience
- infrastructure modernization
- workforce development
- economic development
- environmental recovery

- federal and tribal partnership pathways
