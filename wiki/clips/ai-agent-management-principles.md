## Core Thesis

> "Running AI agents is a management skill."

The best AI PMs in 2026 won't be the best prompters — they'll be the best agent managers.

---

## Three Management Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Over-Specifying
Detailed step-by-step instructions produce rigid, limited output.
**Instead:** Use principles, not procedures.
- "Only surface tools developers can use today"
- "Verify every claim"
- "Skip corporate news"

> "Tell them what good looks like, not how to get there."

### Mistake 2: Stepping in Too Early
Those "bad outputs" in the first two weeks are the most valuable data you'll collect.

> "If the reader can't do something with this today, skip it."

Single rule that turned 47 flagged stories → 7 worth reading.

### Mistake 3: Treating All Agents the Same

| Agent | Needs | Constraints Kill Output If... |
|-------|-------|-------------------------------|
| Research | Tight constraints | No speculation allowed |
| Content | Creative latitude | Rigid formatting limits energy |

## Feedback Loop

```
Identify Pattern → Give Feedback → Agent Updates Own Files → Next Session Fix Loaded
```

**One correction to one agent propagates to the rest.** That's not prompting. That's management.

---

## Compounding Curve

| Timeline | State |
|----------|-------|
| Day 1 | Fixing everything (net negative) |
| Day 10 | Fixing edge cases |
| Day 30 | 90% ready to ship |
| Day 50 | Strategy time, not corrections |

## Three Stages of AI Maturity

| Stage | What | Output |
|-------|------|--------|
| 1 | Agents as tools (one-off tasks) | Weekly shipping |
| 2 | Managing agents (personality files, shared memory, cron, feedback loops) | Daily shipping |
| 3 | Agents managing agents (self-reviews, self-restarts) | Management moves up |

---

## Key Quote

> "At every stage, the constraint is the same. Not the model. The management layer around it."
