🎯 What Bright Data Is Doing in Your System

Bright Data is your TOP-OF-FUNNEL FILTER.

Its job is to answer one question before you spend money on Tavily or outreach:

“Is this business likely to take (and benefit from) a sales conversation right now?”

If the answer is “no,” the lead dies immediately.

🧠 Signal Categories You Are Actively Looking For
1️⃣ Client Sentiment Signals (Reviews = Sales Readiness)
Sources

Google Reviews

Yelp / industry platforms (optional)

Local directories

Signals You Care About
✅ POSITIVE (Opportunity Signals)

⭐ 4.2–4.8 average rating

Frequent mentions of:

“great service”

“responsive”

“professional”

High volume of recent reviews (last 6–12 months)

Why this matters

Good operators buy growth

They answer phones

They show up to appointments

They close deals

These are prime appointment candidates.

⚠️ MIXED (High-Leverage Signals)

⭐ 3.2–4.1 rating

Complaints like:

“hard to get ahold of”

“slow response”

“missed follow-up”

Owner replies to some reviews but not all

Why this matters

These businesses know something is broken

They are open to systems conversations

Messaging angle = fix the leak

This is gold for your offer.

❌ NEGATIVE (Disqualifiers)

⭐ <3.0 rating

Angry, emotional, hostile reviews

Owner arguing publicly

Repeated service failures

Why we discard

Low show rates

Blame external vendors

Pricing disputes

Reputation damage to your brand

👉 These NEVER enter Tavily or outreach.

2️⃣ Growth Signals (Expansion = Budget)

These signals tell you money + intent exist.

What You Look For

Review phrases like:

“new location”

“recently expanded”

“now serving”

Website changes detected by crawl:

New service pages

New locations

Increased review velocity month-over-month

Why This Matters

Growing companies:

Need leads

Need systems

Have urgency

They buy appointments, not “ideas”

3️⃣ Hiring Signals (Pain + Capacity)

Hiring is one of the strongest buying signals.

Signals

Job postings:

Sales

Admin

Customer service

Phrases in reviews:

“short-staffed”

“busy”

“long wait times”

Interpretation

Hiring =

“We have demand but lack process.”

That’s a perfect setup for:

Automation

Appointment systems

Lead qualification

4️⃣ Operational Friction Signals (Your Best Hook)

These are the exact phrases you want Bright Data to surface:

“Didn’t call back”

“Hard to reach”

“Left voicemail”

“No response”

“Booked but no follow-up”

Why These Are Critical

These allow you to:

Justify outreach

Personalize messaging

Defend appointment pricing

This becomes your proof-based opener:

“We noticed multiple reviews mentioning missed callbacks…”

5️⃣ Owner Engagement Signals (Buyer Psychology)
Signals

Owner replies to reviews

Owner updates Google Business profile

Owner responds calmly to criticism

Why This Matters

These owners:

Take responsibility

Care about reputation

Are rational buyers

Respect systems

They show up to calls.

🚦 Scoring Logic (How Bright Data Feeds the Gate)
Example Qualification Rules

Only pass leads that meet at least 2 of 3:

Sentiment score ≥ threshold

Growth or hiring signal detected

Operational friction present

Everything else is discarded early.

This is how you protect:

Margin

Reputation

Client trust

🧠 Why Bright Data Comes BEFORE Tavily (Reconfirmed)
Tool Role
Bright Data Should we even talk to them?
Tavily Who exactly should we talk to?

If Bright Data says “no,”
Tavily never runs.

🧠 Internal Rule (Lock This In)

Bright Data decides IF.
Tavily decides WHO.
GoHighLevel proves WHEN and WHY.

✅ Final Summary (What You’re Using Bright Data For)

You are using Bright Data to detect:

Sales readiness

Growth intent

Budget presence

Operational pain

Buyer psychology

Not just “sentiment.”

This is why your appointment pricing is defensible.
