# Dubb Case Study — Elite Insurance of Merrillville

**Source:** dubb.com/case-studies/elite-insurance-of-merrillville
**Date:** 2026 (Dubb website)
**Subject:** Elite Insurance of Merrillville — life insurance agency

---

## Challenge

- Sean Grant, CEO of Elite Insurance of Merrillville — 20+ years as a top advisor
- Life insurance industry: vast pool of professionals offering similar services
- Problem: how to stand out in a crowded, commoditized market
- Needed to provide clients with a personalized, unparalleled experience

---

## Solution

Sean incorporated Dubb to create **personalized video messages for each client**:
- Captured attention and left lasting impressions
- Used Dubb's video creation features to connect despite virtual communication
- Built deeper relationships and fostered greater trust
- Accessed daily live training sessions for platform guidance

---

## Results

- Clients expressed appreciation for the personalized, thoughtful approach
- Sean positioned himself as a **leading figure** in a competitive market
- Dubb became an **indispensable tool** in his professional arsenal
- Ongoing Dubb support (daily live training sessions) was invaluable
