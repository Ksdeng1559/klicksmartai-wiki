# Bexar County, TX — Census & Housing Brief

> *"The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few."* — Matthew 9:37

San Antonio is the **7th largest city in the United States** — and one of its most housing-burdened. For investors and foundation partners seeking measurable, God-honoring impact, Bexar County presents a large-scale opportunity to deploy capital that transforms communities, stabilizes families, and breaks cycles of poverty through the dignity of a safe, affordable home.

### The "Missing Middle" Gap

San Antonio's housing market has a structural hole between single-family detached homes and large apartment complexes. Proposed city target: 2-to-20 unit multi-family projects. Challenge: smaller projects lack economies of scale, face financing gaps. Urban core is **losing families** to surrounding counties.

## 9. MCF Intervention Thesis

**Why Bexar County is an MCF pilot target:**

1. **115,125-unit structural deficit** — not a cyclical dip; the market has underbuilt for a decade
2. **Missing middle is the gap** — 2-to-20 unit projects are hardest to finance; CDFI gap financing unlocks them
3. **Eviction is concentrated** — 30 properties drive 12% of filings; targeted intervention moves the needle
4. **Homeless response system works** — unsheltered down 25% in 5 years; proven model, needs capital to scale
5. **Faith-aligned impact** — 64.6% Hispanic, elevated poverty, large Catholic/Protestant community with deep CD roots

**Product-market fit for MCF:**
- Missing middle development (highest priority)
- NOAH preservation (acquisition-rehab of at-risk stock)
- Anti-eviction intervention (prevention grants, tenant legal defense)
- Rapid rehousing scale-up (proven ROI — $8.3M rental assistance prevented 2,758 evictions in one year)
