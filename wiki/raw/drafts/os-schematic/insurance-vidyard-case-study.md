---

## Key Intelligence

### The Challenge
- Crowded insurance marketplace — hard to stand out
- Insurance is traditionally a "numbers game" (cold calling, spray-and-pray)
- Trust is everything — clients need to believe you're real before they'll engage
- Digital transformation still in early stages in the industry (96% of financial services pros want to work from home permanently)

### What Worked — Derek Hayden's Video Selling Playbook

1. **User-generated social video** — Derek's Tuesday Tips (Facebook, selfie-style, humor + value)
   - Quality wasn't great but it worked for traction
   - Helped a young producer stand out in a crowded market

2. **Personalized video introductions** — sent to every new contact (underwriters, claims pros, risk control agents)
   - "I want people to understand I'm a real person behind the name in the email"
   - Shows research done, not just a name on a checklist

3. **Screen share video prospecting via LinkedIn** — shows prospect's own website in the video
   - Uses Vidyard Chrome extension
   - LinkedIn direct video message with eye-catching thumbnail

4. **Video proposals for quotes** — present coverage/policy via video instead of PDF
   - Can re-record if explanation wasn't clear
   - Client watches on-demand, multiple times, refers back

5. **Video analytics for follow-up decisions**
   - Who watched, how many times, how long
   - Shared internally = warm referral signal
   - Non-viewed = try a different approach

### Video Selling Pitch (Derek's words)
> "It's easy for someone to hang up the phone in a cold call, but a personalized video is a non-intrusive way to get in front of someone."

### Industry Context
- Virtual selling still new in financial services but quickly becoming the preference
- 96% of financial services professionals prefer permanent remote work
- Industry built on trust = secure video sharing is high priority
- Insurance is relationship-driven — human connection before the first call is critical
