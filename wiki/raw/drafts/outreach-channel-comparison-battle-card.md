# Outreach Channel Comparison
### The Data-Driven Case for Multi-Channel Sales Outreach

---

## The Problem with Single-Channel Outreach

- Cold email response rate: **5.1%** (down from ~7% in 2023)
- 75% of cold emails are opened in the first hour — but most get **no response**
- **50% of sales go to the first vendor to respond** (Kondo 2025)
- Most deals require **5–12 touchpoints** across multiple channels
- Text-only outreach is being ignored. The channel is saturated.

---

## Channel Performance — At a Glance

| Channel | Response Rate | CTR / Open Rate | B2B Conversion | Best Use |
|---------|:------------:|:---------------:|:-------------:|---------|
| Cold email (text only) | 5% | 27% open | 1–5% | Initial cold contact |
| LinkedIn InMail (text) | 3–8% | 20–30% open | 1–3% | C-suite targeting |
| **Email + Video** | **25–30%** | **3x replies** | **3x vs text** | Proposal walkthroughs |

| **MMS (+ picture/video)** | **Similar to SMS** | **+300% engagement** | **Higher than SMS** | Visual demos |

## The Numbers That Matter

### Email + Video
- Sales emails with video: **3x more replies** than text-only (Vidyard)
- Video reply rates: **25–30%** vs 1–5% for text-only (Sendspark 2025)
- AI-personalized video: **15–20%** reply rate (Sendspark)
- After adding video to email: **+92% reply rate** (ReviewTrackers / JB Sales Group)
- Sales team using video: **60%+ report increased response rates** (Vidyard)
- Response time cut in half: **Kinsta case study** (Dubb) — week one

### SMS
- SMS open rate: **98%** (Validity / Gartner)
- Read within 3 minutes: **90%** (Validity)
- SMS response rate: **45%** vs email's **6%** — 7.5x better (SimpleTexting 2025)
- SMS CTR: **10.66%** average (Sender)
- B2B SMS conversion: **21–40%** (RevOps.ai 2024–2025)
- Automated SMS flows: **30x more revenue** per recipient vs one-time sends (Klaviyo 2024)
- First-touch speed: **50% of deals go to the first vendor to respond** (Kondo 2025)

### MMS
- MMS vs SMS engagement: **+300%** (MMS Marketing Stats 2025)
- 67% of insurance agents use MMS for quotes and claims (Zendesk 2024)
- Best for: property images, site tours, proposal previews, visual storytelling

### LinkedIn + Video
- LinkedIn + video InMail: **+40% response** vs text InMail (Business Insider)
- LinkedIn response rate (text): 3–8%
- LinkedIn InMail outperforms cold email by **400–500%** in engagement
