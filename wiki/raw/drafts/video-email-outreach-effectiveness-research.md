## Why Video Works

1. **Attention:** Video grabs focus in a text-saturated inbox
2. **Trust:** Shows face and voice — humanizes the sender
3. **Recall:** Messages with video are remembered longer
4. **Differentiation:** Most reps still don't use video — you stand out
5. **Emotional connection:** Creates relationship before the first call
