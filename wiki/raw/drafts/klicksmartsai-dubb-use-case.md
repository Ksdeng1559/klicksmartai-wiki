# Use Case: Video-First Outreach with Dubb

**Industry:** B2B Sales / Cloud Platform
**Company:** Kinsta (WordPress, application, and database hosting)
**Sales Lead:** Rachel Steinbauer, Director of Sales Operations

---

## The Problem

- Kinsta had no outbound sales team
- Industry was heavily inbound — outbound had never worked
- Cold emails and calls got minimal response
- Needed to cut through the noise and reach potential customers

---

## The Solution — Dubb Video Campaigns

Rachel created personalized video campaigns:
- Walking prospects through proposals
- Giving tours of the product
- Showcasing what Kinsta offers

**Key insight:** Video let her show personality and passion — making her relatable and trustworthy in a way text never could.

---

## The Results

| Metric | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Average response time | **Cut in half** after week one |
| Prospect engagement | Reached previously unresponsive buyers |
| Sales process | More efficient and effective |
| Lead quality | Better qualified opportunities |
| Revenue growth | Predictable, scalable outbound pipeline |

---

## The Pitch

> *"Dubb changed everything for us at Kinsta by providing outbound sales tools. Using video helped us increase engagement rates, responses, and closing rates. Dubb also made it easier for people to understand our services without ever needing to hop on a phone or video call."*
> — Rachel Steinbauer, Director of Sales Operations, Kinsta

---

## Why This Matters for KlickSmartAI OS

Dubb is integrated into the **KlickSmartAI outbound stack** as the video communication layer:

- **Layer 3 (Conversion):** Elevates text/email outreach into personalized video
- **Layer 4 (Engagement):** Two-way video + text follow-up sequences
- **OpenClaw integration:** Dubb Chrome Extension + LinkedIn integration = video-first prospecting

### For GPC Development (example):

Instead of:
> "Hi, I'd like to discuss your development project. Here's our brochure."

GPC could send:
> A 90-second video walking through their approach to a specific project type, referencing the prospect's recent acquisition or development news.

**Result:** Higher reply rate, warmer first conversation, less back-and-forth.
