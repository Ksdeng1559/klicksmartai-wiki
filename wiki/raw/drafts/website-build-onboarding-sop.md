# New Website Build — Client Onboarding SOP

> Standard operating procedure for onboarding new website clients at KlickSmartAI.

---

## Stage 1: Discovery Call

**Objective:** Fill out the **Client Assets** questionnaire (Discovery, Plan, Process).

### Discovery Questions (Client Assets — Section I)

| # | Question | What We Learn |
|---|----------|---------------|
| D1 | Tell us about your company, core services, and industry experience | Business context, positioning |
| D2 | Who is your Ideal Client? | ICP for messaging + design direction |
| D3 | What types of projects do you want to work on? | Service scope |
| D4 | What experience level do you have with website projects? | Manage expectations |
| D5 | Show us 3-5 websites you like or dislike — why? | Design reference points |
| D6 | What is the single most important goal for this website? | Primary KPI / CTA |
| D7 | Who is the perfect visitor and what problem are we solving? | Audience + value prop |
| D8 | What current projects are you working on? | Context, cross-sell opportunity |
| D9 | List 3-5 websites to build upon | Design direction |

### Plan Questions (Client Assets — Section II)

| # | Question | What We Learn |
|---|----------|---------------|
| P1 | What are the minimum pages required? | Site architecture |
| P2 | What are 3-5 powerful sentences about why to choose you? | Core messaging |
| P3 | What is the one thing every visitor should do? | Primary CTA |
| P4 | Provide a basic site map | Navigation structure |

### Process Questions (Client Assets — Section III)

| # | Question | What We Learn |
|---|----------|---------------|
| PR1 | Which pages are in scope for the initial wireframe? | Phase 1 scope |
| PR2 | What is the target date for the first blueprint draft? | Timeline |
| PR3 | Who has final authority to approve the blueprint? | Decision-maker |

### Discovery Call Checklist

- [ ] Book 30-min Zoom or call
- [ ] Send **Client Assets** questionnaire in advance
- [ ] Take notes during call — fill in all D1–PR3 fields
- [ ] Confirm deposit amount and payment method
- [ ] Identify decision-maker and approval workflow
- [ ] Get reference sites explained (what they like/dislike and why)
- [ ] Note any brand assets received (logos, colours, fonts, copy)

**Output:** Completed Client Assets form → saved to wiki as `entities/[client]-onboarding.md`

**Objective:** Gather all assets from client before design begins.

|-------|--------|-------|

- [ ] Services / Products — all service names + descriptions + images
- [ ] Contact — address, phone, email, form preferences
- [ ] [Additional pages] —

## Stage 4: PRD & Wireframe

**Objective:** Translate client assets into a Project Requirements Document + wireframe.

### PRD Sections

1. **Brand Identity** — name, tagline, colours, fonts, tone
2. **Services / Products** — full list with descriptions
3. **Core Pages** — confirmed page list
4. **Site Map** — hierarchy agreed with client
5. **Core Messaging** — 3–5 value proposition statements
6. **Primary CTA** — the one action every visitor takes
7. **Target Audience** — ICP description
8. **Design Direction** — reference sites + what to borrow from each
9. **Open Items** — outstanding decisions (domain, logo, etc.)

### Wireframe

- Blueprint each Phase 1 page (defer Phase 2 pages)
- Use Figma or Canva for initial layout
- Present via Zoom with screen share
- Collect feedback, revise

**Output:** PRD doc in wiki + Google Drive. Wireframe shared for review.

---

## Stage 5: Design & Build

**Objective:** Build the live site based on approved wireframe + PRD.

### Build Stages

1. **Design mockup** — 2 rounds of revisions included
2. **Development** — responsive build, CMS setup
3. **QA testing** — mobile, browser, speed check
4. **Client review** — 1 round of revisions
5. **Launch** — DNS, hosting, go-live

### Revision Policy

> 2 rounds of revisions included in base scope.
> Additional revisions billed at hourly rate.

### Launch Checklist

- [ ] Final content approved by client
- [ ] All forms tested
- [ ] Mobile responsive verified
- [ ] SEO basics (meta titles, descriptions, alt text)
- [ ] Analytics installed
- [ ] DNS pointed
- [ ] SSL confirmed
- [ ] Sitemap + robots.txt
- [ ] Site submitted to Google Search Console

**Output:** Live website, handed over to client.

## Client Assets Questionnaire

> Send this to every new client before the discovery call.

---

**Section I — Discovery**

1. **About Your Company:** Tell us about your organization, core services/products, and industry experience.
2. **Ideal Client:** Who is your Ideal Client?
3. **Project Types:** What types of projects do you want to work on?
4. **Experience Level:** What is your experience level with website projects?
5. **References:** Share 3-5 existing websites you like or dislike — explain your reasons for each.
6. **Build-Upon Sites:** List a few existing websites you would like this project to build upon.
7. **Primary Goal:** What is the single most important objective this website must achieve? (e.g., more leads, sign-ups, authority)
8. **Target Visitor:** Who is the perfect person visiting this site, and what problem are we solving for them?
9. **Current Projects:** List any current projects you're working on.

**Section II — The Plan**

10. **Required Pages:** List the minimum pages needed.
11. **Core Messaging:** What are 3-5 powerful sentences about why visitors should choose you?
12. **Primary CTA:** What is the single most important action every visitor should take?
13. **Site Map:** Provide a basic outline of the main sections.

**Section III — The Process**

14. **Scope:** Which pages are in the initial wireframe, which are for later?
15. **Timeline:** What is the target date for the first blueprint draft?
16. **Final Authority:** Who has the final sign-off authority to approve the blueprint?
