### Slide 3 — Friction Matrix: Policy & Market Results
Regulatory friction has broken scalability of Canadian energy infrastructure.

| Policy Hurdle | Unintended Consequence | Market Result |
|---|---|---|
| Bill C-69 (Impact Assessment) | Approvals slowed to 1 project in 5 years vs. 17 previously | Capital flight + investor uncertainty |
| Bill C-48 & Bill C-5 (Tanker bans) | Political favoritism, limited export routing | Severe price discounts, US monopsony reliance |
