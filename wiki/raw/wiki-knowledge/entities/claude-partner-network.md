# Claude Partner Network

**The Claude Partner Network** is Anthropic's formal partner program for organizations helping enterprises adopt Claude. Launched March 12, 2026 with a **$100M initial commitment** for 2026.

## Overview

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Announced** | March 12, 2026 |
| **Investment** | $100M initial (2026), expect more over time |
| **Membership** | Free |
| **Applications** | Open now at claude.com/partners |
| **Head** | Steve Corfield, Head of Global Business Development and Partnerships, Anthropic |

## What Partners Get

### Financial Support
- Direct support for **training and sales enablement**
- **Market development** funds (customer deployment support, co-marketing campaigns and events)
- Eligible for **investment** from Anthropic

### Team Scaling
- Partner-facing team **5x larger** — dedicated Applied AI engineers on live customer deals, technical architects for complex implementations, localized GTM support internationally

### Access
- **Partner Portal** — Anthropic Academy training materials, Anthropic's own sales playbooks, co-marketing documentation
- **Services Partner Directory** — listed where enterprise buyers find firms with Claude implementation experience
- **Priority access** to new certifications as they roll out
