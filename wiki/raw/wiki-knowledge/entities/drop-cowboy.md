# Drop Cowboy

**What it is:** Ringless voicemail + multi-channel outreach platform
**URL:** https://www.dropcowboy.com/

## Core Services

### Ringless Voicemail Drop
- Direct-to-voicemail delivery (no ring, no call)
- AI Voice Cloning for personalization at scale
- Bulk SMS follow-up

### Multi-Channel
- Ringless Voicemail + SMS/MMS + Unified Inbox
- Two-way text messaging campaigns

### CRM & Automation
- Contact Management (centralized CRM, custom fields)
- Marketing Automation (data sync, triggers, workflows)
- Visual Workflow Builder for campaign sequences

### Compliance Tools
- TCPA Compliance built-in
- DNC Scrubbing (automatic Do Not Call list scrubbing)
- Quiet Hours & Smart Scheduling (time-zone aware)
- Suppression List Management

### Integrations
- Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho + 100+ more
- REST API + Webhooks
- Zapier (2,000+ app connections)
- BYOC: Bring Your Own Carrier (connect Twilio, save 40-60%)

## KlickSmartAI Use Case

Financial advisors and wealth managers are explicitly listed as an industry vertical:
- "Financial Advisors — Client communication & portfolio updates"

This aligns with:
- **WealthWireRadar** subscriber outreach (Wayne Stone + future HNW advisors)
- **GPC Development** outreach to prospect advisers
- **KlickSmartAI GTM** — cold outreach cadence for new client acquisition

Ringless voicemail bypasses the gatekeeper problem for advisor outreach. A sequence: dropcowboy voicemail → SMS follow-up → CRM entry is a proven Wealth Management marketing stack.

## Competitive Alternative
Voice mail drops without ringing = legal cold calling for financial services without TCPA exposure on the voicemail side (still need DNC scrubbing).
