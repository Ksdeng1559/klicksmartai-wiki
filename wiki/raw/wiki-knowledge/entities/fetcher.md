# Fetcher

**"Your AI recruiter — automating passive and active sourcing."**

Fetcher is an AI-powered recruiting platform that automates candidate sourcing for talent teams. Covers both inbound (applicants) and outbound (proactive outreach) recruiting with a credit-based system. Human sourcing services available via the "Amplify" plan.

## Products & Features

### Core Platform — AI Sourcing

- **AI Candidate Search** — search across databases for passive candidates matching criteria
- **Outbound Outreach** — automated outreach sequences to candidates
- **Inbound Management** — applicant intake and management
- **CRM & Integrations** — ATS/CRM integrations included in paid plans

### Amplify Plan — Human Sourcing Services

For periods of high demand, Fetcher's human recruiters curate candidates for you. Designed to rapidly scale hiring volume while keeping costs low.

### Credit System

Each plan includes a set of credits for both inbound and outbound recruiting. Credit allocation is tailored to your strategy — if you lean more heavily one direction, credits are adjusted accordingly.

- Credits **roll over** month-to-month as long as you maintain your seat
- Additional credits purchasable in **1,000 increments** at competitive cost
- No long-term commitment required — scale up during high-demand periods, scale back down

## Competitive Position

Fetcher is a **direct competitor to HUBERT-X** — both are AI recruiting platforms targeting the same problem (automating candidate sourcing for recruiters). HUBERT-X differentiates as a self-hosted, OSS-first, locally-run AI agent vs Fetcher's SaaS platform.
