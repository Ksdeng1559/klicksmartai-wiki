# Vidyard

**What it is:** AI-powered video selling platform for revenue teams — personalized video messages, AI avatars, video hosting, and video automation at scale.

## Feature Table

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Video Messages | Personalized asynchronous video to break through busy inboxes |
| Video Hosting | Branded sharing page, analytics, QBR/onboarding library |
| AI Avatars | Hyper-realistic AI avatars (stock + custom) for scalable video at scale |
| Video Agent | AI automatically creates + sends personalized video based on buyer actions |
| CRM/MAP Integrations | Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Salesloft, Zapier |
| Analytics | Viewer engagement, pipeline attribution, team performance |
| CTA Templates | Banners, email capture, calendar booking embedded in video |
| Custom Backgrounds | Green screen / branded backgrounds |
| Branded Sharing Page | Standard or Advanced (custom HTML/CSS/JS, SSO, custom domains) |

## Use Cases

1. **Sales outreach** — Video messages embedded in cold email/LinkedIn sequences for higher reply rates
2. **Deal progression** — Personalized video follow-ups at key lifecycle stages
3. **Client onboarding** — Branded video hub for new client onboarding flows
4. **QBRs + Renewals** — Hosted video presentations with viewer analytics
5. **ABM targeting** — Personalized AI avatar video triggered by intent signals
