# 🎨 AI Marketing Artifact Operations System — Video, Image & Web

> Mirrored from Notion page `3a79e94cf0a48146a63fed4f93b932eb` on 2026-09-07. Source: https://app.notion.com/p/3a79e94cf0a48146a63fed4f93b932eb?pvs=204

# Purpose
Build a repeatable operating system that turns business knowledge, campaign strategy, brand rules, research, and approved messaging into **video, image, and web marketing artifacts**.
The source playbook’s core idea is that a company should stop acting as the manual integration between disconnected tools. Instead, work should be consolidated into core hubs, external tools should be connected, and agents should operate from structured context. This document applies that same model to creative marketing production. fileciteturn5file0
> **Transformation in one line**
>
> Rebuilding briefs, searching for assets, restating brand rules, and manually coordinating production → an agent-supported system that creates, reviews, stores, and reuses marketing artifacts.
---
# The Creative Operations Loop
## 1. Build the core creative hubs
Give every campaign and artifact one operational home.
## 2. Connect the external tools
Bring research, assets, customer signals, analytics, CRM context, design tools, and publishing systems into the workspace.
## 3. Deploy artifact agents
Use agents to create briefs, generate artifacts, perform QA, repurpose content, and prepare publishing packages.
**Every completed artifact strengthens the next one** because approved prompts, assets, brand decisions, templates, and performance data become reusable context.

# The Five Core Hubs
The original playbook recommends Tasks, CRM, Notes, and Company Knowledge as the minimum operational foundation. For marketing artifact production, use those ideas in five specialized hubs. fileciteturn5file0
## 1. Campaigns & Projects
One database for every campaign, client initiative, launch, funnel, content cluster, and creative sprint.
**Must-have properties**
- Campaign name
- Brand or tenant
- Business objective
- Audience
- Offer
- Funnel stage
- Status
- Owner
- Priority
- Start date
- Due date
- Primary CTA
- Channel
- Related artifact briefs
- Related source material
**What agents do with it**
- Create production tasks
- Generate campaign briefs
- Recommend required artifacts
- Compile daily or weekly production briefs
- Flag blocked work
- Link performance results back to the campaign
## 2. Artifact Briefs
One structured database for every video, image, web page, deck, email, carousel, calculator interface, report, or motion graphic.
**Must-have properties**
- Artifact title
- Artifact type
- Campaign
- Target audience
- Goal
- Core message
- CTA
- Channel
- Format or dimensions
- Duration or page length
- Brand system
- Source evidence
- Required disclosures
- Status
- Owner
- Due date
- Approval status
- Final asset URL
- Performance metrics
**What agents do with it**
- Turn campaign goals into production-ready briefs
- Generate scripts, storyboards, layouts, and wireframes
- Create channel-specific variations
- Prepare review checklists
- Produce handoff packages for Codex, Open Design, OpenMontage, Canva, or publishing tools
## 3. Brand & Design Systems
Store the reusable brand contract for each company, campaign, or white-label tenant.
**Must-have properties**
- Brand name
- Positioning
- Audience
- Voice and tone
- Color system
- Typography
- Logo and asset library
- Image direction
- Motion direction
- UI component rules
- CTA language
- Compliance language
- Approved examples
- Prohibited styles
- Last reviewed
- Verified
**Recommended implementation**
Maintain a `DESIGN.md` or equivalent brand contract for every major brand. Open Design can use this as the design-system layer for web prototypes, motion frames, images, decks, and branded artifacts.
## 4. Source Intelligence & Content Library
This is the evidence and messaging layer agents use before creating anything.
**Store**
- SEO and SERP research
- Customer interviews
- CRM insights
- FAQs and objections
- Case studies
- Product facts
- Lender or financing rules
- Competitive research
- Approved claims
- Statistics and citations
- Existing articles
- Meeting notes
- Testimonials
- Previous high-performing content
**What agents do with it**
- Answer “what evidence supports this?”
- Extract campaign angles
- Identify objections and hooks
- Produce source-grounded scripts and landing-page copy
- Prevent unsupported claims
## 5. Asset & Template Library
Store everything that can be reused.
**Include**
- Logos
- Fonts and brand references
- Photos
- B-roll
- Icons
- Illustrations
- Charts
- Motion templates
- Landing-page sections
- React or Astro components
- Email modules
- Presentation templates
- Voice profiles
- Music references
- Disclosure blocks
- CTA components
- Approved prompt templates

# Artifact Types
## Video Marketing Artifacts
Use the system to create:
- Short-form educational videos
- Mortgage or financing explainers
- Founder-led videos
- AI-avatar videos
- Social ads
- Case-study videos
- Product demos
- Webinar clips
- Investor update videos
- Animated financial charts
- Motion graphics
- Personalized outreach videos
- YouTube long-form videos
- Shorts, Reels, and TikTok adaptations
### Standard video workflow
1. Select campaign and audience.
2. Pull approved research and claims.
3. Generate the creative brief.
4. Produce hook options.
5. Draft the script.
6. Break the script into scenes.
7. Create storyboard and shot list.
8. Generate or select visual assets.
9. Create motion frames and branded graphics.
10. Add voiceover, captions, music, and transitions.
11. Perform brand, factual, and compliance review.
12. Render master version.
13. Generate platform-specific variants.
14. Publish and capture performance data.
### Recommended tool roles
- **Open Design:** visual direction, branded frames, motion graphics, decks, and design-system consistency
- **OpenMontage:** end-to-end video production orchestration
- **Remotion or HyperFrames:** programmatic rendering
- **HeyGen:** avatars and presenter video
- **Kling, Veo, or similar tools:** generated footage
- **ElevenLabs:** voiceover
- **Canva:** fast manual edits and social resizing

2. Pull brand system and approved assets.
3. Select format and dimensions.
4. Define single-message hierarchy.

7. Produce master creative.
8. Create channel adaptations.

10. Store the approved asset and prompt recipe.

## Web Marketing Artifacts
Use the system to create:
- SEO landing pages
- Rank-and-rent sites
- Product or service pages
- Lead-generation funnels
- Comparison pages
- Interactive calculators
- Assessment forms
- Results pages
- Case-study pages
- Local landing pages
- Advisor dashboards
- Client portals
- Audit reports
- Investor dashboards
- Microsites
- Email capture pages
### Standard web workflow
1. Pull the keyword, SERP, offer, audience, and conversion objective.
2. Build the page brief and conversion architecture.
3. Generate wireframe and content hierarchy.
4. Apply the selected `DESIGN.md` brand system.
5. Create a responsive prototype in Open Design.
6. Review visual direction and user flow.
7. Hand the approved artifact to Codex or Claude Code.
8. Convert it into production Astro, React, or Next.js components.
9. Add schema, metadata, accessibility, forms, analytics, and CRM integration.
10. Test mobile usability, performance, and conversion events.
11. Publish and capture rankings, traffic, leads, and conversion data.
### Production boundary
Open Design should create the design artifact and approved front-end direction. Production engineering should remain responsible for:
- Authentication
- Multi-tenancy
- Database logic
- Mortgage calculations
- Payment processing
- CRM workflows
- Accessibility validation
- SEO infrastructure
- Testing
- Analytics
- Security

# Artifact Agents
The source playbook describes every agent as having three parts: **instructions, connections, and triggers**. Use that exact model here. fileciteturn5file0
## 1. Creative Brief Agent
**Instructions**
Turn a campaign objective, audience, offer, research, and brand system into a complete production-ready artifact brief.
**Connections**
Campaigns, CRM, source intelligence, brand systems, asset library, email, and analytics.
**Triggers**
- New campaign created
- New artifact requested
- Agent is mentioned
## 2. Video Production Agent
**Instructions**
Create a source-grounded script, hooks, storyboard, scene plan, visual prompts, asset list, captions, CTA, and rendering handoff.
**Connections**
Open Design, OpenMontage, asset library, brand system, approved sources, voice tools, and rendering tools.
**Triggers**
- Video brief approved
- New article marked for repurposing
- Campaign enters production
## 3. Image Creative Agent
**Instructions**
Generate three visual concepts, select a hierarchy, create the approved image or carousel system, and produce channel adaptations.
**Connections**
Brand system, campaign brief, image tools, Canva, asset library, and social-channel requirements.
**Triggers**
- Image brief approved
- New campaign launched
- New social distribution task created
## 4. Web Artifact Agent
**Instructions**
Turn SEO evidence, campaign messaging, user intent, and brand rules into a responsive wireframe, content hierarchy, conversion flow, and Open Design prototype.
**Connections**
DataForSEO, source intelligence, Open Design, GitHub, Astro or Next.js repositories, CRM, and analytics.
**Triggers**
- Keyword opportunity approved
- Landing-page request created
- New tenant or campaign launched
## 5. Brand QA Agent
**Instructions**
Review artifacts for brand consistency, readability, tone, hierarchy, visual quality, required disclosures, and prohibited claims.
**Connections**
Brand system, approved examples, compliance rules, artifact brief, and generated deliverable.
**Triggers**
- Artifact moves to review
- New variation generated
## 6. Repurposing Agent
**Instructions**
Transform an approved source artifact into a coordinated channel package without changing the core claim or evidence.
**Connections**
Approved artifact, brand system, campaign plan, channel specifications, and asset library.
**Triggers**
- Artifact approved
- Blog article published
- Video master completed
## 7. Performance Learning Agent
**Instructions**
Compare performance by hook, message, format, channel, audience, and CTA. Recommend what to reuse, revise, retire, or test next.
**Connections**
Analytics, ad platforms, SEO data, CRM outcomes, artifact database, and campaign database.
**Triggers**
- Seven days after publication
- Campaign review date
- Performance threshold reached

# Before and After
## Old: Marketing briefs rebuilt manually
A strategist searches old emails, campaign notes, competitor examples, and brand files before every project.
## New: Creative Brief Agent
The agent assembles the context, identifies missing information, and creates a structured production brief.
## Old: One asset created for one channel
A video, page, or graphic is created once and then forgotten.
## New: Repurposing Agent
Every approved master artifact becomes a coordinated web, email, social, deck, image, and video package.
## Old: Brand rules depend on memory
Different tools and contractors interpret the brand differently.
## New: Reusable brand contract
The same `DESIGN.md`, approved examples, tone, components, and disclosure rules guide every artifact.
## Old: Production ends at delivery
Performance data is separated from the creative decisions that produced it.
## New: Compounding creative intelligence
Performance results are linked to the artifact, campaign, message, audience, hook, format, and CTA so the next brief starts smarter.

# Quality Gates
Every artifact must pass the following checks before publishing.
## Source and factual QA
- Claims are supported by approved source material.
- Statistics and quotes are traceable.
- No missing or invented facts.
- Financial or mortgage information has required context.
## Brand QA
- Correct design system used.
- Typography, colors, spacing, and imagery are consistent.
- Tone matches the brand.
- CTA language is approved.
## Conversion QA
- One clear primary action.
- Audience and problem are obvious.
- Proof appears before or near the CTA.
- Friction is minimized.
- Mobile experience is usable.
## Platform QA
- Correct dimensions and duration.
- Text remains readable on the target device.
- Captions and accessibility requirements are satisfied.
- Export format is correct.
## Compliance QA
- Required disclosures are present.
- Unsupported promises are removed.
- Sensitive or regulated claims receive human review.

# The End State
You open the workspace and see:
- Campaign priorities already summarized
- Artifact briefs already prepared
- Missing source material flagged
- Brand rules already attached
- Production tasks already routed
- Approved artifacts ready for repurposing
- Performance data connected to the creative decisions that produced it
The team spends less time reconstructing context and more time making high-value decisions about message, positioning, offer, and creative direction.
> **Operating principle:** consolidate the context, connect the tools, structure the brief, generate the artifact, review the output, publish the package, and feed performance back into the system.
