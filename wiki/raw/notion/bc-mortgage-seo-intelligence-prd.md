# 1. Executive Summary
This project will build and execute a data-backed organic and paid search system for a British Columbia mortgage business. The system will prioritize qualified mortgage applications and funded outcomes rather than traffic volume alone.
The operating system will:
- Build a deduplicated mortgage keyword database using live DataForSEO metrics.
- Validate search intent through live SERP evidence.
- Cluster keywords into distinct borrower-focused webpages.
- Prevent keyword cannibalization.
- Identify Red Zone opportunities where commercial value is stronger than the current search results.
- Define website architecture, URL structure, content briefs, internal linking, local SEO, paid search, and conversion tracking.
- Provide 30-day, 90-day, and 12-month execution roadmaps.

## Scoring guidance
**Commercial intent:** 30 = ready to apply; 20 = strong consideration; 10 = educational with commercial relevance; 0 = no direct business value.
**Local intent:** 15 = explicit BC or city intent; 10 = implied local service intent; 5 = national with local relevance; 0 = non-local.
**Organic opportunity:** Higher scores for low-to-moderate difficulty, weak ranking pages, forum-heavy SERPs, homepages ranking instead of strong topic pages, and shallow local competitors.
**SERP opportunity:** Higher scores for PAA, featured snippets, forum presence, incomplete competitor answers, and attainable Local Pack visibility.
# 13. Red Zone Framework

# 14. SERP Clustering and Cannibalization Rules
## Merge when
- SERP overlap is approximately 70% or higher.
- Search intent is the same.
- The same page type ranks.
- The same conversion action is appropriate.
- Keywords are wording variations of the same need.
## Separate when
- Search intent is materially different.
- The borrower situation is different.
- Google ranks different page types.
- Local intent differs.
- The financing product differs.
- The conversion offer differs.
## Required cluster output
- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- Search intent
- Recommended URL
- Page type
- SERP-overlap result
- Cannibalization risk
- Supporting content
- Internal-link destination

# Addendum A — Semrush MCP Strategic Intelligence Layer
**Added:** July 23, 2026  
**Purpose:** Extend the DataForSEO-led operating system with Semrush MCP for competitive discovery, market validation, traffic-value modelling, and rank-and-rent opportunity analysis.
> **Decision:** DataForSEO MCP remains the primary programmable evidence and recurring measurement layer. Semrush MCP will serve as the strategic competitive-intelligence and second-opinion layer. Metrics from the two systems must not be treated as interchangeable without documenting the database, location, device, match logic, and retrieval date.
## A1. Strategic rationale
The system should use Semrush MCP and DataForSEO MCP together to answer four different questions:
1. **Where is the market opportunity?**
2. **Which competitors currently capture that opportunity?**
3. **Can a dedicated page or topical site realistically rank?**
4. **What is the likely traffic, lead, application, and funded-mortgage value?**
This dual-source approach reduces the risk of making webpage or investment decisions from a single search-volume estimate. Semrush supplies broad competitive context and historical visibility signals; DataForSEO supplies localized live SERP evidence and repeatable API-level validation.

## A11. Trust, quality, and compliance requirements
Because mortgage content can materially affect financial decisions, the rank-and-rent asset must not operate as a thin or anonymous lead-generation website. Required trust controls include:
- Named licensed mortgage professional or brokerage sponsor.
- Author and qualified reviewer attribution.
- Brokerage and jurisdiction disclosures.
- Clearly defined service area.
- Transparent explanation of potential rates, fees, risks, and alternatives.
- No approval guarantees or unsupported savings claims.
- Update and review dates.
- Editorial and correction policy.
- Privacy, consent, and secure-data-handling controls.
- An explanation that B lending is generally a transitional strategy requiring an exit plan.
- De-identified borrower examples that accurately reflect real underwriting situations.
The preferred model is a broker-sponsored regional authority property whose marketing rights or qualified opportunities can be licensed by territory while maintaining visible professional accountability.
