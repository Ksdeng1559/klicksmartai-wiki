# Product Overview
LeadSniper SGI is an AI-enabled domain audit and growth-intelligence application that evaluates a website, identifies low-competition and high-demand search opportunities, analyzes live SERPs and content gaps, measures AI-search visibility, and produces prioritized go-to-market recommendations after every audit.
The application is built on OpenSEO and DataForSEO, with optional supporting sources including Google Search Console, GA4, Serper, Exa, Parallel, Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, YouTube, reviews, and government or industry data.
The operating model is:
**Audit → Evidence → Opportunity → Recommendation → GTM Strategy → Execution → Measurement**
# 1. Product Objective
Create a multi-tenant application that allows a user to enter a domain and receive:
1. A technical and content audit.
2. Live SERP evidence for priority topics.
3. Low-competition, high-demand keyword opportunities.
4. Content and competitor gaps.
5. AI-search visibility and citation analysis.
6. Recommended pages, tools, content, offers, and campaigns.
7. A prioritized GTM strategy based on commercial opportunity.
8. Ongoing rank, citation, lead, and revenue monitoring.
# 2. Core Product Promise
The system must answer six business questions:
- What is preventing this domain from ranking or being cited?
- Where is demand stronger than competition?
- Which competitors currently own the opportunity?
- Where are competitors ranking with weak, incomplete, outdated, generic, or poorly localized content?
- Which single commercially viable keyword cluster should be built first?
- What content, offer, tool, or campaign is most likely to produce qualified demand and measurable organic growth?
## Initial Viable Offer
The MVP must be capable of producing and delivering an **Initial Viable Offer** called the **Organic Gap Sprint**.
> We identify the strongest commercially viable content gap your competitors have already proven, build the first optimized keyword cluster to fill that gap, connect it to a conversion offer, and measure whether organic impressions, rankings, traffic, and qualified leads improve.
The customer is not buying a keyword export or audit report. The customer is buying a completed organic-growth opportunity.
Each Initial Viable Offer must produce:
- Competitor and search-gap audit
- One prioritized keyword cluster
- One optimized pillar, service, comparison, or tool page
- Two to four supporting content assets
- Internal-link architecture
- Conversion offer and CTA
- Search Console baseline
- 30/60/90-day organic growth roadmap
The operating principle is:
**One customer → one competitor gap → one keyword cluster → one conversion offer → one measurable result**

# 4. Primary User Journey
## Step 1 — Create an Audit Project
The user enters:
- Domain
- Brand name
- Business description
- Products or services
- Target locations
- Target customer segments
- Primary conversion goals
- Average lead or customer value
- Known competitors
- Optional Google Search Console and GA4 connections
## Step 2 — Crawl and Audit the Domain
The application analyzes:
- Crawlability and indexability
- HTTP status codes
- Canonicals and redirects
- Robots.txt and sitemap coverage
- Titles, descriptions, headings, and duplicate content
- Internal links and orphan pages
- Schema markup
- Page speed and Core Web Vitals
- Mobile usability
- Content depth and topical coverage
- Existing calls to action and conversion paths
- AI crawler accessibility
- Current rankings and indexed pages
## Step 3 — Establish the Search and AI Opportunity Universe
The application generates and collects:
- Seed keywords
- Related keywords
- Questions and People Also Ask results
- Autocomplete and related searches
- Commercial and transactional queries
- Local intent variations
- Competitor keywords
- Zero-volume and emerging topics
- AI conversational prompts
- Google AI Overview and AI Mode opportunities
- ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity prompts
## Step 4 — Pull Live SERP Evidence
For each priority keyword or cluster, the system records:
- Top-ranking URLs and domains
- Titles and page types
- Featured snippets
- Local pack presence
- AI Overview presence
- Video, image, forum, shopping, and other SERP features
- Ranking authority and backlink indicators
- Search intent
- Content format patterns
- SERP volatility
- Location and device
- Collection date
## Step 5 — Identify Content and Competitive Gaps
The system compares the audited domain with selected and discovered competitors to identify:
- Missing keyword clusters
- Missing service pages
- Missing local pages
- Weak or outdated content
- Missing comparisons and alternatives
- Missing FAQs and supporting answers
- Missing tools, calculators, assessments, and templates
- Missing schema and structured content
- Internal-linking gaps
- Backlink and citation gaps
- AI citation-source gaps
- Topics where competitors rank but provide weak content
## Step 6 — Score Opportunities
Every opportunity receives a transparent score based on demand, competition, commercial fit, competitor weakness, conversion value, and production feasibility.
### Organic Opportunity Score
- 25% commercial value
- 20% ranking feasibility
- 20% search demand
- 15% current authority relevance
- 10% content gap
- 10% conversion fit
### Initial Viable Offer Score
The system must also identify the single strongest first engagement opportunity.
- 25% commercial intent
- 20% competitor content weakness
- 15% ranking feasibility
- 15% customer-service fit
- 10% search demand
- 10% conversion potential
- 5% production speed
The score must not rely on search volume alone. A lower-volume cluster may outrank a high-volume cluster when transaction value, commercial intent, competitor weakness, expertise fit, and conversion potential are stronger.
## Step 7 — Generate GTM Recommendations
The system converts research into an actionable growth plan covering:
- SEO page strategy
- AIO and citation strategy
- Content calendar
- Local SEO strategy
- Lead-magnet recommendations
- Offer and conversion-path improvements
- Competitor displacement strategy
- Partnership and backlink opportunities
- Social and community topics
- Paid-search opportunities where appropriate
- Outreach targets
- Recommended implementation sequence
## Step 8 — Monitor Results
After execution, the application tracks:
- Rankings
- Organic visibility
- AI mentions and citations
- Competitor share of voice
- Search Console impressions and clicks
- Content performance
- Leads and conversions
- Revenue attribution
- Technical regressions
- New content and keyword gaps

# 11. MVP Acceptance Criteria
The MVP is complete when a user can:
1. Register and create an organization.
2. Create a project and enter a domain, services, locations, competitors, and conversion goals.
3. Crawl the domain and classify existing pages.
4. Connect Google Search Console and save a baseline.
5. Discover at least 500 relevant keyword and question candidates for the pilot domain.
6. Remove irrelevant, duplicate, and low-fit terms.
7. Retrieve localized live SERPs for priority keywords.
8. Classify intent using language and actual SERP page types.
9. Compare SERP overlap and create defensible keyword clusters.
10. Identify competitor topics, weak competitor content, missing intent, and missing conversion assets.
11. Map each cluster to an existing page, new page, consolidation, supporting article, comparison, calculator, or monitor-only recommendation.
12. Calculate an Initial Viable Offer score and select the strongest first cluster.
13. Generate an implementation-ready pillar or service-page brief.
14. Generate two to four supporting content briefs.
15. Generate an internal-link plan.
16. Generate a conversion offer and CTA recommendation.
17. Produce an Organic Gap Sprint proposal and 30/60/90-day roadmap.
18. Track impressions, clicks, rankings, indexed pages, cluster performance, and conversions after publication.
19. Generate a secure, shareable report.
20. View provider costs and usage.
21. Use tenant-aware MCP tools.

# 13. Suggested 30/60/90-Day GTM Output
## First 30 Days — Foundation and Quick Wins
- Resolve critical technical issues.
- Improve titles, descriptions, schema, and internal links.
- Publish or refresh quick-win pages.
- Create one high-intent lead magnet or calculator.
- Establish baseline SEO and AI visibility.
## Days 31–60 — Authority and Demand Capture
- Publish priority service, pillar, local, and comparison pages.
- Address competitor and citation gaps.
- Launch backlink, partnership, or digital PR outreach.
- Build supporting content clusters.
- Begin lead nurture and CRM workflows.
## Days 61–90 — Scale and Optimization
- Expand successful clusters.
- Improve conversion paths.
- Test offers and calls to action.
- Track rankings, AI citations, leads, and revenue.
- Re-score opportunities using fresh evidence.
