## Purpose
This is the command centre for Dennis Eng's AI-native mortgage and business operating system.
## Operating Model
- **Notion:** source of truth for goals, projects, decisions, workflows and knowledge
- **Mortgage CoPilot:** broker command centre and client-delivery operating layer (front door: [www.mortgagesbydenniseng.ca](http://www.mortgagesbydenniseng.ca))
- **LeadSniperAI Marketplace OS:** front door for business funding requests via the `/funding-assessment` funnel — see [LeadSniperAI Marketplace OS](https://app.notion.com/p/3a09e94cf0a481a79a53e07966c1ac20)
- **Atomic CRM:** sales pipeline and communication system of record
- **n8n:** automation and orchestration
- **AI agents:** research, interpretation, strategy and execution support
- **GitHub / Obsidian / Qdrant:** governed technical and mortgage knowledge
## Core Workflow
Discovery → Research → Intelligence → Opportunity → Strategy → Execution → Learning
## Website Rebuild — Goals
The public website ([www.mortgagesbydenniseng.ca](http://www.mortgagesbydenniseng.ca)) is being rebuilt as the front door of the operating system, not a standalone brochure site.
### Strategic Goals
- **Lead with "Story Over Score":** every page opens with narrative discovery — business story, family story, real estate story, equity story, five-year story — before documents or rates
- **Position for alternative lending:** B-lender, self-employed, reverse mortgage, private mortgage, CMHC MLI Select, construction/laneway and debt consolidation each get a dedicated SEO pillar page (13 total)
- **Feed the operating system:** website enquiries flow into Atomic CRM with the five discovery questions captured as structured fields, so client narratives become institutional knowledge (Layer 3)
### Technical Goals
- Next.js 15 / React 19 build replacing the single-file HTML SPA (resolves the multiple-`` conflict and missing `` landmark)
- JSON-LD structured data on every pillar page: FinancialService, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList
- Homepage `` rewritten to contain the primary keyword
- MIX design system enforced via CSS custom properties only — navy #0B1E33, gold #C9A24B, off-white #F6F3EC, Playfair Display + Inter
- LeadConnectorhq booking widget retained
### Rollout Phases
1. **Phase 1:** Homepage, B-Lender, Reverse Mortgage, CMHC MLI Select
2. **Phase 2:** Self-Employed, Debt Consolidation, Private Mortgage, Laneway
3. **Phase 3:** Long-tail pages
