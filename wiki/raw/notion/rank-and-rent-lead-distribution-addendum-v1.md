## Addendum Mandate
Build a portfolio of search-driven mortgage properties that generates qualified organic borrower demand and routes that demand to approved mortgage professionals through transparent lead-purchase, referral, co-brokering, or commission-sharing arrangements.
This addendum extends the BC Mortgage Search Intelligence Operating System from a single-brand acquisition engine into a controlled **Mortgage Search Asset Network**.
`Search demand → Ranking asset → Borrower education → Assessment → Consent → Qualification → Broker matching → Application → Funding → Revenue attribution`
## Strategic Principle
The model is not ordinary lead resale. The objective is to own narrowly defined borrower-intent assets, provide accurate and useful mortgage guidance, qualify the opportunity, and place each borrower with an appropriate licensed professional.
The defensible advantage is created by:
- Organic distribution rather than rented advertising attention
- Situation-specific authority rather than generic mortgage content
- Story Over Score qualification rather than unfiltered form leads
- Exclusive or tightly controlled broker routing
- Full attribution from keyword to funded outcome
- Long-term ownership of the domain, content, data, rankings, and conversion system
## Business Models
### Model A — Exclusive Qualified Lead Sale
A participating broker purchases an exclusive, consented, qualified opportunity.
**Best suited to:** private mortgages, refinances, second mortgages, debt consolidation, bank-declined borrowers, self-employed mortgages, and location-specific opportunities.
**Commercial structure:**
- Fixed fee per accepted lead
- Higher fee for document-ready or application-ready leads
- Replacement or credit policy for invalid contact data, duplicate leads, or opportunities outside the agreed criteria
- No guarantee of approval or funding
### Model B — Funded-Deal Referral or Commission Split
The opportunity is introduced to a participating mortgage professional and revenue is earned only when the mortgage funds.
**Commercial structure:**
- Percentage of gross commission
- Fixed referral amount per funded mortgage
- Tiered split based on lead quality, exclusivity, fulfillment work, or lender type
- Written attribution window and renewal rules
Any referral, co-brokering, fee-sharing, disclosure, licensing, brokerage-approval, privacy, advertising, and recordkeeping requirements must be reviewed and approved before launch.
### Model C — Territory or Cluster Sponsorship
A broker pays for controlled access to a defined city, borrower segment, or content cluster.
Examples:
- Private mortgage leads in Surrey
- Self-employed mortgage leads in Vancouver
- Construction financing leads in the Fraser Valley
- Mortgage-after-consumer-proposal leads across British Columbia
The platform retains ownership of the domain, rankings, forms, analytics, and audience. The broker purchases distribution rights, not ownership of the search asset.
### Model D — Managed Organic Pipeline
The platform operates a complete search and conversion channel for a broker or brokerage.
Includes:
- Keyword and SERP research
- Content production
- Technical SEO
- Conversion assets
- Lead qualification
- CRM routing
- Attribution and reporting
Commercial options include setup fees, monthly management fees, performance fees, and funded-deal participation.
## Portfolio Architecture
### Tier 1 — Core Authority Brand
[**mortgagesbydenniseng.ca**](http://mortgagesbydenniseng.ca) remains the primary trust, authority, education, and direct-advisory property.
It should not be diluted into a generic lead-resale site. It validates expertise, publishes flagship Story Over Score content, and provides evidence for the broader portfolio.
### Tier 2 — Borrower-Situation Assets
Build domains or tightly focused sections around durable, high-intent borrower problems.
Priority examples:
- Bank declined mortgage
- Mortgage with CRA debt
- Self-employed mortgage using bank statements
- Private mortgage for debt consolidation
- Mortgage after consumer proposal
- Construction financing in BC
- Commercial property financing for business owners
- Alternative mortgage exit strategy
### Tier 3 — Geographic Assets
Create location assets only where live SERP evidence demonstrates differentiated local intent and commercial value.
Priority markets may include Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Richmond, Coquitlam, Langley, Abbotsford, and the Fraser Valley.
Avoid thin city doorway sites. Each geographic asset must contain real local context, distinct borrower scenarios, appropriate lender considerations, original FAQs, and a locally relevant conversion path.
### Tier 4 — Tools and Assessment Assets
High-value tools can rank independently and improve lead quality:
- Self-employed mortgage readiness assessment
- Bank-statement income estimator
- Debt-consolidation cash-flow calculator
- Alternative mortgage exit-plan builder
- Construction financing readiness assessment
- Private mortgage cost and exit calculator
Each tool must explain assumptions, limitations, costs, risks, and next steps.
## Search Asset Approval Gate
A new rank-and-rent asset is approved only when all required conditions are met:
1. **Distinct search intent:** SERPs show a unique borrower need or page format.
2. **Commercial viability:** The opportunity has realistic funded-deal economics.
3. **Broker demand:** At least one credible broker or brokerage is willing to service the opportunity.
4. **Fulfillment capacity:** The assigned broker has the licensing, lender access, response capacity, and expertise required.
5. **Trust viability:** The topic can be served with accurate, transparent, non-misleading content.
6. **Attribution viability:** Lead source, consent, broker assignment, application, and funding can be tracked.
7. **Cannibalization control:** The new asset will not weaken the authority brand or an existing portfolio property.

## Lead Quality Ladder
### Level 1 — Inquiry
Minimum information submitted with valid consent and contact details.
### Level 2 — Assessment Lead
Borrower completes the relevant situation-based assessment.
### Level 3 — Qualified Opportunity
Core information is validated:
- Purpose and requested amount
- Property location and type
- Estimated value and mortgage balance
- Down payment or equity position
- Income source and cash-flow story
- Credit event and Cause & Cure
- Timeline
- Intended exit strategy
### Level 4 — Advisor-Ready Opportunity
The lead includes an AI-assisted case summary, qualification flags, recommended broker specialty, missing-document list, and borrower expectations.
### Level 5 — Application-Ready Opportunity
The borrower has supplied the agreed intake and documentation package and is ready for licensed advice and submission.
Pricing and revenue participation should increase with quality, exclusivity, completeness, and conversion probability.
## Story Over Score Conversion Standard
Every borrower journey should gather the narrative before presenting a generic document checklist.
The assessment sequence should cover:
1. Equity and asset position
2. Real income and payment flow
3. Business and household expenses
4. Foreign income or asset path where applicable
5. Credit Cause & Cure
6. Loan objective
7. Exit strategy
8. Readiness and timing
The uploaded alternative-lending framework treats the document package as evidence supporting an already-defined borrower narrative. The rank-and-rent network should use the same approach so participating brokers receive useful opportunities rather than raw names and phone numbers.
## Broker Network Operating Standard
Every participating broker should have a verified profile containing:
- Licensed jurisdictions and brokerage
- Product specialties
- Geographic service area
- Lender access
- Minimum and maximum deal sizes
- Accepted borrower scenarios
- Languages
- Response-time commitment
- Capacity limits
- Escalation contact
- Commercial agreement
- Disclosure requirements
### Routing Logic
Leads should be matched using:
`Borrower situation + location + property type + loan amount + equity/LTV + income method + urgency + broker capacity`
Do not route solely by geography.
### Service-Level Expectations
Recommended operating targets:
- Automated acknowledgement immediately
- Broker acceptance or rejection within 5 minutes during service hours
- Human contact as quickly as reasonably possible
- Unaccepted opportunities automatically rerouted
- Every disposition recorded in the CRM
- Borrower receives clear notice of who will contact them and why
## Revenue Attribution
Atomic CRM should maintain the complete chain:
`Domain → Landing page → Keyword/cluster → CTA → Consent → Assessment → Lead grade → Assigned broker → Contacted → Application → Approved → Funded → Gross commission → Platform revenue`
Required fields:
- Source domain
- Landing-page URL
- Search cluster
- First and last touch
- Consent timestamp and wording version
- Lead grade
- Assigned broker and brokerage
- Assignment timestamp
- Acceptance status
- Application date
- Lender category
- Funded date and amount
- Gross commission
- Lead fee, referral fee, or split
- Invoice and payment status
- Dispute or replacement status
## Broker Commercial Agreement Requirements
Before distributing leads, establish written terms covering:
- Nature of the relationship
- Lead ownership and permitted use
- Exclusivity
- Pricing or commission formula
- Attribution period
- Renewals and repeat transactions
- Response-time expectations
- Borrower-contact standards
- Privacy and data-security responsibilities
- Consent and disclosure obligations
- Record retention
- Complaints and dispute handling
- Invalid-lead and replacement policy
- Non-circumvention where enforceable
- Termination and outstanding revenue
- Regulatory and brokerage approval
## Trust and Consumer Protection Standards
Every search asset must:
- Clearly identify the operator of the website
- Explain whether the site is a mortgage brokerage, broker-operated property, marketing platform, or lead-matching service
- Avoid suggesting that it is a lender unless it is legally entitled to do so
- Avoid guarantees of approval, rates, savings, or funding
- Disclose when information may be shared with a mortgage professional
- Obtain meaningful consent before routing personal information
- Explain that final advice, suitability assessment, approval, rates, terms, and disclosures come from the licensed professional and lender
- Publish privacy, terms, complaint, and contact information
- Maintain evidence and revision dates for financial content
The precise requirements must be confirmed with the applicable brokerage, licensing authority, privacy counsel, and legal adviser before launch.
## SEO Production Workflow
### Stage 1 — Market Selection
- Identify a borrower problem or financing decision
- Pull current DataForSEO demand and SERP evidence
- Estimate funded-deal economics
- Confirm broker fulfillment demand
- Approve or reject the asset
### Stage 2 — Asset Design
- Choose domain, subdomain, or directory strategy
- Define search cluster and page map
- Create conversion and assessment flow
- Define consent and routing language
- Assign lead-quality criteria
- Assign broker or broker pool
### Stage 3 — Content and Build
- Create cornerstone commercial page
- Publish supporting borrower guides
- Add comparison and FAQ resources
- Add calculator or assessment
- Add local evidence where justified
- Implement schema, analytics, CRM, call tracking, and event tracking
### Stage 4 — Claude SEO Quality Control
Run page-type and site-wide checks for:
- Technical SEO
- On-page optimization
- Content completeness
- Schema
- Internal linking
- Local SEO
- AI-search readiness
- Core Web Vitals
- Image optimization
- Conversion path integrity
No asset enters paid distribution or broker assignment until critical findings are resolved.
### Stage 5 — Commercial Validation
Track:
- Indexed pages
- Ranking keywords
- Qualified organic sessions
- Assessment-start rate
- Assessment-completion rate
- Valid-contact rate
- Broker-acceptance rate
- Contact rate
- Application rate
- Approval rate
- Funded rate
- Revenue per lead
- Revenue per funded file
- Time to recover build cost
### Stage 6 — Scale, Repair, or Retire
After an agreed test period:
- **Scale:** expand supporting content, internal links, locations, and broker capacity
- **Repair:** improve rankings, qualification, routing, or broker fulfillment
- **Merge:** consolidate overlapping assets or cannibalized pages
- **Retire:** redirect or de-index assets that lack search, trust, or commercial viability
## Portfolio Economics Model
For every asset, maintain a simple operating model:
`Expected monthly organic leads × qualified rate × broker acceptance rate × funded rate × platform revenue per funding`
Also calculate:
- Build and content cost
- Ongoing SEO and hosting cost
- Qualification and support cost
- Revenue per visitor
- Revenue per accepted lead
- Revenue per application
- Revenue per funded mortgage
- Payback period
- Twelve-month contribution margin
All assumptions must be labelled as verified, supported inference, strategic hypothesis, or insufficient data.

### Pilot Asset 1 — Self-Employed Mortgage Using Bank Statements
**Conversion asset:** income-readiness assessment  
**Broker requirement:** strong BFS and Alt-A lender bench  
**Commercial model:** exclusive qualified lead or funded split
### Pilot Asset 2 — Mortgage With CRA Debt or Tax Arrears
**Conversion asset:** equity and debt-reset assessment  
**Broker requirement:** refinance, private, and alternative expertise  
**Commercial model:** advisor-ready opportunity fee plus funded participation where permitted

**Conversion asset:** construction-readiness assessment  
**Broker requirement:** construction, commercial, land, and draw-mortgage experience  
**Commercial model:** exclusive opportunity, territory sponsorship, or commission participation

- Calculate cost per qualified organic opportunity

## Pilot Success Gates
A pilot asset may scale only when:
- Search visibility is increasing
- Content quality and trust standards are met
- Lead consent is valid and auditable
- Brokers accept the opportunity type
- Response standards are consistently met
- Lead quality improves over the test period
- Economics show a credible path to positive contribution margin
- Complaint, privacy, and disclosure risks remain controlled
## Immediate Decisions Required
1. Will the first pilot use separate domains, subdomains, or folders?
2. Will opportunities be sold exclusively, routed to a broker pool, or retained for Dennis first?
3. Will revenue begin with fixed qualified-lead fees, funded splits, or a hybrid?
4. Which brokerage or legal reviewer will approve the referral and commission structure?
5. Which two to four brokers will form the first fulfillment bench?
6. Which asset will be the first proof of concept?
## Recommended Default
Start with a **hybrid model**:
- Dennis retains first right of review for strategic or high-value opportunities.
- Clearly defined overflow or out-of-scope opportunities are routed to approved brokers.
- Use exclusive qualified-lead pricing for early cash flow.
- Add funded-deal participation only after brokerage and legal approval.
- Keep all domains, content, analytics, assessments, and CRM data under the platform’s control.
This protects trust, proves lead quality, produces near-term revenue, and preserves the long-term value of the organic search portfolio.
