## 2. Most Searched Life Insurance Product Type in Canada

### 🏆 Term Life Insurance — #1 Most Searched Product

**Live search data (Empathy.com, March 2026):**
- **Life insurance search demand up 83% YoY** globally
- **Term life search volume up 54% YoY** — the single biggest driver
- Quote-related searches up 27% — buyers are further along in the funnel
- "Using life insurance to build wealth" searches up 1,100% — new emerging intent
- Generic "top companies" searches down 74% — buyers are getting more specific

**Canadian context (Serper, Google.ca):**
- "Term life insurance Canada" dominates search results across all major brokers
- Ratehub.ca, PolicyMe, PolicyAdvisor, Term4sale all rank for high-volume term queries
- No-exam/simplified issue searches rising — digital-native buyers skipping the medical
- Most searched product length: **Term 10** (lowest cost, highest affordability)

#### Why Term Insurance Dominates Search
1. **Affordability** — cheapest entry point ($12–$37/month for healthy non-smokers)
2. **Simplicity** — easy to understand (pure death benefit, fixed term)
3. **High intent** — searchers know what they want, ready to compare
4. **Renewal/Conversion optionality** — term 10/20 can convert to whole life
5. **Digital-native buyers** — healthy 25–45 age group prefers online purchase journey

### Other High-Search Volume Products

## 3. Key Person Insurance — Business Owner Niche

### Search Profile
- "Key person insurance Canada" is a **specialized, high-intent search**
- Target buyers: small business owners (1–50 employees), professional partnerships, incorporated professionals
- Decision drivers: protecting business loans, funding buy-sell agreements, replacing key talent

### What Business Owners Search For
- "Key person insurance cost Canada"
- "Business owner life insurance best rates"
- "Buy-sell agreement insurance funding Canada"
- "Key person disability insurance Canada"

### Key Providers Active in This Space
- **BFL CANADA** — Executive Benefits Solutions (major player)
- **Westland Insurance** — Executive plans, conversion options
- **Canada Life** — Business solutions
- **ThinkInsure** — SMB-focused, broker-assisted
- **Swoop CA** — Simple online quote

### Coverage Types Business Owners Need
1. **Key Person Life Insurance** — company owns, pays premiums, receives payout
2. **Key Person Disability** — income continuation if key person can't work
3. **Buy-Sell Funding** — funds partner buyout on death/disability
4. **Business Loan Protection** — repays business debt

### Why This Is a Gold Mine for IDC
- High ticket value per policy ($500K–$5M coverage)
- Business owners often have complex needs (combination of key person + personal coverage)
- Less price-sensitive than personal buyers
- Strong referral potential — business owners network with other business owners

## 4. Final Expense Insurance — Seniors Segment

### Search Profile
- **Bread-and-butter senior leads:** Age 50–80, seeking $5,000–$25,000 policies
- **Premium range:** $30–$70/month for $10,000 coverage (healthy non-smoker senior)
- **Most-searched variants:** Guaranteed acceptance, no medical exam, pre-existing conditions accepted

### Why Final Expense Leads Convert Well
1. **Defined need** —买家 know exactly why they're buying (funeral costs, estate)
2. **Less price-sensitive** — smaller coverage amounts
3. **Emotional purchase** — family protection, leaving inheritance
4. **Fewer comparison shoppers** — less price competition vs term
5. **Retention** — seniors tend to keep policies long-term
