## ROI Calculator Logic

| Input | Field |
|-------|-------|
| Hours/week (manual) | Total across all team members |
| People involved | Headcount doing the manual work |
| Avg hourly cost | Salary + benefits / hours ($25–50 typical) |
| Efficiency estimate | 50% (conservative) / 70% (realistic) / 85% (aggressive) / 95% (full) |
| Project fee | Proposed automation cost |
| Monthly maintenance | Ongoing hosting/support |

**Output:** Annual cost of manual work → Annual savings → ROI multiple → Payback period

## Industry Deep Dives

### 1. Marketing Agencies
**Price:** $10K–$45K | **Buyer:** Agency Owner / COO | **Spend:** $3K–$8K/mo

**Flag:** Avoid agencies under $20K MRR. No margins or budget. Target $50K+ MRR minimum.

**Tip:** Lead with reporting. Demo automated client reporting that pulls from actual platforms — 30-second attention grab.

**Discovery Qs:**
1. How many hours/week does your team spend pulling and formatting client reports?
2. Walk me through what happens from new client sign to fully onboarded.
3. How are you tracking which team members work on what across all accounts?

**10 Pain Points (ranked):**
1. Client reporting (5+ platforms, formatting, sending)
2. Campaign performance dashboards (manual spreadsheet updates)
3. Client onboarding (welcome sequences, folder creation, access provisioning)
4. Content calendar management and approval workflows
5. Lead routing and attribution tracking
6. Invoice generation and payment follow-ups
7. Social media scheduling and cross-posting
8. Proposal and SOW generation from templates
9. Team utilization tracking and resource allocation
10. Client communication logging across email, Slack, and PM tools

2. Job posting distribution across 10+ boards
3. Interview scheduling and calendar coordination
4. Candidate status updates and pipeline tracking
5. Client reporting on placement metrics
6. Reference check coordination
7. Offer letter generation
