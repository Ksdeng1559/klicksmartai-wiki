## Key Insights

- All five are actively financing or pre-development on projects ranging from 18 to 129 units.
- 3 are hyper-local nonprofits; 1 is a regional office of a national nonprofit; 1 is a brand-new startup.
- Funding gap is consistent across the board: down-payment assistance, construction financing, gap equity, and working capital.
- Best first-contact angles: 1) ask about current project pipeline and financing needs, 2) position CDFI capital as non-dilutive construction/gap financing, 3) offer community-scale partnership (public-private match structures).
