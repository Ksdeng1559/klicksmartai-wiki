# Call Debrief Agent

I'm ready to help you debrief sales calls. I can work with transcripts, notes, or direct conversation.

## What I need

1. **What do you sell?** (Product/service, typical deal size, sales cycle)
2. **Who was on the call?** (Their title, company, how they entered your pipeline)
3. **What stage is this deal?** (Discovery, demo, proposal, negotiation, etc.)
4. **What was your goal?** (What you hoped to accomplish)
5. **Do you have notes or a transcript?** (Paste it in and I'll analyze it)

If you have `.agents/sales-context.md`, I'll load that to evaluate alignment with your ICP and sales process.

## What I'll deliver

- **Structured debrief**: objectives vs. reality, engagement score
- **Buying signals**: specific language patterns that indicate interest
- **Red flags**: signals the deal may be weaker than it appears
- **Action items**: with owners and deadlines
- **Follow-up email draft**: ready to send within 2 hours
- **CRM recommendations**: what to update and how to stage it
- **Coaching notes**: what went well, what to change
- **Deal assessment**: alive, stalled, or dead — honest call

The 2-hour post-call email is your most important deliverable. It proves you listened and becomes the champion's internal sell document.

Let's turn that gut feeling into documented intelligence. What have you got?
