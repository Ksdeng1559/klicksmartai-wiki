# CEO Skills Framework

A collection of 10 AI skill prompts for founders and CEOs, covering strategy, operating rhythm, people management, and execution. All skills read from `BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md` if present — set that up first.

## The 10 Skills

### 1. Strategic Sparring
**Trigger**: Hard business decisions requiring a thinking partner  
**Structure**: 5-phase decision process — Understand → Map Options → Stress-Test → Model Consequences → Drive to Clarity  
**Key rule**: 70/30 questions-to-observations ratio. Ends with a documented decision record: choice, reasoning, key assumptions, triggers for revisiting.  
**Not**: therapy, brainstorming, or validation — rigorous thinking partnership only.

### 2. Weekly Review (15 minutes)
**Trigger**: End of each week  
**Structure**: 6 sections — Scorecard → Wins → Losses & Lessons → Priority Check → Next Week's Focus → Energy Check  
**Key rule**: Maximum 3 priorities for the upcoming week. Energy check is non-negotiable.  
**Output**: Timestamped markdown file for continuity tracking.  
**Principle**: "A loss without a lesson is just a bad week. A loss with a lesson is tuition."

### 3. Quarterly Review (60–90 minutes)
**Trigger**: End of each quarter  
**Structure**: 8 sections — Scorecard → Revenue & Growth → Wins → What Didn't Work → Strategic Priorities (Keep/Kill/Start) → Team & Capacity → Next Quarter's Big Bets → Annual Goal Check-In  
**Key rule**: Every new priority requires removing an old one. Constraint forces clarity.  
**Output**: `reviews/quarterly-review-[YYYY]-Q[N].md`  
**Principle**: "Weekly reviews keep you on track. Quarterly reviews make sure you're on the right track."

### 4. Hiring
**Trigger**: Considering a new hire  
**Three modes**: Plan (define role + strategy) → Evaluate (score candidates) → Decide (hire/no-hire)  
**Core question**: "What problem does this hire solve?" If you can't answer in one sentence, you're not ready.  
**Rules**: Must-haves non-negotiable. Work samples beat behavioral questions. 3–4 interview steps max over 2 weeks. Never hire to avoid work yourself.  
**Red flags**: "Rockstar", "wear many hats", 15+ requirements, hidden comp.

### 5. Delegation
**Trigger**: Founder doing work that should belong to the team  
**Process**: Time Dump → Four-Bucket Sort (Only You / Teach Someone / Hire For / Eliminate) → Resistance Coaching → Delegation Plan → New Calendar  
**Principle**: "The founder who can't delegate caps their company at whatever they can personally handle."

### 6. Financial Review
**Trigger**: Monthly or when financial clarity is needed  
**Six areas**: Revenue picture → Profitability & margins → Cash & runway → Unit economics → Pricing strategy → Spending decisions  
**Principle**: "Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, cash is reality."  
**Key bias**: Pricing is usually too low — challenges this directly.

### 7. One-on-Ones
**Trigger**: Before each 1:1 with a direct report  
**Modes**: Meeting prep → Feedback drafting → Performance conversations  
**Framework**: Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI) for feedback  
**Goal**: Build the team, not just manage it.

### 8. Meeting Prep & Follow-Up
**Trigger**: Before or after any significant meeting  
**Output (prep)**: One-page sharp brief — who, what, objective  
**Output (follow-up)**: Action items, follow-up email draft, flagged issues  
**Prerequisite**: `BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md` for context-specific tailoring

### 9. Prospect Research
**Trigger**: Before a sales call  
**Input**: Company name (minimum), contact name/role, connection source, website  
**Output**: Personalized pre-call intelligence brief  
**Prerequisite**: `BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md` or `sales-context.md` to tailor to what you sell

### 10. Content Repurpose
**Trigger**: After creating any long-form content  
**Input**: Transcript, blog post, script, or article  
**Output**: Newsletter version + social posts (3–5) + LinkedIn post + short-form video scripts + Twitter thread (on request)  
**Principle**: Every piece stands alone. No AI filler. Your voice throughout.
