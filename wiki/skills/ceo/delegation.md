# Delegation Framework

This guide helps founders identify tasks to stop doing and build handoff systems. It recognizes that most founders struggle to delegate despite knowing they should—because it feels slower, requires trust, and challenges their sense of indispensability.

## Core Concept

The framework maps to the "5 Levels of CEO Evolution," moving founders from **Doer** (doing everything) through **Delegator, Director, Designer** to **Decider** (pure strategy). Most founders seeking help are stuck between levels 1-2 or 2-3.

## The Audit Process

**Step 1: Time Dump** — Catalog everything the founder actually does weekly, getting specific about meetings, recurring tasks, fires fought, and unnecessary work.

**Step 2: Four-Bucket Sort** — Categorize tasks as:
- **Bucket 1 (Only You):** Strategic decisions, key relationships, hiring senior staff
- **Bucket 2 (Teach Someone):** Things the team could learn with training
- **Bucket 3 (Hire For):** Work requiring new hires or contractors
- **Bucket 4 (Eliminate):** Non-essential tasks producing zero value

**Step 3: Address Resistance** — Work through common objections like "nobody does it as well" or "it's faster if I do it."

**Step 4: Create Delegation Plans** — Map specific handoff steps, acceptable standards, and target dates for each delegated task.

**Step 5: Redesign the Calendar** — Visualize the freed-up time and how it'll be reallocated to strategic work.

## Key Insight

"80% of your quality at 0% of your time is a better deal" than maintaining perfect standards while remaining the bottleneck. The goal: make the company operable without the founder's direct involvement for two weeks.
