# Vector 6 — Mortgage-Lens Keyword Research

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Purpose:** Quantify the mortgage-lens attack surface (HELOC, home equity, refinance, second mortgage, CMHC commercial) for the business-loan comparison site.
**Method:** DataForSEO keyword_overview/live + keywords_for_keywords/live + keyword_ideas/live, location Canada (2124), language EN.
**Total spend:** $0.16 across 4 API calls
**Conclusion:** The mortgage-lens surface is **smaller than the previous estimate** — 680 vol/mo total across 28 commercial-intent terms. The moat is real but narrower than the original analysis claimed.

---

## TL;DR

- **Vector 6 total addressable volume: 680/mo across 28 terms**
- The 6 pages I originally proposed (`heloc vs business line of credit`, etc.) **don't have search volume individually** — most are 0-10/mo
- The real commercial-intent terms in this cluster are **"self employed loans"** (170/mo), **"loans for self employed bad credit"** (70/mo), **"cmhc commercial mortgage"** (50/mo)
- The mortgage-lens differentiator is **structurally true** (Swoop can't add this content) but the **search volume is too thin to justify 6 dedicated pages**
- **Right move:** Merge mortgage-lens content INTO existing pillars (especially self-employed, BDC vs private, and one new "self-employed business funding" page) instead of 6 standalone pages

## What this means for the strategy

### The "mortgage-lens moat" is real but smaller than estimated

**The structural argument holds:** Swoop can't add HELOC/equity/refinance content because their commission model excludes it. Business-loan alt-lenders can't add it because they don't have mortgage licenses. Only mortgage brokers with a business-credit lens can produce this content.

**The search volume argument is weaker than the original estimate:**
- Original estimate: 90-260 vol/mo for `heloc vs business line of credit` alone
- Real DataForSEO data: 0-10 vol/mo for every "heloc vs business loan" variant
- Real commercial-intent volume in the entire mortgage-lens cluster: 680/mo
- That's **2% of the consolidated 31,830/mo total addressable search volume**

### The actual commercial-intent themes

The data shows the real Vector 6 is **"self-employed borrower access to capital"** (170 + 110 + 70 + 50 + 50 = 450 vol/mo just on the top 5 terms), not "HELOC vs business credit" (which is essentially a zero-volume query).

The mortgage-lens angle is the **explanation mechanism** (because a self-employed borrower faces different underwriting than a W-2 borrower, and the right capital product depends on their specific situation), not the **keyword target**.

### Recommended restructure

**Drop the 6-page mortgage-lens cluster from the build plan. Replace with:**

1. **One new pillar: `/business-loans-canada/self-employed-business-loans/`** — covers the 450 vol/mo self-employed cluster AND embeds the mortgage-lens comparison as a major section
   - Internal sub-sections: HELOC option, home equity option, business LOC option, unsecured business loan option, BDC option
   - Comparison table: which product for which borrower profile
   - The mortgage-lens angle is the **differentiation within the page**, not a separate page

2. **Update the `/bdc-vs-private-lender/` pillar** — add a section on "BDC vs private lender vs home equity" for self-employed borrowers

3. **Update the `/government-grants-canada/` lead magnet** — add a section on mortgage-broker access to self-employed financing (cross-sell to mortgage brand)

4. **Skip standalone `heloc vs business line of credit`, `home equity business loan canada`, `second mortgage business loan`, `refinance house for business` pages** — they have 0-10 vol/mo, not worth the page builds

### Updated lead economics

The previous estimate of $15K-21K/mo revenue at month 12 included mortgage-lens leads at $1,000 avg commission. With the corrected Vector 6 surface:

- Vector 6 self-employed content: 28 terms × 450 total vol × 10% capture × 5% conversion = 2-3 leads/mo
- At $500-1,000/lead (mix of business loan referral + mortgage advisory) = $1,000-3,000/mo
- This is **one-tenth** of the original mortgage-lens revenue estimate

**Revised month-12 revenue: $5K-15K/mo, not $15K-21K/mo**

This is still a real business. Just a smaller one than the previous analysis suggested.

## What the data doesn't show (and what would change the conclusion)

The mortgage-lens commercial-intent volume is 680/mo on **broad seeds**. A more focused pull could find additional terms:

- Pull against seed: "self employed mortgage canada" (residential mortgage for self-employed borrowers — this is Dennis's core mortgage business, with potentially higher volume)
- Pull against seed: "self employed mortgage broker" (intent: find a broker who specializes)
- Pull against seed: "stated income mortgage canada" (alt-lending mortgage products)
- Pull against seed: "home equity loan for self employed" (specific intersection)

**Recommended follow-up:** Run a $0.10 keyword_ideas pull on these 4 residential-self-employed seeds to quantify the cross-sell surface from the existing mortgage business. If the volume is significant, the Vector 6 strategy pivots from "self-employed business loans" (current) to "self-employed mortgage + business funding" (combined).
