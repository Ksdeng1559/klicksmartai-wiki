## Industries NOT listed but likely served

Based on the asset types they accept (A/R + inventory + equipment + RE) and the deBanked article's note that they get "roughly half of their deal flow from mortgage brokers," Kingsmen likely also serves:

- **Real estate investors** (mortgage broker channel)
- **Professional services** (law, accounting, consulting) — A/R-heavy
- **Restaurants beyond franchise** (bars, catering, food trucks)
- **Hospitality** (hotels, B&Bs — overlap with Glasslake's hotel space)
- **E-commerce / online retail** (A/R from marketplaces)
- **Manufacturing SMBs** (lower-end of Corp division)
- **Auto dealerships** (overlap with Glasslake automotive)
- **Daycares** (overlap with Glasslake daycares)

## Industries ABSOLUTELY not served (deduced)

- Residential mortgages (that's Glasslake, B-lenders, banks)
- Cannabis dispensaries (most alt-lenders exclude)
- Adult entertainment (most alt-lenders exclude)
- Cryptocurrency / blockchain (most alt-lenders exclude)
- Nonprofits (most alt-lenders exclude, except mission-driven lenders)
- Pure real estate speculation (not a business)
