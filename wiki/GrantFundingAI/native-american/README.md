## Why Use a Native-Specific Center?

These centers provide:
- Expert knowledge of **tribal laws** and regulations
- **Cultural community nuances** specific to tribal procurement
- Specialized certification guidance (tribal preferences, tribal business designations)
- Connection to tribal economic development offices
- Non-Native firms can also use these centers to learn how to sell to Tribal governments
