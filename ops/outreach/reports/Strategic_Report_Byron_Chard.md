## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Byron Chard is the President and CEO of Chard Development Ltd., a prominent BC-based real estate firm. He is currently leading a significant strategic pivot from "Merchant Development" (build-to-sell) to an "Institutional Model" (build-to-hold), specifically focusing on Purpose-Built Rentals (PBR).

**Core Intelligence:**
- **Scale:** Over 1.3M sq ft developed; 3,100+ homes in the current pipeline.
- **Pedigree:** Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) with 30+ years of industry experience.
- **Key Signal:** Transitioning to long-term institutional partnerships (Pension Funds) and recurring revenue models.

- **Asset Mix:** Transitioning from condominiums to a diversified mix of Purpose-Built Rentals, hotels, and commercial strata.
