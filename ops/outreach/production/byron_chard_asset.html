The Concentration Paradox

For institutional developers, success often creates a unique financial vulnerability. As your portfolio shifts from transactional condos to long-term Purpose-Built Rentals (PBR), your wealth becomes increasingly concentrated in a single asset class and a single geography.

The Paradox: Your company has never been more stable, yet your personal balance sheet has never been more exposed.

While the cash-flow from 3,100+ units is a powerful engine, true Sovereignty requires the decoupling of personal security from professional footprint.

The Institutional Risk

High sensitivity to BC provincial policy, interest rate shifts, and municipal zoning changes.

The Liquidity Trap

Wealth tied up in equity that is high-value but low-velocity.
